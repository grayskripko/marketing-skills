Your pipeline isn't lying to you. Your stage definitions are.

Last quarter I audited 9 B2B SaaS pipelines (20-200 people). In 7 of them, "Stage 3 - Proposal" meant three different things depending on which rep you asked. Forecast accuracy was off by 30-45%.

The fix took two weeks, not a new tool:
1. One written exit criterion per stage
2. A required field that proves it
3. A weekly 15-minute "stale deal" review

I put the stage-definition template I use into a one-page checklist. Comment "STAGES" and I'll send it.
