# UK invoice field sources

All sources read 2026-10-11. Current accessible official guidance on that date; recheck deciding official guidance when the relevant supply/issue date, known rule change, conflicting guidance or special treatment requires it. The planned 2026-10-13 package submission is maintainer scheduling, not a user review deadline. Dates below are displayed publisher dates, not presumed commencement dates. Summaries are paraphrased. | Source and date | Field knowledge and scope |
|---|---|
| [GOV.UK invoice requirements](https://www.gov.uk/invoicing-and-taking-payment-from-customers/invoices-what-they-must-include), no publication/update date displayed | UK ordinary invoice: identifier, seller name/address/contact, customer name/address, description, supply and invoice dates, charges, applicable VAT, owed total. Sole trader legal/trading names and legal-service address; incorporated full company name and all directors if directors shown. |
| [HMRC VAT Notice 700](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/vat-guide-notice-700), published 2014-12-17; updated 2026-06-25 | Sections 16.2–16.6: tax point and normal 30-day issue deadline with exceptions; VAT-registered identity; sterling VAT total; unit price for countable supplies, with indivisible total-net-price treatment and omission where neither normally provided in the sector nor required by the customer; no-VAT line totals; £250 simplified threshold, customer agreement, Northern Ireland/EU exclusion. Section 17.3: pro-forma marking and subsequent proper invoice. Section 17.5: rounding concessions depend on trader type. Do not use removed section 16.8: update history says removed 2026-05-29. |
| [HMRC VAT Notice 700/21](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/record-keeping-for-vat-notice-70021), published 2013-09-26; updated 2024-03-18 | Sections 4.1, 4.4–4.6: full fields, simplified threshold is VAT-inclusive; sterling totals per rate; no exempt supplies in shortened invoices. Above £250 full or modified form. Its broad non-retailer modified wording does not resolve the detailed retailer-manual restriction below. |
| [HMRC full invoice manual](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/vat-trader-records/vatrec5010), manual published 2016-04-10; updated 2025-01-16 (manual-level dates) | Full field matrix: sequence; tax point/issue date; seller VAT identity; customer identity; description/quantity/rate/net/unit price; total net; cash-discount rate if offered; sterling VAT. Letters and multiple series allowed; explained retained cancellations need not invalidate sequence. Reverse-charge/margin references are conditional. |
| [HMRC retailer modified invoices](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/vat-trader-records/vatrec16043), same manual-level dates | With customer agreement, VAT-inclusive lines plus footer totals: positive-rated gross, VAT, positive-rated net, separate zero-rated supplies. Other full fields remain. Restricted to retail establishments and stock on public sale. Use full form if this restriction cannot be established; flag rather than silently generalize. |
| [GOV.UK company stationery](https://www.gov.uk/running-a-limited-company/signs-stationery-and-promotional-material), no publication/update date displayed | Company name on documents. Company number, registered office and registration location requirement is stated for business letters/order forms/websites; page explicitly sends invoices to separate invoice requirements. Do not invent extra invoice blockers from this page. |
| [GOV.UK charging VAT](https://www.gov.uk/charge-reclaim-record-vat), no publication/update date displayed | Registration before charging VAT; headline standard 20%, reduced 5%, zero 0%; inclusive/exclusive calculations. These rates do not classify an ambiguous supply. Legacy /vat-record-keeping/vat-invoices redirects here; store canonical URL. |

Review choices (authored operating guidance, not statutory requirements): answer in the requested shape, using a field/location-to-fix table only when useful; remove or relabel a misleading VAT line for an unregistered seller without changing the agreed price; distinguish unseen from missing; ask one deciding question after supported fixes; keep bank details out of the report; no automatic account changes. No claim that a host has built-in image extraction.

[HMRC foreign-currency invoices](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/foreign-currency-transactions-vat-and-tour-operators), read 2026-10-11; published 2014-07-01, updated 2018-06-18: invoice sterling net and VAT totals for each rate, not each line. UK market selling or HMRC period rates are standard; other VAT methods need approval, no forward-derived rates. Advance invoices use sterling supply values. This specific guidance adds to Notice 700's shorter total-VAT wording.

Citation convention: use the canonical URLs above unless the exact fragment has been checked in the current source HTML.

Source comparison checked 2026-10-11: [VATREC5060](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/vat-trader-records/vatrec5060), manual published 2016-04-10, updated 2025-01-16, describes regulation 14's narrower sterling-total-VAT invoice minimum. The separate foreign-currency guidance above expressly directs invoices to show sterling net and VAT totals by rate. Follow that explicit HMRC guidance for the proposed field additions; do not call the rate-group net additions a quoted requirement of regulation 14 or turn source reconciliation into unsolicited commentary.
