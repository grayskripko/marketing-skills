# UK credit-note sources

All sources read 2026-10-11. Recheck deciding official guidance when the relevant supply/issue date, known rule change, conflicting guidance or special treatment requires it. The planned 2026-10-13 package submission is maintainer scheduling, not a user review deadline. Summaries are paraphrased. [HMRC VAT Notice 700](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/vat-guide-notice-700), published 2014-12-17, updated 2026-06-25:

- 18.2.3: genuine reduction/overcharge, real refund/offset entitlement; issue within 14 days of refund payment. Identify note/date, supplier name/address/VAT number, customer name/address, credited description/quantity/amount, total net, VAT rate and sterling VAT, original invoice number/date. Unidentifiable originals require other evidence of VAT accounted for.
- 18.2.1 and 18.2.3: agreed no-VAT-adjustment exception requires customer's full input-tax recovery; note says “This is not a credit note for VAT”.
- 18.2.4–18.2.5: zero/exempt credits separate, no VAT credit; original tax-point rate applies.
- 18.2.2: discount credit when taken; without a credit note, original discount timing/recovery terms plus payment records support adjustment.
- 18.2.6: VAT already wrongly declared needs separate return-error correction; a note alone does not fix it.

[GOV.UK invoice requirements](https://www.gov.uk/invoicing-and-taking-payment-from-customers/invoices-what-they-must-include), no publisher date displayed: seller-type identity rules used to preserve legal/trading or incorporated names in the credit draft.

Review choices (authored operating guidance): do not backdate a late note; show the actual deadline and lateness, propose prompt issue with the true date, and assess any VAT-return error separately; lateness alone does not require HMRC contact or an adviser referral; distinguish unseen fields; partial credit is not full cancellation; avoid repeating bank details. These are review safeguards, not invented tax deadlines.

Additional official sources read 2026-10-11:

- [HMRC changes in consideration](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/vat-supply-and-consideration/vatsc06635), manual published 2016-04-10, updated 2025-03-07: refund or monetary offset triggers reduction/accounting period; promised credit alone insufficient. Missing original particulars no longer normally replaced by alternative evidence. Simplified-credit and no-original-invoice exceptions apply. No-original-invoice case: VAT customer request starts window and original reference is not required.
- [2019 amending regulations, official PDF](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/1048/pdfs/uksi_20191048_en.pdf), made 2019-06-25, effective 2019-09-01: inserted regulation 15C(5) counts 14 days beginning with the reduction day, so last day = start + 13 days. 15C(8)–(10) distinguish request-trigger and simplified routes; simplified route omits note number, customer and original reference, not issue date. 24C includes monetary offsets. HMRC's accessible current manual corroborates these branches. The PDF is as made, not a consolidated amendment-history claim.

The shorter Notice 700 phrase “within 14 days” does not establish inclusive counting by itself. Use the express legislative start-day rule. Do not present alternative-evidence wording as blanket permission under post-2019 note requirements.
