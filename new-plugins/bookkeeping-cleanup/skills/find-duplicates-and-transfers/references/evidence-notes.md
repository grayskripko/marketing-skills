# Evidence and workpaper conventions

The workflow here is this plugin's rule of thumb, not an accounting standard, legal requirement or accuracy claim. The user's approved chart, policies and source records decide each proposed change. There are no built-in tax rates, legal deadlines or jurisdiction-specific treatment rules.

Design evidence came from the project's accounting research A and B, read 2026-10-09. The research supports the user jobs and caution around source-linked proposals; it does not validate professional conclusions.

- [Bookkeeping categorization request](https://www.reddit.com/r/Bookkeeping/comments/1i0vwuz), read 2026-10-09 in research A: wording evidence for reviewing coding; forum advice is not an accounting rule.
- [Expense categorization request](https://www.reddit.com/r/smallbusiness/comments/1vckpjj/smallsolo_business_owners_whats_your_actual/), read 2026-10-09 in research B: wording evidence for catch-up work; not a frequency or accuracy estimate.

The build reused those dated reports; these pages were not fetched again. The general review conventions are separate from the dated Intuit guidance linked in SKILL.md; use that guidance only for supported QuickBooks Online cases. Source IDs, separate currencies, unresolved alternatives and before/after controls are this plugin's review conventions.

A useful source reference is “ledger.csv, row 12, transaction L08” or “receipts.pdf, printed page 3, file page 5.” Keep those page meanings separate. Mask personal identifiers in displayed excerpts. Refer to the source instead of copying account numbers.
