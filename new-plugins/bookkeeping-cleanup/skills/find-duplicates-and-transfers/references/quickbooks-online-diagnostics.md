# QuickBooks Online cleanup diagnostics

Verified 2026-10-11. Applies only to supplied QuickBooks Online records using the US help documentation. Obtain product/edition and each candidate's feed state, origin, posted ID, links, attachments and explicit reconciliation status from supplied exports or readable screenshots. Unknown state means an evidence request, not a platform correction. Do not infer reconciliation from a bare checkmark code. Other products remain governed by supplied evidence.

## D1 — Duplicate diagnosis and correction dependencies

[Intuit: Fix duplicate transactions](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/duplicate-transactions/fix-duplicate-transactions-quickbooks-online-bank-feeds/L1fjxl88f_US_en_US). Retrieved 2026-10-11; readable article loaded. Publication/update date not exposed in retrieved text. Relevant sections: Steps 3–4; “Income is doubled.”

- Request the register filter: “All plus deposits” can repeat displayed transactions. Check payment/deposit links; linked parts are not duplicates.
- For a confirmed duplicate, recommend that the reviewer retain the reconciled record; otherwise prefer a matched or attached record. Use supplied facts; request only missing facts before recommending a survivor. Recommend that the reviewer preserve an attachment from the extra record with the retained record; record that dependency without modifying records or attachments. Removing a reconciled entry can disturb the beginning balance.
- Pending/For review: propose exclusion of the extra download, retaining one for matching or adding. This is not removal of a posted ledger entry.
- Added from feed (Categorized/Posted): propose reviewer consideration of undo, then match to the existing record or exclude the extra. Capture that record's ID. Exclusion alone does not remove an already posted duplicate expense. A linked match may need undo before exclusion. For a supplied closed period, first obtain the accountant's timing decision: undo the addition only if correction in that period is authorized; a later-period adjustment is an alternative, not an additional reversal. List verification of one remaining economic transaction and reconciliation continuity as a reviewer task, without correcting the duplicate twice; verify post-change state only from supplied post-change records, otherwise mark it pending.
- Manually entered: distinguish reviewer consideration of deletion from voiding; void retains a zero-valued record, while deletion is not restorable.
- Invoice payment plus deposit added as income: request payment/deposit linkage; propose undo-and-match of the added deposit rather than a standalone revenue reversal.

These are reviewer proposals only; the plugin performs no live operations.

## E1 — Personal movement and exclusion

[Intuit: Exclude a downloaded bank transaction](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/bank-transactions/exclude-bank-transaction-downloaded-quickbooks/L8icSVVJ6_US_en_US). Retrieved 2026-10-11; readable article loaded. Page displays “Updated 8/10/2026 15:14” (retained as displayed). Relevant section: “Check if you should exclude the transaction.”

Exclusion omits the download from registers and financial reports. Intuit recommends adding a genuine personal expense rather than excluding it so the bank account can agree with its statement. Request entity type and approved non-business treatment; propose the supplied appropriate account. Owner's draw is an example in the article, not a rule for every entity. Missing business purpose or a mixed-use split does not establish an entirely personal expense. Lead with whether to exclude, then give an action for each supplied row. When an open-period, unposted withdrawal is fully supported as personal and approved policy supplies its account, recommend adding it there without an extra approval gate. Keep mixed-use allocation and unknown purpose pending; request a supported split, and require recognition review only where the supplied policy requires it.

## Workpaper use

Use a concise decision and ordered actions for a requested review note, or per-row actions for a feed question; do not force a workpaper. For applicable findings, record product, feed state/origin, posted ID, linked IDs, filter, reconciliation evidence and attachments alongside the proposal. Separate diagnostic uncertainty from category uncertainty. Attach the relevant source label D1 or E1 to the internal rationale. Capture reviewer-selected timing for a supplied closed period, and any reconciliation dependency, before including a correction in the proposed scenario. Request the affected reconciliation report and beginning-balance evidence when a proposal touches reconciled records. An export lacking these fields cannot support a definite platform remedy.
