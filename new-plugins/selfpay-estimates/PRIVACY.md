# Privacy: Selfpay Estimates

This package contains skill instructions, references and an icon. It runs no service and includes no executable scripts.

It reads pasted inputs and files supplied for the estimate task. These can contain patient identity, birth dates, diagnosis or service codes, fees, dates and provider identifiers. Use patient aliases and remove identifiers that are not needed for the draft. Restore required identifiers in the practice's own system.

Inputs are processed by the assistant used for the conversation. The package has no additional recipients, storage or telemetry. Conversation retention and user-saved output files are controlled by that assistant and the user. Delete the conversation or supplied files using the host's controls.

For a current-rule question or source refresh, the assistant may fetch public CMS pages/PDFs, official eCFR or GovInfo rule text, OPM holiday tables and RegInfo form-status pages, including public redirects. Searches are restricted to cms.gov, ecfr.gov, govinfo.gov, opm.gov and reginfo.gov and contain policy terms only. Patient and practice information must not appear in network requests. Pasted-text work needs no account, key or paid API. The package provides no account connectors, patient messaging or uploads.
