## Timing, applicability and fields

5. [CMS GFE FAQ Part 5](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/faqs-good-faith-estimate-uninsured-self-pay-part-5.pdf), listed 2024-12-13, Q1–Q8. Read 2026-10-11. Q6: 3–9 business-day lead -> one business day after scheduling; 10+ -> three; request -> three. Q7: Monday–Friday except federal holidays, by 11:59 p.m.; state enforcers may exclude state holidays too. Q5: a voluntary short-notice scheduling estimate is not PPDR-eligible merely because the bill is $400 higher. Q1 identifies health-sharing and specified non-comprehensive products as uninsured absent other qualifying coverage; Q3 includes dental/vision. Q6 discourages delaying care solely for late estimates; Q8 addresses requests to reasonable office contacts. Q2 requires reasonable inquiry, not mandatory insurer contact.
6. [CMS provider GFE/PPDR guidance](https://www.cms.gov/CCIIO/Resources/Regulations-and-Guidance/Downloads/Guidance-Good-Faith-Estimate-Patient-Provider-Dispute-Resolution-Process-for-Providers-Facilities-CMS-9908-IFC.pdf), 2021-12-21, pp. 3–8. Read 2026-10-11. Cost inquiries are requests. Recurring GFE: explicit span/frequency/count, no more than 12 months. Changes require a new estimate at least one business day before service; a late replacement accepts the original relevant estimate. Request-only estimates are updated on scheduling. Paper-mail issuance uses the postmark deadline, not arrival (printed p. 8). Written paper/electronic delivery follows patient preference, can be saved/printed, and issued GFEs form part of the medical record. Use Part 3 for the newer co-provider enforcement policy, not this file's expired 2022 cutoff.

## Conditional fee paths

12. [CMS GFE FAQ Part 4](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/faqs-good-faith-estimate-uninsured-self-pay-part-4.pdf), 2022-12-27, corrected 2023-02-21, Q1–Q3. Read 2026-10-11. Q1 distinguishes new patients whose sliding-fee eligibility is unresolved from existing patients with established discounts. Q2 allows an abbreviated estimate only when the provider expects no bill for all items/services on the covered dates, or any items/services if unscheduled; also requires no included co-provider/co-facility care and retains identity/provider/location/date and patient-rights content. Q3 explains resulting PPDR implications. Mixed paid/free care stays on the standard path. Read the actual conditional provisions before using them.
