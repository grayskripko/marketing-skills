## Numeric and date anchors

- Form 943/944 annual lookback: second preceding calendar year; 2026 → 2024. For a transition, include that year's relevant 941 annual taxes or 944 taxes as directed by Publication 15; do not use an empty current-form history as zero.
- 941 return-payment exception: current or prior quarter line-12 tax < $2,500 and no current-quarter $100,000 next-day event. A current quarter at exactly $2,500 still qualifies if the prior quarter is below it and the other condition holds.
- Small shortfall: no more than max($100, 0.02 × required deposit), with timely makeup. Monthly makeup: return deadline. Semiweekly: earlier of return deadline or first Wednesday/Friday on/after following month's 15th. This does not waive the full-deposit target.
- Deposit holidays in 2026 (Publication 15 section 11): 01-01, 01-19, 02-16, 04-16, 05-25, 06-19, 07-03, 09-07, 10-12, 11-11, 11-26, 12-25. State-only holidays are excluded for deposits. Filing holiday rules may depend on filing location; check applicable form/calendar guidance instead of assuming deposit rules cover all filing situations.
- Derived 2026 941 dates: Q1 04-30 / conditional 05-11; Q2 07-31 / conditional 08-10; Q3 11-02 / conditional 11-10. The conditional date is the 10th of the second month, not standard adjusted date + 10 days.
- Q4/annual 2026 payroll-return standard date derives as 2027-02-01, conditional 2027-02-10. Confirm year-specific annual instructions and the 2027 calendar before final use. The W-2 deadline is already explicit in 2026 W-2 instructions; it is not conditional on deposits.
