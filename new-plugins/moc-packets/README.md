# MOC Packets

Build a management of change (MOC) packet from a proposed process change, its design specification, the site MOC form and the affected-document export. For plant MOC coordinators and process engineers at chemical manufacturers, operators and process safety consultancies. Paste or attach the change description, current and proposed design data, the MOC form and your document, training and approval records. No account, API key, install or connection is needed.

## What it does

| Skill | Result |
|---|---|
| moc-packet-build | Before/after table and clause-to-evidence matrix for one change against OSHA PSM 1910.119(l), PSSR (i), mechanical integrity (j) and EPA RMP 68.75, with missing and contradicted items |
| moc-open-items | Open-items register across one or more MOCs: document updates, training sign-offs, authorizations, PSSR items, overdue temporary changes |
| replacement-in-kind-check | Attribute-by-attribute comparison of a replacement against the existing design specification, with differences and unstated values for the owner's determination |

Try: "Put together the MOC package for this valve change", "What's still open on these MOCs before start-up?", "Does this substitute gasket match our piping class?", "Check our MOC form against PSM".

The skills carry the (l)(2) items and timing, (l)(3) training before start-up, PSI and procedure updates, the PSSR trigger, OSHA's enforcement answers on replacement in kind, maintenance-frequency and organizational changes, MOC record retention, RMP plan-update deadlines, and the dating and paperwork errors found by the US Chemical Safety Board. Dated official sources are in [references/moc-sources.md](references/moc-sources.md).

## Limits

The skills work only from what you supply. They do not approve a change, decide that something is a replacement in kind, write a technical basis, run a hazard review, set operating limits or stamp a design. An item counts as done only when a supplied record shows it; with too little data they say what is missing instead of concluding. Results support, but do not replace, the sign-off of your authorized approvers and the qualified engineer or process safety reviewer. Temporary-change extensions follow your site procedure. Scope is US federal OSHA PSM and EPA RMP; other jurisdictions or site rules are applied only from the procedure you paste.

## What this package runs and connects to

A skills-only package: Markdown instructions and reference text, JSON metadata, a license and a static SVG icon. No scripts, dependencies, hooks, MCP servers or background processes. Network scope during use: none. The skills fetch no URLs, run no searches and send no data anywhere; source links in the references are for human reading. The publisher receives no task data and runs no telemetry. Your assistant provider processes the conversation under its own settings.
