# Privacy: Excel Formulas & Queries

This package contains instructions, references and an icon. It runs no service.

It uses cells, formulas, headers, sample records and query text you paste to draft spreadsheet advice. These may contain business records or personal data. Paste only the fields the task needs and redact names, contact details and unrelated records.

Network scope: nothing is fetched. No account access, file access, credential collection, telemetry or external writes. No recipients beyond the assistant provider processing your conversation under its own terms. The package stores nothing; the assistant's retention policy applies. You can remove pasted material or delete the conversation through the assistant's controls.

Questions can be filed in the repository's issue tracker: https://github.com/grayskripko/marketing-skills/issues
