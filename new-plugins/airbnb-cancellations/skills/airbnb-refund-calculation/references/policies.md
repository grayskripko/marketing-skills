## Conflicts that change the result

475 monthly Firm/Strict describes spent nights plus next 30, then separately says after check-in no refund/all nights. Keep after-check-in monthly outcome unresolved until reservation-specific terms or current Airbnb quote supplies the result. Do not use the short-stay rule to erase that conflict.

3832 legacy Italy late-policy clauses mix host and guest perspectives ('paid' vs 'refund'). Use clear timing rules; request the reservation breakdown for money when those clauses do not unambiguously identify the recipient. Its old 24h-after-check-in issue clause differs from current 2868's 72h-from-discovery rule; use the current override with case-specific evidence.

3066 states the cleaning transition for Australia; 2812 describes a general booking-date split. Apply 2812 where the supplied booking aligns, and request its breakdown if regional terms conflict. Never assume cleaning is always returned in full before arrival.

## Calculation conventions authored for this workflow

Ledger separation, one-time subtraction of completed refunds, exact timestamp comparisons, no invented allocation of unequal night prices, conditional output for missing data, and no double direct refund are workflow rules of thumb. They organize the supplied facts; they do not create additional Airbnb entitlements. Host net payout needs actual payout deductions and splits.
