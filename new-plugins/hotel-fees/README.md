# Hotel Fees

Check US hotel room prices, resort fees and checkout totals against the FTC fee rule; identify mandatory fees, permitted exclusions and total-price prominence, then draft corrected displays.
For hotel teams, lodging advertisers and reviewers working on US short-term lodging prices. Supply pasted text or readable exports of rates, fee conditions and booking steps; add screenshots or layout details to assess prominence. No accounts, keys or installs are needed.

| Skill | Input | Result |
|---|---|---|
| check-hotel-fees | advertised rates, fee schedule, selection conditions and checkout breakdown | fee classifications, reconciled totals, specific display findings and corrections |
| draft-hotel-prices | approved prices, fee purposes, stay details and target surfaces | replacement ad, search-result and checkout text with placement instructions |

## Try it

- “Is this mandatory resort fee included early enough?”
- “Check these booking export rows and tell me which prices to fix.”
- “Rewrite our search card and checkout price block from this fee schedule.”

## Inputs that matter

Include dates, nights, rooms, guests, currency, per-night versus per-stay fees, payment options, discount eligibility, add-on defaults, tax amounts and the point when payment is requested. Unknown amounts or purposes become specific missing-detail markers. Plain text establishes wording and arithmetic; visual hierarchy needs supplied visual evidence.

## Data and network

Reads only material supplied in the conversation or files you identify for this task. No network requests, telemetry, account access or external writes. Official rule knowledge is bundled in each skill and its dated references. To update it, paste newer official text or update the package's references. Guest names and payment details are unnecessary.
