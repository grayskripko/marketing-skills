# FTC lodging fee rules — source register

Read and rechecked 2026-10-11. Scope: US temporary lodging offers, ads and checkout displays; government charges and fee selection conditions must be established from the supplied transaction. These are paraphrased decision notes, not copied source text.

## Current authority

- [FTC Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/rule-unfair-or-deceptive-fees-frequently-asked-questions). Loads as HTML. Effective 2025-05-12; page labels the guide May 2025 without a day-level revision date. Sections on mandatory fees, ancillary services, payment processing, consumer choices and promotions resolve opt-out fees, same-platform payment alternatives, selected extras and restricted discounts. Its national/regional advertising section requires the maximum applicable total for the targeted area. Coverage section includes intermediaries, business buyers and discounted hotel extended stays; it excludes ongoing tenancy and sets no fixed length threshold.
- [16 CFR Part 464](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-464). Loads as HTML; displayed currency 2026-10-07, read 2026-10-11. Section 464.1 defines government charges, shipping, total price and disclosure characteristics. Section 464.2 requires upfront totals and relative prominence, excluded-charge explanations and final payment before consent. Section 464.3 covers fee purpose and refundability representations. Section 464.4 preserves stronger state protections. Title-wide amendment date is not a Part 464 amendment date.
- [Federal Register final rule, document 2024-30293](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2024-30293.pdf). Loads as a 281-page PDF. Published 2025-01-10; effective 2025-05-12. Section III.A.8 discusses mandatory fees, maximum total, itemization and exclusions; III.B.1 discusses contingent fees, payment surcharges and promotional prices. Use alongside the current codified text, not as a claim of later amendments.
- [FTC effective-date release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/05/ftc-rule-unfair-or-deceptive-fees-take-effect-may-12-2025). Loads as HTML, dated 2025-05-05. Confirms implementation on 2025-05-12 and that the rule regulates price transparency rather than banning a fee type or amount.

## Field decisions

| Supplied fact | Result | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Required cleaning collected at property | Include upfront; collection location does not make it optional | FAQ, mandatory charges; final rule III.A.8 |
| Facilities fee removed only if challenged | Include; default billing blocks effective selection | FAQ, ancillary services |
| Card fee with cash available only on arrival | Do not presume viable online alternative | FAQ, payment processing |
| Affirmatively chosen extra | May exclude upfront; disclose and add before payment | FAQ, optional ancillary services; §464.2(c) |
| Hotel calls its own charge a tax | Require actual government imposition before classifying as exclusion | §464.1 and §464.3 |
| Member discount offered to an ineligible public audience | General total outranks it until eligibility is met | FAQ, discounts; final rule III.B.1.e |
| Export has prices but no visual hierarchy | Arithmetic/wording can be checked; request the relevant visual evidence | §464.1 disclosure characteristics |

## Calculation and output conventions

These are package workflow choices: preserve fee units, compute per-stay charges once, show formulas, give the corrected display before explanations and mark missing amounts instead of inventing them. The rule sets no required table shape, pixel minimum or fixed nightly-versus-stay copy template. For known stay data, show the complete stay total; any nightly equivalent should use the same mandatory-fee-inclusive total and remain secondary.
