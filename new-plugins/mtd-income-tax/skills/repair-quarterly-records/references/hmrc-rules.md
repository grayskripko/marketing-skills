## Quarterly periods and policy
https://www.gov.uk/guidance/use-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax/send-quarterly-updates
Manual updated 2026-09-07. Sections: “What will be sent”, “Update periods and deadlines”, “Making changes after the fourth quarterly update”.

Updates contain cumulative category totals, not invoices. Standard starts 6 April, calendar starts 1 April. Ends: 5 July/5 October/5 January/5 April or 30 June/30 September/31 December/31 March. Deadlines: 7 August/7 November/7 February/7 May. Choice locks for the tax year after a submission. No-activity quarters still require updates. Tax adjustments are not prerequisites. 2026/27 late-quarterly penalty points are waived; tax-return deadlines remain. Later corrections normally flow into the next cumulative update; where a fourth-update resend is needed, it must precede tax adjustments; then complete annual adjustments and finalisation.

## Required records and concessions
https://www.gov.uk/guidance/use-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax/create-digital-records
Manual updated 2026-09-07. Sections: “What to include”, “Simpler categorisation”, “Correcting your digital records”, “Using more than one software product”.

Required fields: amount, date, category. Each trade is separate; UK properties aggregate; foreign properties retain individual records and aggregate separately. Joint records cover the user's share. Simplification below £90,000 is source-specific; reaching £90,000 requires full categories from year start and next year. Foreign-property turnover is exempt from this test. Residential finance remains separate. Joint-property expenses can be deferred by choice; sole-property expenses cannot. Without a stated income-only election, include supplied expense shares in working totals; do not treat optional deferral as a blocker. Net income needs gross income and expense records. User/software sequence: correct the source, then transfer digitally; agent source edits require user authorisation. After fourth update: repair the digital source and resend where needed before tax adjustments, then complete annual adjustments/finalisation; alternatively make a supported category-total correction mirrored in digital records before finalisation. CSV import/export and linked cells can preserve links; retyping/copy-paste cannot move already-submitted records. The user must keep records at least five years after the tax year's 31 January filing deadline; explain this when relevant, without creating agent-side retention.

## Exact category direction
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/update-notice-for-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax/making-tax-digital-for-income-tax-update-notice
Updated 2026-03-27; publication wrapper also loaded:
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/update-notice-for-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax
Sections 1, 2.1, 2.2 and 3 define update start/end and category totals. Category presence does not prove tax deductibility.

### Trade categories (section 1)
Income: Turnover; Other business income.
Expenses: Cost of goods bought for resale or goods used; Construction industry — payments to subcontractors; Wages, salaries, and other staff costs; Car, van, and travel expenses; Rent, rates, power, and insurance costs; Repairs and maintenance of property and equipment; Phone, fax, stationery, and other office costs; Advertising; Business entertainment costs; Interest on bank and other loans; Bank, credit card and other financial charges; Accountancy, legal and other professional fees; Other business expenses.

### UK property categories (section 2.1)
Income: Total rent; Other income from property; Premiums for the grant of a lease; Reverse premiums and inducements.
Expenses: Rent, rates, insurance and ground rents; Property repairs and maintenance; Non-residential property finance costs; Residential property finance costs; Residential finance costs brought forward; Legal, management and other professional fees; Costs of services provided, including wages; Travel expenses; Other allowable property expenses.

### Foreign property categories (section 2.2)
Income: Total rents and other receipts; Premiums for the grant of a lease.
Expenses: Allowable property expenses (rent, repairs, legal fees, cost of services provided).
Section 3 permits income-only joint-property reporting. Do not apply joint treatment to solely owned property.

## Cash-basis agent receipts
https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim1094
Read 2026-10-11, “Amounts Paid to Agents on Behalf of the Landlord”. Rent is recognised when paid to the collecting agent, not when passed on to the landlord. Obtain gross amounts, agent receipt dates and expense payment dates for period allocation; a net payout date alone is insufficient.

## Software handoff
https://www.gov.uk/guidance/choose-the-right-software-for-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax
Updated 2026-07-13. Sections: “Types of software”, “Choosing more than one product”, “How to choose the right software features”.

Software can create records or bridge existing records to HMRC. Multiple products require digital links. One software product is used for each separate submission. Check support for the user's income sources and accounting/update periods. HMRC's linked finder lists recognised software; recognition does not constitute a product recommendation. A supplied pack is not an account integration. Verify CSV imports/resends with record counts and cumulative previews. For supported category-total corrections, compare software totals and correction confirmation with the corrected digital records; do not require a bridging preview for that route.

## Corrections versus annual adjustments
https://www.gov.uk/guidance/use-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax/adjust-your-self-employment-and-property-income
Manual updated 2026-09-07. Sections: “Making adjustments”, “Accounting adjustments”, “Adjustments … chosen accounting period”.

Correct errors promptly during the year. After the fourth update, annual totals can need reliefs, disallowable-cost, prepayment/accrual or capital-allowance adjustments before the return. Cash basis is the default; traditional accounting can require annual accounting adjustments. A 2026/27 starter selecting calendar periods needs no 1–5 April adjustment of the kind applicable to 2025/26 testers. HMRC says further guidance will follow for some other accounting dates: do not invent those procedures.
