# Privacy: Review Evidence

This plugin contains instructions and reference text. It ships no executable service.

- Data: selected authorized work notes, goals, ratings and feedback may include employee names, roles, dates and work history. Supply only what the review needs. Redact contact details, medical/accommodation information and unrelated employees' records. Removing names does not guarantee anonymity.
- Purpose: organize evidence, draft a performance review or self-assessment, and check claims against supplied sources.
- Recipients: your existing AI assistant provider processes the conversation under its own terms. The plugin adds no external recipient, service, telemetry or data transfer of its own.
- Retention: the plugin adds no storage. Your assistant's conversation and file retention apply. An internal source table means an intended audience, not enforced privacy or a private data store.
- Network: nothing is fetched. No web search, URL reading, HRIS or email connection, monitoring, sending, credentials or system writes. A host may use its existing file-reading or calculation tools on the supplied material.
- Controls: use selected records, redact before sharing, inspect drafts and keep internal tables separate from employee-facing text. Use your assistant's own conversation deletion controls.
