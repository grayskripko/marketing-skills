# Source notes

These are drafting rules of thumb chosen for this package, not rules from law, a platform or a study. No numerical threshold or legal conclusion is prescribed.

Compare supplied records. Separate supported requirements, proposed changes and explicit approvals. Correct unsupported draft additions without turning settled facts into questions. Preserve unresolved choices and the status of budgets and dates. Keep private internal notes out of client drafts.

The procedures were informed by project research on pre-quote client briefs. That research is secondary evidence, not a new external source visit or proof of current market demand. No external source was fetched for the build. Comparative performance remains untested. All deciding steps are in SKILL.md.
