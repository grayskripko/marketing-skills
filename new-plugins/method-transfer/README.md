# Method Transfer

Check an analytical method transfer between a sending and a receiving laboratory: the method differences between sites, whether the results meet the protocol's acceptance criteria, and the transfer report. For analytical-transfer leads, CMC and QC scientists at pharmaceutical companies, CDMOs and contract laboratories. You paste the method texts, protocol, results and deviations into the request; no account, connector or setup is needed.

| Skill | What you get |
|---|---|
| transfer-gap-check | The differences between the filed method, the sending lab's method, the receiving lab's SOP and the protocol, most consequential first |
| transfer-acceptance-check | Passed, not passed or cannot be concluded yet, with each protocol criterion tested against both sites' results |
| transfer-report | A draft transfer report in your template or a standard structure, with a conclusion that follows only from the predetermined criteria |

Try: "What differs between our HPLC method and the CRO's SOP?", "Did this transfer pass the protocol criteria?", "Draft the transfer report from this protocol and these results."

## What it does

Each skill first lines up your records: method numbers, versions and effective dates; protocol approval date; lot numbers; preparations, analysts, instruments and runs per site; units and reporting basis; deviations and their status. It catches a protocol that cites a different method version from the one run, a sending method that has drifted from the filed method, near-match lot numbers, missing preparations, results produced before the protocol was approved, and open deviations behind a result. Then it applies the points a plain answer tends to miss: criteria must be set before testing and are never widened afterwards, both sites test the same lots, stability-indicating methods need degraded samples at both sites, and transfer criteria for an unchanged method do not cover a receiving SOP that changes it. Each rule has a dated source in [references](references/official-sources.md).
