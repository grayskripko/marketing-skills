# Privacy: Grant Applications

The grant-review skills process only text, attachments and local files you explicitly supply through your existing assistant; they prohibit web search, URL retrieval, account access, dependency downloads and paid APIs, treat supplied links as text, and use Grants.gov and GitHub links only as source or support references. They permit existing local file, calculation and word-counting tools, which may execute code, but do not direct software installation, application submission, message delivery, publication or account-setting changes; originals must not be overwritten and copies must not be retained by default. The manifests configure no executable launcher, hook, MCP/LSP server or account connector, and the publisher operates no task-data collection, telemetry or storage service. Your assistant provider controls conversation, attachment and tool-artifact retention under its terms. Supplied grant material may contain staff, donor, beneficiary, signature or financial data: remove unnecessary identifiers, personal contacts, bank details and credentials, retaining nonidentifying signer role and signed/unsigned status where required. Answers should cite sections or rows rather than repeat sensitive fields; delete uploaded material or conversations through your host when appropriate.
