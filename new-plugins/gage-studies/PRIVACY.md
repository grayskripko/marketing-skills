# Privacy

Gage Studies is a skills-only package: Markdown instructions, one reference file, JSON platform metadata, a license and a static SVG icon. It bundles no executable scripts, dependencies, servers, connectors or hooks, and installing it runs no program.

It works only on the measurement data, software output and study details you paste or explicitly supply. It makes no network requests: no web search, URL fetching, uploads, account connections or telemetry. If your assistant has a code tool, the calculation skill may use it on your machine or in your assistant's sandbox to compute results from the data you supplied; nothing is sent anywhere by the plugin.

The publisher receives no data and runs no collection or storage service for this plugin. Your conversation and attachments are processed and retained under your assistant provider's own settings. Part numbers, customer names and drawing details are not needed for the calculations; you can replace them with neutral labels.
