# Privacy

Spares Stocking is a skills-only package: Markdown instructions, one reference file, JSON platform metadata, a license and a static SVG icon. It bundles no executable scripts, dependencies, servers, connectors or hooks, and installing it runs no program.

It works only on the stock, reservation, purchase order, usage and policy rows you paste into your request. It does not open files, attachments, memory or earlier conversations, and it makes no network requests: no web search, URL fetching, uploads, account connections or telemetry.

The publisher receives no data and runs no collection or storage service for this plugin. Your conversation is processed and retained under your assistant provider's own settings. Supplier names, prices and site names are not needed for the checks; you can leave them out or replace them with neutral labels.
