## Authored defaults and operational practices

These are this plugin's rules of thumb, not Google requirements: 90-day comparable refresh review; same-season annual comparison; at least 30% click decline with at least 100 clicks in each period as a review screen; fewer than 10 organic clicks over a full 12 months with zero known meaningful key events and zero known relevant referring domains as a pruning-review screen; launch and 30-day follow-up checks. They prioritize inspection, never authorize automatic removal or establish statistical significance. Sparse, new, seasonal and long-cycle pages need different windows or no numerical screen. User thresholds control when consistent with evidence and continuing reader needs.

Full outer joins, verified match keys, provisional supplied-row reporting groups, impression-weighted position estimates, raw URL preservation, safe row-ID mappings, output prioritization, rollback copies, useful-section inventories and linked-heading checks are authored operational practices and rules of thumb. Reporting groups do not prove content equivalence or Google-selected canonicals. They guard against lost rows, inflated totals and broken reader routes; they do not guarantee ranking or link-signal preservation.
