# Privacy — Scope Change

This package contains instructions, references and a local icon. It runs no service and ships no executable tools.

It uses project terms, supplied scope versions, approval messages, incoming requests, rates and estimates to compare work and prepare drafts. These records may contain names and confidential business information. Readable files are accessed only when selected by the user and supported by the assistant. No account, credentials or paid API is required.

Network scope: nothing is fetched, including linked documents, robots.txt or search results. No messages, account drafts, approvals or financial writes are made. There is no telemetry and no plugin storage. The AI assistant provider processes the conversation under its own terms and retention settings; those controls also govern uploaded files.

Redact personal email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses and unrelated internal notes before providing material. Client drafts omit personal contact details and private background that is not part of the requested deliverable. Use your assistant's controls to delete conversations and files where available.
