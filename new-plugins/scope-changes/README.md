# Scope Changes

Check whether a new client request is extra work by comparing it with the approved scope and later approved changes. Draft a client reply and proposed change order.

For freelancers and small agencies. Paste your scope of work or proposal with its version label, revision and acceptance terms, approval messages and the new request. Add your rates and estimates if you want proposed costs or dates. Pasted text needs no account, installation, API key or paid API. Readable user-selected files work when your assistant supports them.

## Choose the job

| Skill | Ask it to |
|---|---|
| check-scope-request | Check whether each requested item is included, additional or unclear, with supporting passages. |
| compare-approved-changes | Compare versions and messages to show what was approved and what remains proposed. |
| draft-change-order | Draft proposed additions, supplied costs, timing and approval needs. |
| reply-to-extra-work | Write only the client reply when that is all you need. |

For example: “Is this extra work? Here is our agreed scope and the client's email.” Or: “Draft a change order using my estimates; the requested deadline is not approved.”

## What you get

Included revisions and repairs to agreed work stay separate from extra work. An unclear request stays unclear until the relevant detail is confirmed. A new file, a quote or a client request does not automatically replace approved terms. The assistant checks explicit acceptance of a specific proposal against the supplied approval terms.

Proposed fees and dates remain proposals. Review the draft before using it with your client. The draft does not decide whether an agreement is legally enforceable or payment is owed. To interpret a contract, you need the jurisdiction that applies to the contract and a qualified lawyer's review. If the jurisdiction is missing, it stays unknown.

## Data and network

Nothing is fetched: no URLs, web search, accounts, connectors or paid APIs. The instructions use pasted text and readable files you select. They do not send messages, create account drafts, start client work or change financial records. There is no plugin code, telemetry or persistent storage. Your assistant processes and may retain the conversation under its own terms. Redact personal contact details and unrelated private notes.

## Limits and troubleshooting

If an attachment cannot be read, paste the relevant passages with their version labels. If approvals conflict, paste the relevant messages and versions. The assistant can draft without estimates. It marks missing estimates as [DETAIL NEEDED: …]. It does not monitor accounts or authenticate signatures.
