# California payment sources

Read 2026-10-11. This self-contained register records the payment sources and reading date.

- [2026 CSLB law book](https://cslb.ca.gov/Resources/GuidesAndPublications/2026/2026_CSLB_Law_Book.pdf), printed pp.387–388 (PDF pp.410–411), BPC 7159.5(a)(1)–(8): entire contract base excludes finance charges; ordinary deposit cap min($1,000, 10% of base); progress schedule dollars/cents with work/material detail; no payment ahead of performed work or delivered materials, except deposit. Qualifying full security exempts only identified provisions.
- Same book, printed pp.374–385, BPC 7159: contract fields, notices, January 2026 contact/subcontractor changes. The exception omits cap/progress statements and lien warning, not every field or lien releases.
- Same book, printed pp.391–395, BPC 7159.10: conforming service/repair at most $750, buyer initiated, no extra goods/services, no payment until completed/approved as applicable. A failed condition invokes BPC 7159(c)–(e).
- Same book, printed pp.455–467, 16 CCR 858–858.9: blanket coverage and approval, cancellation/rescission, posted record details and retained historical records.
- [CSLB consumer guide](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/Resources/GuidesAndPublications/HomeImprovementContractsConsumerGuide.pdf), October 2024 footer, printed p.4: no special-order exception, blanket-bond branch, final pool plastering plus required equipment/decking/fencing, separate finance charge and proportional commission. Predates January 2026 contract contact/disclosure changes.
- [CSLB What Is a Contract?](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/Consumers/Hire_A_Contractor/Home_Improvement_Contracts/What_Is_A_Contract.aspx): ordinary cap and no special-order exception. It omits the security branch.
- [CSLB binding agreements](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/Consumers/Hire_A_Contractor/Contracts_And_Binding_Agreements.aspx): practical blanket-bond exception summary.
- [CSLB bond requirements](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/Contractors/Maintain_License/Bond_Information/Bond_Requirements.aspx): $25,000 ordinary license bond from January 1, 2023. Its surety blanket-endorsement list is not the full-performance blanket-bond approval.

16 CCR 858.2(a), qualifying-individual certification, printed p.461 of the same 2026 official book (read 2026-10-11): approved blanket-bond contract forms contain notice that the bond is on file with the Registrar, or the issuing surety’s name and address. This is additional form information, not an exemption from nonpayment fields.
