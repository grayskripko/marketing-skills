## Statutory decision map

All entries below paraphrase California statutory provisions reproduced in the 2026 official book; source URL and reading date are in the table above. These are legal field rules, not authored thresholds.

- **7159(a), (b):** qualifying residential improvements over $500 in aggregate; exceptions include conforming service-and-repair contracts. A quote-stage review identifies what must enter the eventual agreement; document stage is a factual question, not determined by its title.
- **7159(c)(1)–(3):** legibility, ordinary 10-point text/10-point bold headings; signed/dated copy before work; first-page signing date, cancellation destination name/address/email, cancellation-address statement and assistance phone. AB 1327 and SB 517 amendments effective January 1, 2026; printed p.385 history says chapter 585 prevails.
- **7159(c)(4)–(8):** lien-release provision; incorporated written signed change-order form; owner’s right to demand performance/payment bond near signatures; subcontractor yes/no checkboxes and, if yes, on-request identifying-list disclosure; that same list statement appears on each change order, without a statutory requirement to repeat the yes/no boxes; contractor cannot have an interest in joint control. These are not erased by the payment exception.
- **7159(d)(1)–(7):** identity/license, applicable salesperson identity/registration, contract heading, filled-copy notice (12-point bold), price, separate finance charge, project/material/equipment heading and detail; pool plan/scale drawing.
- **7159(d)(8), (9):** actual downpayment and statutory cap statement (12-point bold); progress section with heading, phase details, dollars/cents and statutory no-advance statement (12-point bold). Notice presence alone does not prove the amounts comply.
- **7159(d)(10)–(13):** approximate start and substantial commencement; approximate completion; incorporated-document list if applicable; extra-work/change-order note.
- **7159(e)(1)–(5):** CGL and workers’ compensation notices with truthful applicable option and required attachment statement if attached; extra-work information including possible recovery despite procedural failure; full lien warning; CSLB information in at least 12-point. Do not infer insurance exemption eligibility merely because no employees are stated.
- **7159(e)(6), (7), (f):** ordinary three-business-day notice, five for senior buyer (65+) only for contracts entered into on or after January 1, 2021 under 7159(f), declared-emergency restoration seven. Ordinary three/five exceptions include negotiation at contractor’s business; do not transfer that exception to the seven-day branch automatically. Right-to-cancel notice: 12-point bold, signature/receipt/date, language of oral presentation; completed duplicate detachable cancellation forms in contract language. Permitted attachment requires the statutory acknowledgment checkbox. Use the actual subsection for precise wording; email is an allowed cancellation method in 2026. This package checks fields, not cancellation deadlines.
- **7159.5(a)(1)–(7):** base includes profit/labor/materials and excludes finance charges; deposit lesser of $1,000 and 10%; progress schedule dollars/cents and work/material detail; nondeposit payments cannot exceed performed work or delivered materials, including financed advances; paid-work releases on request before next payment; proportional commission if provided.
- **7159.5(a)(8):** qualifying performance/payment or lien/completion bonds, or registrar-approved equivalent/joint control covering full performance/payment, exempt (a)(3)–(5). Also exempts the specified cap statement, progress details/statement and lien warning; permits advance payment. Not a blanket exemption from the contract statute.
- **7159.6:** change-order scope, price change and effect on progress payments or completion date. Failure does not automatically foreclose compensation for performed work under other remedies.
- **7159.10(a), (b):** service/repair amount at most $750; buyer initiates; only necessary goods/services; no payment until fully corrected and, if applicable, accepted by building department. A document presented as service/repair that fails any condition falls under 7159(c)–(e) regardless of price.
- **7159.10(d), (e):** separate service/repair written-form fields/notices, pricing or time/material estimate/rates, parts-return choice, one service charge, cancellation-before-work notice. Buyer can cancel until both signed copy received and work begins; failure of qualifying conditions creates further cancellation rights. The cancellation notice needs buyer signature/date and immediate proximity to the buyer signature space; an attachment also requires the applicable contract checkbox and 12-point-bold acknowledgment. Under (e)(12)(A)(ii)–(iii), substitute five for every three for buyers 65+ only for contracts entered into on or after January 1, 2021; assess older agreements under historical text. Keep seven for the declared-emergency branch. Do not reuse ordinary notices blindly.
- **16 CCR 858.1, 858.8, 858.9:** blanket security covers 100% of outstanding home-improvement obligations. Check explicit CSLB approval, number, amount, surety and disposition/effective dates. Cancellation rescinds approval; historical bond details persist at least five years, so old number/amount does not establish current exception.
