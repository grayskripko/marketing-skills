# UTM Governance

Create a UTM naming convention and check campaign links against your campaign register. Turn pasted links and existing rules into a naming sheet, flagged links, corrected URL proposals and meaningful exceptions. For marketing teams and agencies coordinating campaign tags. Supply pasted text, CSV/TSV tables or readable exports; no account, key or installation beyond the skills package is needed.

| Skill | Input | Output |
|---|---|---|
| utm-taxonomy | register, naming rules, approved aliases and exceptions | naming sheet, identity-preserving register, unresolved decisions |
| utm-link-audit | URL list, register and approved naming rules | flagged rows, corrected URL proposals, exception register |

Examples: “Settle our campaign names before the agency brief.” “Check these newsletter links and give me the corrected versions.” A combined request reconciles names before auditing links.

Historical campaign names and IDs remain exact. Future spelling proposals and reporting aliases are separate from original records. Conflicting IDs, duplicate query fields, ambiguous aliases, missing values and published-link reuse remain visible decisions. Corrections preserve unrelated parameters, click IDs and anchors. Sensitive or signed links need an owner rebuild, or a supplied public replacement destination, before a usable replacement can be proposed.

Google-specific knowledge includes manual parameter mappings, case-sensitive reporting, conditional click-ID fallback, channel matching and fields whose reporting is disputed by current documentation. Sources and dates are in [references/sources.md](references/sources.md).

Network: none. Skills read supplied text and files and bundled references; they do not fetch campaign URLs, robots.txt, redirects, Google accounts or remote registers. No service, telemetry or account integration ships. Deliverables are proposals in the conversation. Redact personal data, access codes and signatures before supplying URLs.
