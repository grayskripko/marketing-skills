# Privacy

The skills use feed text, exports, and screenshot descriptions supplied in the conversation. No network is required for those inputs. They have no account connector, telemetry, external storage, or bundled code. Conversation and tool retention follow the host application’s settings.

If browsing is available, official requirements may be fetched from help.pinterest.com, create.pinterest.com and policy.pinterest.com. When you request public URL inspection, the agent may fetch your feed and specific relevant article/image URLs, their redirects, and robots.txt for each inspected host, including redirect hosts. Fetches disclose the requested URLs to their hosts and the browsing provider. Never submit private feeds, signed links, passwords, cookies, or access tokens. Redact personal information from screenshots. Website verification values stay in your own Pinterest and website interfaces.

The agent provides instructions and proposed feed edits. You make changes in your own website and Pinterest settings. No Pins are published by these skills.
