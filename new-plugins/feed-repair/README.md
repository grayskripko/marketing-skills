# Feed Repair

Check this pasted RSS feed and tell me why Pinterest won't turn my articles into Pins.
For website publishers using Pinterest native RSS publishing. Bring pasted feed XML, an export, or a screenshot you describe; no account or setup is required for diagnosis. Connecting a feed requires a Pinterest business account, a claimed website, a hosted RSS URL, and desktop settings.

- **Check RSS feed:** whole-feed blockers and item defects, exact supported repairs, and setup steps when requested or relevant.
- **Set up RSS publishing:** website claiming and desktop settings, then timing and visibility troubleshooting.

No account connection, automatic monitoring, or publishing is included. The agent guides you through Pinterest settings. A pasted fragment cannot establish whole-feed validity or live reachability.

Network: no network is needed for pasted inputs. With available browsing, the agent may fetch official pages at help.pinterest.com, create.pinterest.com and policy.pinterest.com to refresh requirements. Only when you request URL inspection may it fetch your public feed and the specific article/image URLs needed for diagnosis, their redirects, and robots.txt for each inspected host, including redirect hosts. It does not crawl Pinterest or use publishing services. No telemetry or bundled executable code.

References record official requirements checked on 2026-10-11. Check them again before the planned 2026-10-13 submission. See [privacy](PRIVACY.md) and [build notes](BUILD-NOTES.md).
