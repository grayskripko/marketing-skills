# Privacy

Cookie Banners contains skill instructions and reference text. It runs no service, executable scripts or connectors.

It reads the banner text, screenshots, CMP settings and inventory material supplied in the conversation or files explicitly selected for the task. These may contain domains, provider names, purpose lists and consent configuration. Cookie values, visitor identifiers, account identifiers and credentials are not needed; redact them before sharing. The skills do not repeat incidental personal data.

The material is used to prepare corrections and evidence checklists in the current conversation. No URLs are fetched, no searches are run, and no network destinations receive data from the plugin. There is no telemetry, CMP login or account modification.

The plugin stores nothing independently. The assistant provider processes and retains the conversation and attachments under its own settings. You control which material you provide and can remove it through your assistant's conversation controls.
