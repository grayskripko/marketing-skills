# Copywriting Assistant

Draft Google responsive search ads from your facts, with character counts and notes on where required text must appear. Review ad combinations, US digital-ad disclosures and missing product details readers need to choose. Also draft pages and email, or review existing copy for what makes it hard to understand or act on.
For marketers, founders and editors who know their readers and the next step they want them to take.
Supply your brief or copy, product facts and intended next step. Once your assistant has the plugin, you need no connected account, key, paid API or extra software.

## What this package runs and connects to

This is a skills-only package of Markdown instructions and reference text, JSON metadata, and a static SVG icon. It includes no executable scripts, dependencies, installers, hooks, MCP or LSP servers, or background processes. Installing it does not run a program or connect an account.

These skills work only from task text, attachments, and local files that you explicitly supply. They do not instruct the assistant to run code, install software, search the web, fetch URLs or robots.txt, call APIs, send messages, publish content, or change account settings. URLs in this package identify sources or support pages; they are not connection endpoints or retrieval instructions. A supplied URL is treated as text: paste the relevant content instead.

The publisher receives no task data and operates no collection, telemetry, or storage service for this plugin. Your assistant provider still processes and may retain your conversation and attachments under its own terms. Any output file you explicitly request is created by your assistant's existing file tools, not by a bundled program.

## Skills

| Skill | Give it | You get |
|---|---|---|
| copy-from-reader-brief | Brief, reader, next step and facts | Search-ad asset set with counts and placement notes, or a page, email or other ad draft; missing details marked |
| reader-task-copy-review | Copy and what its readers need to do | Specific passages, unsafe ad combinations or missing reader details, with suggested changes; no automatic rewrite or score |

## Try it

- “Draft Google responsive search ads for these inserts. Keep the compatibility restriction in every relevant asset.”
- “Draft an email for these subscribers. Use the facts below and ask them to choose a pack.”
- “Review this ad for workshop owners deciding whether to request a quote. What is missing before they can act?”

For example, a brief saying “fits only our A6 covers; some other brands do not fit” keeps both scope words. It does not become “fits all A6 notebooks”. Missing delivery timing becomes `[DETAIL NEEDED: delivery timing]`.

## How it differs from Copywriting Editor Kit

Start here for search-ad assets with embedded format rules, disclosure placement and product-category reader questions, as well as pages and email around the reader’s next step. Choose Copywriting Editor Kit to edit an existing draft, score a website against a checklist, check what evidence supports each claim, or record a writing style. Both plugins can draft and review website copy.

## Inputs and setup

Paste text or attach a readable text export. Say what the reader needs to do. Include the format, next step, facts, limits and any required wording. You do not need to fill out a questionnaire. For a page, copy its text or export it from your editor; for an email, paste the subject and body without recipients. For Google responsive search ads, the embedded reference supplies format rules. For other placements, provide their requirements.

If an attachment is unreadable, paste the relevant text. URLs alone are not read. There is no API or account setup. Product claims use only the material you supply; format guidance comes from the embedded references. The assistant can help turn a pasted export into a brief. Never provide credentials.

## Limits

It does not send or publish copy, operate accounts, scrape audience data, make up reviews or invent urgency. Drafts do not give legal or platform approval. A copy review cannot tell you how many readers will buy or respond. The [format-and-reader reference](skills/copy-from-reader-brief/references/format-and-reader.md) records sources checked on 2026-10-11. It covers Google responsive search ads, US FTC digital-ad guidance and product-description usability observations. For other rules or later platform changes, provide current official text and jurisdiction. Missing product facts stay marked rather than guessed. The assistant can suggest placement, but cannot verify an ad preview, device visibility or acceptance from text alone.

## Data and network

Network scope: none. It does not fetch URLs or robots.txt files, search the web, access accounts or contact external services. No usage tracking. The plugin contains instructions and reference text. It has no executable code and stores nothing itself. Your assistant provider processes pasted text and attachments under its own terms and retention policy. Redact personal data before sharing.
