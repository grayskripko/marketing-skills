# IRS source register

All sources loaded and were read 2026-10-11. Scope: US federal charitable gift documentation. This records the current pages, not changes after today. Submission target: 2026-10-18.

- [Written acknowledgments](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/charitable-contributions-written-acknowledgments): $250; organization name, cash amount or noncash description without valuation, appropriate benefit statement and value. Donor name and EIN are not in its required-field list. Live IRS page; read 2026-10-11.
- [Substantiating contributions](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/substantiating-charitable-contributions): monetary gifts need a bank record or charity communication with organization, date and amount. Its shortened deadline summary is read with Publication 1771. Live IRS page; read 2026-10-11.
- [Publication 1771](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p1771.pdf), pages 2–8: separate gifts under $250 are not aggregated; annual letters may cover multiple gifts; deadline is earlier of filing or due date including extensions. January 31 is customary. Benefits have exceptions; donated labor is not valued as a deductible gift. Page 2 excludes vehicles/boats/airplanes above $500 and conservation easements from ordinary substantiation coverage. Live PDF linked from IRS pages, revision November 2023; read 2026-10-11. Its token-benefit dollar examples explicitly use 2023 figures. Use Rev. Proc. 2025-32 for 2026 instead of those older examples.
- [Quid pro quo contributions](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/charitable-contributions-quid-pro-quo-contributions): whole payment over $75 triggers disclosure at solicitation or receipt; prior adequate solicitation disclosure suffices. State good-faith benefit value and limit to excess over FMV. Pure sales and supported benefit exceptions differ. Live IRS page; read 2026-10-11.
- [Tax Exempt Organization Search](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/tax-exempt-organization-search): Pub. 78 data checks deductible-gift eligibility; determination and revocation records are also available. Live IRS page; read 2026-10-11. No individual organization lookup performed.

Author workflow choices, not IRS obligations: drafts first, approved values, refund/duplicate conflict checks, and a zero floor for potential charitable portions. These choices are this plugin's rule of thumb.
