# Givebutter source register

All sources loaded and were read 2026-10-11. Scope: publicly documented receipt behavior. Relative update labels are recorded as displayed, not converted into calendar dates.

- [Automatic receipts and thank-you messages](https://help.givebutter.com/en/articles/2219216-how-to-manage-automatic-receipts-and-thank-you-messages): campaign override, static messages, mixed transaction types, campaign-only tests, deleted-message fallback, verified 501(c)(3)/fiscal-sponsor feature gate, import/offline exceptions, changed text on resend. Updated “over a week ago”; read 2026-10-11. The FAQ qualifies the introduction's broad automatic-receipt statement.
- [1099-K and tax questions](https://help.givebutter.com/en/articles/6604894-1099-k-forms-and-other-tax-related-questions): final beneficiary eligibility, donor-covered fees included, tips excluded. Updated “over 2 weeks ago”; read 2026-10-11. Its 2025 1099-K figures are outside this job and are not receipt thresholds. Broad exemption wording is interpreted with IRS eligibility evidence.
- [Resend a transaction receipt](https://help.givebutter.com/en/articles/5488994-how-to-resend-a-transaction-receipt): admin chooses transaction row → menu → Resend Receipt; original or chosen address. Two resends/minute; faster requests may produce “Failed to resend receipt.” Updated “over 2 weeks ago”; read 2026-10-11. This is a documented rate-dependent cause, not a diagnosis of every failure.

For acknowledgment fields, thresholds, timing, eligibility and monetary recordkeeping, read the [IRS source register](../../review-donation-acknowledgment/references/sources.md). Each linked IRS source loaded and was read 2026-10-11. Publication 1771 is revision November 2023 and explicitly dates token-dollar examples to 2023; use the dated 2026 limits below; verify other gift years separately.

Author workflow choices, not platform requirements: itemized fee/tip arithmetic, avoiding duplicate fee inclusion, conditional potential portions and donor-specific facts excluded from shared messages. These choices are this plugin's rule of thumb.
