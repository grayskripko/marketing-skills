# Metric sources and planning choices

Opened and read 2026-10-10.

- [YouTube impressions and CTR FAQ](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7628154?hl=en): CTR uses views originating from counted impressions; total views are not the same numerator. Audience and placement affect the result. Sequential title or thumbnail changes do not isolate their effects. Support: sections on calculation mismatch and avoiding misleading comparisons. No universal cutoff is adopted.
- [YouTube thumbnail impressions and watch time](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314486?hl=en): the metric table distinguishes views, views from thumbnail impressions and watch time. External embeds and several other placements are outside counted impressions. Support: metric table and counted/not-counted placements. Current definitions must remain attached to the supplied export.
- [Get started with YouTube Analytics](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9002587?hl=en): Advanced Mode supports comparison and export. Some source/demographic data can be limited. Support: opening explanation and notes. Screens and reports can vary; these skills promise no live retrieval.

The following are this plugin's rules of thumb, not platform requirements: align format and upload-age windows before ranking; keep source conflicts visible; use one intended change in the experiment brief; set the observation window before inspecting results; decide directionally when no target was supplied. These choices improve interpretability but do not certify causal inference or statistical significance.

Rate weighting and duration conversion are arithmetic identities only when their rows, units and denominators match. Rounded supplied CTR yields an approximate weighted rate. Estimated unique viewers cannot be added without overlap information. The worked examples are fictional and demonstrate the instructions; they are not evidence of effectiveness.
