## DOB NOW Build FAQ — deciding source

URL: https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/industry/dob-now-build-faqs.page
Loads with the full FAQ text. No displayed page revision date. Relevant questions/sections:

- Other Frequently Asked Questions, “Which job types can be combine filed?” and Plumbing/Sprinkler/Standpipe, “How do I add Plumbing to a General Construction (GC) filing?”: PL/SP/SD group is separate from GC/FO/EA/MS/ST; the latter group requires GC. Either PL or GC can be I1 with the other subsequent. Different permit license types explain GC/PL separation.
- Subsequent Filings, “When can I submit a subsequent job filing?”: I1 must be submitted rather than Pre-filing; approval is not needed; I1 LOC closes the ordinary subsequent window. “Can a subsequent filing have a different design applicant?”: yes; an eligible combined application is an option with the same applicant. Question about locked subsequent fields: inherited fields update after I1 submission.
- New Building and Alteration-CO, “Do I need to request a Letter of Completion for the subsequent filing?”: no; subsequent status stays Permit Entire and I1 moves to CO Issued. Keep this separate from ordinary Alteration closure.
- Permits, “What do the terms combined filing and composite permit mean?” and “Do I have to pull a composite permit?”: approval precedes permit choice; single-type permits remain available; different contractors cannot share a composite permit.
- “How do I withdraw a work type from a combined filing or composite permit?”: saved combined filing cannot drop a work type; withdraw/refile the filing. “If I pull a composite permit, can I later withdraw one of the work types?”: a permitted composite permit needs whole-permit withdrawal and individual replacement permits. The source distinguishes filing withdrawal from permit withdrawal: retain that distinction.
- Supersede and Withdrawal, “How do I withdraw the entire job filing?”: Withdrawal Letter through DOB help form, possibly inspection for permitted filing. Applicant/contractor dashboard withdrawal is a different operation. “Can the initial job filing be withdrawn if there are subsequent job filings?”: yes, with separate requests for subsequent withdrawals.

The pasted phrase “Selected work types cannot be combined” is matched by its work-type context, not treated as a published numeric error code. Do not assign meanings to undocumented codes.
