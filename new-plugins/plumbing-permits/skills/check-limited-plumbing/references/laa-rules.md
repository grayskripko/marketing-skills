# NYC limited plumbing source register

All sources read 2026-10-11. Jurisdiction: NYC; LAA plumbing scope. Required pages and additional official guides load. Undated pages/PDFs have no verified revision date. Reopen deciding rules before the user's filing submission. Never turn a retrieval date into an effective date.

## Limits and category scope

https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/industry/limited-plumbing-alterations.page

Category 1, current published table: $35,000 per building including appliance and labor costs in any 12 months. Five added fixtures/connections and five new domestic-system sprinkler heads are rolling-period limits. New piping excludes Category 2 work. Rearranged heads: 20 with specified occupancy restrictions; domestic supply is additionally required by the replacement guide and skilled-trades page. Standpipe piping/parts limits exclude connected sprinklers/combined systems as listed. Category 2: no cost/time limit, but only listed operations. Same-count existing branch rerouting, primary backflow, existing-roughing relocation and qualifying in-kind replacements are distinct from new installations. Existing domestic-system head replacement: 30 with unchanged orifice/type/deflector. Gas burner: 2.8 million Btu/h; boiler: 1 million Btu/h. Direct-vent appliance/dryer rows have one-/two-family and four-story-or-less conditions. All costs must still be disclosed. Only one category and one work type per application. Page states applicability to LAAs submitted on/after 2014-12-31.

## Route and ordinary-work boundary

https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/property-or-business-owner/project-requirements-owner-residential.page

Sections “No permit, but work requires a Licensed Master Plumber” and “Master Plumber”: ordinary plumbing under AC 28-105.4.4 still requires that license; work beyond limited alteration needs design-professional permit documents. “Replacement” alone does not prove an exemption.

https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/industry/limited-alteration-application.page

LAA overview excludes construction work and primarily addresses existing repair/replacement, with specific new-installation exceptions. Plumbing applicants are Licensed Master Plumbers. Separate eligible trade routes exist for fire suppression/oil work; do not infer a plumber covers all trades.

## Existing LAA withdrawal

https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/pdf/laa_supersede_withdrawal_sn.pdf

Service notice published 2021-10-13, effective 2021-10-15; linked from current Build FAQ. LAA requests occur in Build, for Permitted filings. Withdrawal is by permit applicant for a new job filing, not PAA. No-work contractor withdrawal, matching-BIN/scope alternative LAA, and correct-address matching-scope LAA are the listed reasons. No withdrawal inspection; performed work requires a matching replacement LAA or supersede. Ordinary Build whole-job withdrawal rules are separate.

## Interpretation choices

Building totals, provisional results when prior work is missing, and one focused follow-up are editorial workflow choices for applying the published limits. Separate applications do not supply evidence of an independent building-wide allowance. Do not infer unseen prior work or exemption classifications.

## Rechecked boundaries — read 2026-10-11

### Reportable ordinary work after the 2026 amendment

https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/pdf/op128-inst.pdf

Revision 03/26, pages 1–2: LMP monthly report covers work underway or performed in the preceding month; deadline is the following month's 15th. License and insurance must be active. This reporting obligation concerns AC 28-105.4.4 ordinary work, not every permit-exempt minor repair. Item 5 uses OP128: HHW; the other listed items use OP128. Its generic backflow wording must be read with the notice's specific secondary-backflow scope, not extended to primary devices.

https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/pdf/ordinaryplumbing-in.pdf

Distributed 2026-04-02; amendments effective 2026-02-22 under Local Law 142 of 2025. Items 1–4/6–9 use OP128; item 5 uses HHW. Secondary backflow is the ordinary-work item; an internal secondary RPZ needs DOB OP129. Qualifying HHW replacements require a chimney affidavit. Primary backflow remains the separately listed Category 2/DEP route. A sprinkler take-off blocks ordinary-work treatment of domestic-system alterations.

Source conflict: page 1 brackets the old two-bathroom/one-kitchen restriction, while page 2 repeats it and a 20-operation monthly limit. Page 2 also broadly excludes gas, despite explicit gas appliance/connector exceptions on page 1. Do not resolve these by accepting broader eligibility. For a case that depends on the disputed monthly limits, obtain the applicable enacted provision or DOB clarification before giving a final ordinary-work conclusion. Gas appliance exceptions do not extend to gas piping. The undated replacement guide lists secondary backflow as Category 2; it does not override the dated 2026 ordinary-work notice for an operation qualifying under the amended provision.

### Sprinkler occupancy disagreement

Dedicated category table, Category 1 item 4: light hazard, restaurant service, mercantile areas. Skilled-trades Category 1 item 4: light or ordinary hazard, with residences, restaurant service and mercantile as examples; explicitly domestic supply. Replacement water guide page 6 also requires domestic supply for rearranging up to 20 heads. These sources agree on the domestic-system boundary, but not the full occupancy wording. Do not call the broader occupancy list unqualified corroboration. If eligibility depends on an ordinary-hazard occupancy outside the narrow table, report the conflict and resolve its applicable provision before a final eligibility conclusion.

## Lost-task source recheck — 2026-10-12

### Cost-cap conflict

https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCadmin/0-0-0-155475

The October 2026 published Administrative Code §28-101.5 definition of LIMITED PLUMBING ALTERATIONS states a $50,000 Category 1 building-wide 12-month cap. The DOB category and skilled-trades pages above still publish $35,000; retain those as attributed guidance, not an unqualified statement of the controlling statutory cap. Establish the enacted provision and effective date applicable to the filing, giving applicable enacted law priority over conflicting summary guidance. If the date cannot be established, state the cost conflict and calculate both outcomes only where it affects the requested answer. A six-fixture building total fails the five-fixture limit under either cap; give that decisive result first. Neither dollar amount is an editorial default. The future EBC transition alone does not resolve this conflict.
