# Privacy

Preflight supplied podcast transcripts and settings for Apple and Spotify ingestion; return route, eligibility, format, timestamp and overwrite findings with the corrections you request.

Inputs are the transcript text, RSS fragments, warning text, settings and local exports you choose to supply. The skills use bundled references. Runtime network fetches: none. They do not log into Apple, Spotify or a host, request credentials, upload transcripts or change settings.

The package has no server, telemetry, analytics or separate storage. Your hosting assistant processes the supplied content under its data settings. Any corrected file is returned in the conversation or saved locally when you request it. Keep private feed tokens and unrelated personal data out of supplied settings; use a redacted export. Supporting excerpts in findings include only what is needed for the correction.

Source verification during development reads public documentation; episode content is not sent to those sites. Support: https://github.com/grayskripko/marketing-skills/issues. Share a redacted example when reporting a problem.
