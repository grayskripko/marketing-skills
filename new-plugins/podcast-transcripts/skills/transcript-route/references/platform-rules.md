## Apple — Transcripts on Apple Podcasts

URL: https://podcasters.apple.com/support/5316-transcripts-on-apple-podcasts
Read 2026-10-11. Loaded substantive support content.

Public supplied transcripts: host RSS transcript tag; “Display transcripts I provide”; replacement follows refresh. Subscriber episodes: Connect VTT/SRT upload. Private RSS outside catalog: no displayed transcript. Accepted languages: English, Danish, Dutch, Finnish, French, German, Italian, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish, Swedish. Unsupported language: episode-note link. Episode override and correct language/explicit metadata affect display; listener restrictions hide transcripts for explicit episodes. Dynamic audio changes omit changed segments. Accurate full text is required. VTT supports speaker names. Missing new transcripts: troubleshoot after 24 hours; over 10 hours may not be transcribed, not a universal upload ban.

## Spotify — Managing episode transcripts

URL: https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/managing-episode-transcripts-on-spotify/
Read 2026-10-11. Loaded substantive support content.

Some shows hosted with Spotify or elsewhere are eligible. Web management, no creator-mobile management. Download VTT, edit locally, upload and Save; no direct editing. Upload VTT/SRT, max 5 MB, timestamps for sync. Host support can affect syncing. Automatic generation replaces an uploaded transcript. Separate show/episode display controls and RSS distribution toggle. Other platforms may not support distributed transcripts. The page defines neither a byte convention for MB nor an exhaustive eligibility test or error-code catalog.
