# Privacy: Remittance Denials

This package contains instructions, references and an icon. It runs no service.

Inputs may include claim/service identifiers, dates, amounts, payer decisions and health information. Use anonymous row labels and remove patient names, birth dates, addresses and member numbers. The skills request only facts needed to interpret an adjustment or identify its deciding evidence. Local files are read only when you supply them for the task; there is no directory-wide scan.

Your assistant provider processes the conversation and supplied files. The plugin has no database, independent retention, analytics or telemetry. Conversation and file retention follow your assistant settings; use its controls to remove them.

Network scope: public X12 CARC and RARC pages, ecommerce.x12.org/code-updates, relevant cms.gov remittance/redetermination pages and the timely-filing manual, and public payer-policy URLs you supply. Official-domain searches contain only code IDs or policy names. Direct public-page fetches also request robots.txt and may follow normal redirects. No claim text, patient identifiers or private files enter search queries or request URLs. No accounts, credentials, paid APIs or subscription downloads are accessed. No claims, appeals, bills or messages are transmitted.
