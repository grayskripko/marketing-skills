## Trends field meanings and limits

URL: https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-trends — read 2026-10-11.
Scope: Pinterest Trends; some features are in regional testing.

Search normalization uses the term’s share of platform searches for the same time frame, then maps that plot’s minimum to 0 and maximum to 100. Zero means the relative minimum, not zero search activity. The selected region normally applies. Audience trends instead cover worldwide engaged users/followers, the last 90 days, refreshed daily; an absent audience view means insufficient audience size, with no numeric threshold given. History can span two years, and comparison accepts four terms. Spotlight measures saves; search covers keywords. Shopping covers product categories with Top vertical, Age, Gender and Ranked by filters; preserve the displayed metric. Category predictions and top products appear where available. Moments show interest building, peaking and cooling with phase-specific detail pages; a phase label alone is not a dated curve. Predictions exist only where indicated. Keep metric labels and history/prediction boundaries. These rules decide whether a supplied panel supports regional keyword timing, category timing or audience context.

## Native scheduling

URL: https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/schedule-pins — read 2026-10-11.
Scope: business-account standard Pins.

Scheduling is up to 30 days ahead, one Pin at a time, with ten waiting Pins. Scheduled date, title, board, description and link can change; media cannot. Newly scheduled Pins can take a few minutes to appear in the profile’s scheduled section. Use these limits only for a requested native handoff, not to shorten the planning calendar. No undocumented error codes or account-specific limits are claimed.
