# Privacy

Mill Certificates is a skills-only package: Markdown instructions, one reference file, JSON platform metadata, a license and a static SVG icon. It bundles no executable scripts, dependencies, servers, connectors or hooks, and installing it runs no program.

It works only on the certificates, purchase order lines, receiving rows and supplier details you paste or explicitly supply. It makes no network requests: no web search, URL fetching, uploads, account connections or telemetry. Supplier messages it writes are drafts for you to send yourself.

The publisher receives no data and runs no collection or storage service for this plugin. Your conversation and attachments are processed and retained under your assistant provider's own settings. Supplier names, contact details and project names are not needed for the checks; you can replace them with neutral labels.
