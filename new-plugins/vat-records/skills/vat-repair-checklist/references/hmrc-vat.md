## Digital records and link boundary

URL: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/vat-notice-70022-making-tax-digital-for-vat/vat-notice-70022-making-tax-digital-for-vat
Publisher updated: 2022-04-01. Loaded successfully; read 2026-10-11.
Sections 3.2.1–3.2.3, 3.3, 3.4 and examples in section 5: identify the electronic-account boundary per stream; later transfers need digital links. Linked formulas and file imports qualify; retyping and copy/paste do not. An emailed spreadsheet qualifies when subsequently imported. Manual initial capture can be permitted. Genuine adjustment workings may be outside compatible software, with adjustment totals recorded separately. The filing endpoint uses HMRC's API. Sections 3.3.1–3.3.4 cover identity, transaction fields and summary data. Section 3.3.3.2 sets petty-cash boundaries: each gross purchase below £50; gross entry no more than £500. Sections 3.5–3.6 distinguish retail daily gross takings and Flat Rate Scheme purchase exceptions. No current soft-landing permission is asserted.

## Record detail, aggregation and retention

URL: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/record-keeping-for-vat-notice-70021
Publisher last updated: 2024-03-18. Loaded successfully; read 2026-10-11.
This newer notice includes digital record guidance previously in 700/22; use its corresponding current sections for cross-checks. Section 2.4: VAT evidence generally kept at least six years. Sections 7.1, 7.2 and 7.4: business identity/schemes; sales tax point/net/rate; purchases tax point/value/input tax claimed, without compulsory input rate splits. Sections 7.5–7.6: supplier-statement conditions and petty-cash boundaries. Sections 7.10–7.13: separate adjustment totals, daily gross takings, Flat Rate Scheme exceptions. Sections 9–14: software, digital-link methods, external calculations, spreadsheets and accountant handoffs. Section 14 examples distinguish digital invoice data upstream from permitted manual paper-invoice entry. Keep invoice evidence as well as digital fields. Same-period/same-rate sales on an invoice may be combined; invoice purchase totals may be recorded. Aggregation does not establish input-tax entitlement.

## Software and authorisation

URL: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/find-software-thats-compatible-with-making-tax-digital-for-vat
Publisher last updated: 2025-05-01. Loaded successfully; read 2026-10-11.
Sections Search for software, After you've got the right software, How to use the feature, Software issues: compatible record/submission software or a bridge for spreadsheets; the official finder is HMRC's online software search. Existing businesses are normally automatically signed up; do not prescribe obsolete general sign-up steps. Software authority lasts 18 months; users can check/withdraw permissions. Human tax agents/accountants use an HMRC agent services account; the AI assistant does not sign in or operate it. Product-entry and software problems go to the provider; HMRC explains legal requirements. The package gives setup advice, not an account integration.

## Exemption boundary

URL: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/apply-for-an-exemption-from-making-tax-digital-for-vat
Publisher last updated: 2024-10-29. Loaded successfully; read 2026-10-11.
Sections Check if you're already exempt, Check if you can apply, Applying: insolvency and a final return after VAT registration cancellation are automatic exemptions. Other digital-exclusion grounds require application and HMRC consideration; age alone is not a blanket exemption. Continue the usual returns pending HMRC's letter. Avoid importing Income Tax exemption rules into VAT. Older notice text must be reconciled with this newer operational guidance.

## Use sources in the requested answer

Use only the section that decides the user’s question: Notice 700/21 §10 for digital links (Notice 700/22 §3.2.1 states the same rule); Notice 700/21 §§2.4, 6 and 8 for invoice-image retention; Notice 700/21 §§7.2, 7.4–7.6 for aggregation, §§7.4 and 7.10 for provisional purchase VAT and adjustments, §§7.12–7.13 for scheme exceptions, and §§7.2 and 7.9 for category and summary coverage. Cite the relevant section alongside the finding rather than adding a source report. A recorded purchase date alone does not establish the required tax point; preserve that distinction without declaring an unsupported missing-field defect. For retention, consult §2.4 for the applicable retention start and duration and the digital-record guidance before advising replacement of paper originals with images. Spreadsheet fields alone do not replace retained invoice evidence.

## Review conventions

Locating source/destination cells, assigning repair owners, preserving an original copy, comparing row counts and period totals, and testing a changed source value in a separate trial copy are this package's review conventions, not additional HMRC statutory requirements. They help demonstrate whether a proposed repair actually transfers the user's data. Never treat them as permission to alter a filed return.

## Additional record-coverage guidance

[Notice 700/21](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/record-keeping-for-vat-notice-70021), §§7.2, 7.4, 7.9 and 5.2, supplies the inline period-category, provisional recovery, same-period purchase grouping and summary checks. Section 7.10 permits separately recorded adjustment-type totals without rewriting each original supply. Check required totals wherever held in the linked account, not only in the bridge. These source-backed decisions prevent unnecessary missing-field repairs and false coverage passes.
