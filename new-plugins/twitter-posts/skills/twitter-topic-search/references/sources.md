# Topic selection from supplied posts

Read [supplied post intake and relationships](../../twitter-account-research/references/sources.md) before filtering structured data. It contains dated official archive guidance, documented API-shaped field mappings and worked relationship examples. Those mappings are not a universal account-archive schema.

Search the supplied full own-text body when present, keeping quote context and repost targets separate. A quote-only keyword does not establish an own-text match. Keep replies that also quote identifiable by both relationships; exclude either relationship for “only originals.” Do not classify a reduced export's missing relationship fields as originals. Missing full text can change whether a match is knowable. Author counts require actual supplied author identities, not display-name guesses. No live topic search or retrieval is supplied.
