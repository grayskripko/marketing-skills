# IntakeQ Forms

Fix IntakeQ insurance questions and Billing Type mappings, and turn anonymous insurance profile examples into an exception checklist.
For health and wellness administrators. Paste a questionnaire or export, mapping table, payer settings and anonymous profile examples. The review needs no IntakeQ account connection, API keys or paid API.

| Skill | Use when | Output |
|---|---|---|
| correct-insurance-mappings | Questions or rules fail to populate profile fields | Corrected questions and mappings, with staff exceptions |
| check-insurance-exceptions | Profile values, badges or billing behavior need explanation | Profile and payer exception checklist with staff actions |

Try “Check these IntakeQ mappings” or “Explain these insurance badges and why coinsurance isn't applying,” followed by your text. Use anonymous labels; remove names, dates of birth, member IDs and account identifiers. Settings and mappings remain proposals for your staff to review in IntakeQ or PracticeQ.

The embedded official guidance covers mapped insurance questions, Billing Type rules, manual benefit fields, Allowed Amounts, secondary payer handling and the October 2026 insurance badges. Dated URLs are in [references/official-rules.md](references/official-rules.md).

Network: none during skill use. The skills read supplied conversation text and files. They contain no connector, executable service or telemetry.
