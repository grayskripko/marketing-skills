## Conflict and operational choices

279 and 2869 disagree on the support/evidence anchor and period. Retain both; a source loading today does not resolve that disagreement. Earlier-date action target, no invented hourly cutoff, aliases, evidence indexing, conditional amounts and repair-versus-replacement alternatives are preparation choices, not additional Airbnb requirements. If the damage date is unknown, earlier-date calculation is incomplete: act now and ask support to clarify. Japan response workflow comes from article 3344, not the expired summary. Japan filing/evidence deadline and applicable full policy remain to be confirmed with the specialist.

The Resolution Center account page https://www.airbnb.com/resolutions redirects to login. It is a user filing destination, not an accessible connector or an allowed policy-fetch URL.

## Amount and eligibility details — read 2026-10-11

[HDP terms](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2869), §§6.2–6.4 and 7: income requires pre-loss confirmed Airbnb bookings canceled because eligible damage made the home uninhabitable. Exclude non-continuing costs, mortgage and property taxes; loss period ends when habitability can be restored. Cleaning reimbursement is only the excess over cleaning fees paid/charged. Stained linens require unauthorized invitees or a pet breaching house rules; smoke requires smoking against house rules. Unexplained inventory loss is excluded. Ordinary-property valuation uses the lowest applicable actual cash value, repair or like-kind replacement/rebuild amount; actual cash value includes depreciation. Deduct other same-loss recoveries. USD 3 million is a per-stay cap.
