## Authorization settings and claims

URL: https://support.simplepractice.com/hc/en-us/articles/7007890860045-Using-Authorization-Tracking
Title: Using Authorization Tracking. Page updated August 26, 2026 23:36.
Scope: SimplePractice client authorization tracking and generated claims, not a payer's approval policy.

Deciding details: one active entry; its number populates box 23 on primary and secondary claims. Secondary claims need the correct payer-specific number entered manually. Remove it when a payer does not require one. A tracking-only placeholder is permitted. An optional expiry reminder is one month ahead. Remaining appointments can be recorded separately in chart notes, progress/psychotherapy notes or Client Notes. Chart notes can be pinned with a far-future date; that display date does not establish when a supplied balance was measured. This page does not document automatic decrement behavior or payer-specific consumption units.

## Claim form constraints

URL: https://support.simplepractice.com/hc/en-us/articles/207600026-Creating-and-printing-CMS-1500-claim-forms
Title: Creating and printing CMS 1500 claim forms. Page updated December 11, 2025 18:42.
Scope: SimplePractice claim editor and printed/downloaded forms.

Deciding details: maximum six appointments/service codes per claim. Review generated fields. Secondary PDFs omit SimplePractice-specific electronic COB fields, including those in boxes 8 and 24. Box 31 is initially blank; electronic filing needs no signature, while mail/fax needs manual signing. Missing PDF fields do not establish missing electronic entries.

## Notification interpretation

URL: https://support.simplepractice.com/hc/en-us/articles/34040488528269-Using-practice-notifications
Title: Using practice notifications. Page updated December 12, 2025 03:40.
Scope: practice notification state, not approval validity.

Deciding details: deleting or marking a notification read/unread changes its state for all team members. Read items remain in Notifications and can be hidden by the Unread filter. Account Owners and practice managers can toggle “Clients with expiring insurance authorizations” in Notification preferences at practice-wide level. This is separate from the per-authorization reminder option. Notification visibility does not establish approval validity.
