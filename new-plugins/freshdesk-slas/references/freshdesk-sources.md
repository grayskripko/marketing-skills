## Calendars

[Business hours and calendar hours](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/37627-what-are-business-hours-and-calendar-hours-): modified 2025-12-17, loaded. Signup cohorts are separated before/after December 2025. Newer calendars map to groups; unassigned tickets use the default. Additional calendars require Pro+. Up to five breaks per day; holidays recur annually; regional selection removes exclusive holidays. Freshdesk uses one schedule across channels, Omni supports channel-specific schedules. Calendar hours include nights, weekends and holidays.

## Policies and events

[Understanding SLA Policies](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/37626-understanding-sla-policies): modified 2026-08-20, loaded. Newer cohort: first matching policy applies to new tickets; additional policies require Pro+. AND means all conditions, OR any. Freshdesk minimum is five minutes; Omni thirty seconds in fifteen-second increments. Maximums: 1,000 policies and 500 business-hours schedules. Enterprise permits custom condition fields. Agent replies/public notes count; automation Send Reply does not satisfy first response. Agents can edit resolution due times. These are newer-cohort rules, not account rollout proof.

[Every-response targets](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000001634-setting-sla-targets-for-every-response): modified 2021-06-18, loaded. Priority-specific targets use business/calendar clocks; next-response due times are distinct ticket-view/report fields. Current plan table shows Pro/Enterprise. No universal reopen/reset algorithm is established.

[Timer start and pause](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/229718-when-a-ticket-is-created-does-the-timer-start-right-away-how-can-i-stop-the-timer-when-i-respond-to): modified 2023-03-31, loaded. Newly created Open tickets start timing. Status-field configuration can disable timers for Pending/waiting states. A label alone establishes no pause.

[Private notes and SLA responses](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/225358-is-a-private-note-classified-as-a-response-under-sla-policies-): modified 2019-02-21, loaded.
