The comparison procedure is this plugin's rule of thumb. It is not a law, a vendor requirement or a certification method. Runtime claims must come from the user's approved sources, not from these notes. Core rules are in SKILL.md.

Build basis: the project's PLUGINS2.md row for support-content-audit and domain-customer-support-A.md / domain-customer-support-B.md, research dated 2026-10-09.
