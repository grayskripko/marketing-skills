# Privacy

SVHC Declarations is a skills-only package: Markdown instructions, one reference file, JSON platform metadata, a license and a static SVG icon. It bundles no executable scripts, dependencies, servers, connectors or hooks, and installing it runs no program.

It works only on the bill-of-materials rows, part data, supplier declarations and requests you paste into the conversation. It makes no network requests: no web search, URL fetching, uploads, account connections or telemetry. Customer notices and supplier emails it writes are drafts for you to send yourself.

The publisher receives no data and runs no collection or storage service for this plugin. Your conversation is processed and retained under your assistant provider's own settings. Supplier names, contact details and customer names are not needed for the checks; you can replace them with neutral labels.
