# Sources for SVHC declaration rules

Each source below carries its own read date. That is when the package was built: it is not the date the answering agent looked anything up, not a supplier's assessment date and not a user record. The package makes no network requests at run time; these notes were written into it when it was built. The user's own Candidate List snapshot, supplier declarations and part data decide each case.

## 1. REACH Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006, consolidated version of 22 June 2026

https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02006R1907-20260622 — read 2026-10-13.
- Article 3(3): an article is an object which during production is given a special shape, surface or design which determines its function to a greater degree than does its chemical composition.
- Article 7(2): producers and importers of articles notify the Agency of a Candidate List substance when it is present in those articles in quantities totalling over one tonne per producer or importer per year and above 0.1% w/w. Article 7(3): not where exposure to humans or the environment can be excluded during normal or reasonably foreseeable use including disposal; then appropriate instructions go to the recipient.
- Article 33(1): any supplier of an article containing a Candidate List substance above 0.1% w/w provides the recipient with sufficient information, available to the supplier, to allow safe use of the article, including as a minimum the name of the substance.
- Article 33(2): on request by a consumer, the same information, free of charge, within 45 days of receipt of the request.

## 2. Court of Justice, Case C-106/14, judgment of 10 September 2015

https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:62014CJ0106 — read 2026-10-13 (operative part).
- For a product made up of more than one article, the importer determines for each article whether a Candidate List substance is present above 0.1% w/w of that article (Article 7(2)).
- Under Article 33, the supplier of a product one or more constituent articles of which contain such a substance above 0.1% w/w of that article informs the recipient and, on request, the consumer, giving as a minimum the substance name.

## 4. ECHA pages on Candidate List substances in articles

https://echa.europa.eu/regulations/reach/candidate-list-substances-in-articles/communication-in-the-supply-chain — read 2026-10-13. Directly after inclusion, suppliers of articles containing the substance above 0.1% w/w provide recipients (industrial or professional users and distributors, not consumers) with enough information for safe use, as a minimum the substance name. Consumers can request similar information; reply within 45 days, free of charge.

https://echa.europa.eu/regulations/reach/candidate-list-substances-in-articles/notification-of-substances-in-articles — read 2026-10-13. Notification when both above 0.1% w/w and over one tonne per year; no later than six months after inclusion; exemptions for excluded exposure and for a substance already registered for that use.

https://echa.europa.eu/scip — read 2026-10-13. Suppliers of articles containing Candidate List substances above 0.1% w/w on the EU market submit information to the SCIP database as from 5 January 2021 (Waste Framework Directive).

## 5. ECHA Guidance on requirements for substances in articles, version 4.0, June 2017

https://echa.europa.eu/documents/10162/2324906/articles_en.pdf/cc2e3f93-8391-4944-88e4-efed5fb5112c — read 2026-10-13 (ECHA-17-G-19-EN).
- Section 2.5: packaging is not part of the substance, mixture or article it packages; it is a separate article with the same requirements.
- Section 3.2.3.1, Table 5: (I) article made from a mixture: substance weight / total article weight; (II) substance in a mixture joining articles (adhesive, solder): over the total weight of the complex object, i.e. the joined articles plus the mixture, normally in dry form; (III.A, III.B) coated article: over the uncoated article plus the dry coating; (III.C) coated complex object: over the whole coated object; (IV) very complex objects: rules I–III apply to each article or simpler complex object.
- Section 3.2.1: communicate to recipients when the article is first supplied after inclusion, and to consumers on request within 45 calendar days, free of charge; as a minimum the substance name.

## Rules of thumb of this plugin (no external source)

- Part numbers are matched after removing only spaces and case differences; a one-character, suffix or revision difference is a near match, never a link.
- A declaration that says "latest list" covers at most the list in force on its signature date.
- A missing declaration is reported as missing, never as "no SVHC".
- A concentration stated on a homogeneous material is converted to the article only when the material mass is known.
