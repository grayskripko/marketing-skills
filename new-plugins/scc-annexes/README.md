# SCC Annexes

Check EU international-transfer SCC modules and Annexes I–III against supplied parties, transfers and implemented security measures; produce a completion workbook and contradiction ledger.
For privacy, procurement and compliance teams preparing EU transfer contracts. Supply pasted SCC text or exports, party roles, transfer records, subprocessor lists and evidence of implemented security measures. No account, key, installation or paid API is needed for the input workflow.

- **complete-scc-annexes:** turn supplied records into a completion workbook, with proposed wording, evidence, missing fields and linked contradictions.
- **check-scc-contradictions:** compare a draft or signed SCC package against operational records and produce a contradiction ledger with focused corrections.

The workbook is delivered as spreadsheet-ready tables: Transfers, Parties, Annex I.B, Authority, Measures, Subprocessors, Choices and Issues. Ask for CSV exports or a local workbook when the assistant supports file creation. The ledger separates conflicting statements, missing evidence and missing fields. Supplied approved clause text is identified and preserved; proposals are kept separate.

Paste redacted records or provide files through your assistant. Keep organisation names and transfer identifiers consistent across records. Actual data-subject records, passwords and encryption keys are unnecessary.

Network: official public pages and PDFs from commission.europa.eu, eur-lex.europa.eu and edpb.europa.eu may be fetched to refresh the bundled rules, including their official redirects. Public searches are restricted to those domains and use only instrument names or clause topics. Party names, contract text and security evidence are never search terms or URL parameters. No connector, account access, telemetry or external write is included. User records are processed in the conversation; files are created only when requested.
