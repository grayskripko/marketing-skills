# Official source register

Read 2026-10-11. Scope: EU/EEA international-transfer SCC completion under Decision 2021/914. These are paraphrased decision rules, not a bundled copy of approved SCC text. Preserve and identify approved text supplied by the user. Field/evidence/status conventions are package output conventions.

## Transfer instrument and field rules

[Decision (EU) 2021/914](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2021/914/oj/eng), adopted 2021-06-04; OJ publication 2021-06-07. Read 2026-10-11: full English decision and appendix loaded; EUR-Lex reports **In force**. Article 1 covers importer processing outside direct GDPR scope. Appendix module headings map the four role pairs. I.A requires party identity, contacts, activities, roles and signature/date; I.B requires subjects, data, sensitive-data safeguards, frequency, nature, purpose, retention, and processor subject matter/duration. Clause 13/I.C and prescribed II cover Modules 1–3; Module 4 security remains in 8.2. III applies to Modules 2–3 with Clause 9 Option 1; Option 2 instead needs an agreed list and advance written change notice. Clause 9 periods are blank agreed choices. Module 3 routes authorisation to the controller and informs the exporter. Clause 9 and III concern further subcontracting by the importer; they do not list that importer as its own subprocessor. Annex II’s final paragraph requires assistance measures for (sub-)processor transfers only, including assistance to the exporter for Module 3. Clause 17 has Option 1 for Modules 1–3 and an exporter-establishment-law Option 2 only for Modules 2–3, both conditioned on beneficiary rights. Read EU/EEA governing-law scope with Q&A 37. Clause 18 is separate: raw EU text specifies Member State courts for Modules 1–3 and a country forum for Module 4; verify applicable EEA adaptation for an EEA forum rather than extrapolating from Q&A 37. Clauses 14–15 conditionally cover Module 4 EU-collected data combinations. Clause 12 contains module-specific liability rights. Clause 5 gives SCCs priority over conflicting related terms. Clause 7 accession needs consent, completed appendix and I.A signature. The appendix allows combined information only when transfers and roles remain clear.

## Additional official guidance

[EDPB Recommendations 01/2020, version 2.0](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2021-06/edpb_recommendations_202001vo.2.0_supplementarymeasurestransferstools_en.pdf), adopted 2021-06-18; read 2026-10-11, 48-page final PDF loaded. Roadmap steps 1–6 separate mapping, mechanism, local-law/practice assessment, supplementary measures, procedural steps and reassessment. Annex 2, Use Cases 1 and 6: effectiveness depends on access requirements and key/control arrangements; an importer needing plaintext access is not protected from public-authority access merely by storage encryption. Keep country-assessment evidence and control effectiveness separate from annex completion.

[EDPB Guidelines 05/2021, version 2.0](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2023-02/edpb_guidelines_05-2021_interplay_between_the_application_of_art3-chapter_v_of_the_gdpr_v2_en_0.pdf), adopted 2023-02-14; read 2026-10-11, 24-page final PDF loaded. Section 2's three criteria require an exporter subject to GDPR for the processing, disclosure/availability to another controller or processor, and importer in a third country or international organisation. The importer may itself be subject to GDPR. A Chapter V transfer can therefore exist even where 2021/914 is not the matching instrument. Remote access by a separate third-country recipient belongs in the transfer map; location alone is insufficient to classify processing roles.

## Output conventions

Supported/missing/conflicting/not-applicable statuses, stable transfer IDs, evidence locations, implementation states and separate proposed wording are output conventions. They help prevent missing evidence being treated as a contradiction or an unsigned draft being treated as an executed contract. They are not statutory field names. Measures inspection areas are a prompt to examine supplied evidence, not a universal demand for every example control. No fabricated source refresh, signature, authority, authorisation or implemented measure is permitted.
