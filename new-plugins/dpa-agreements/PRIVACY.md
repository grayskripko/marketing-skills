# Privacy: DPA Agreements

Runtime fetches: none. This skills-only package contains instructions and references; it runs no service and ships no connectors or executable scripts.

It reads the agreement text and task-selected files provided to the assistant. These may contain business identities, signatory details, contact information, processing descriptions and security information. Their purpose is to reconcile contractual commitments and evidence and draft questions within the conversation. Individual customer or employee records and credentials are not needed.

The plugin has no telemetry, external recipients or independent retention. The assistant provider processes and retains the conversation and supplied files under its own settings. You can redact unnecessary identifiers before providing inputs and use the assistant's conversation/file deletion controls. Outputs stay in the conversation unless you separately choose to save or send them.

Embedded source URLs are citations, not runtime fetch instructions. Updated guidance can be supplied as pasted text. Build-time verification used only public EU and EDPB sources listed in the references file.
