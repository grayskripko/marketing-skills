## Rebuild verification — read 2026-10-11

Reopened all four official pages for this fix. Article 3061 defines equipment by recording/transmission capability and applies the disconnected ban to shared private-room spaces. Article 2914 desktop steps distinguish the Safety devices checkbox “Noise decibel monitor present” from the dedicated “Noise decibel monitors” path; camera details include on/off state, Continue and Save. Coverage is useful inventory evidence, not a separate mandatory field established by those steps.

Article 3589 requires legal necessity plus a non-hotel Hotel Business License, (no 24/7 desk OR inability to meet license requirements with an exterior camera), statutory-purpose description and entry-only coverage with no audio capability. Its hotel examples include ryokans, aparthotels, hostels, resorts and boutique hotels. Signage is encouraged. Its camera steps name Add details and device/location; operating-state detail is supported by article 2914. The 2024-03-11 announcement explicitly prohibits exterior-to-interior coverage.
