| [Authentic and trustworthy reviews](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2673) | What we don't allow; what doesn't qualify; enforcement; own-review removal. Specific requester roles, pending requests and two submissions per same review determine access independently of merits. Received-review disputes can be requested at any time. Own-review removal uses publication plus 30 days. Decisions usually take 48 hours. Sensitive categories are excluded. |
| [Content Policy](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/546) | Prohibited content; enforcement. Includes harassment/threats, sexual content, discrimination, impersonation, rights violations, spam and separate-business advertising; public private information includes identifiable listing location. Applies to AI-edited content too. |
| [Reviews for homes](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/13) | Who can leave a review; canceled reservations; publication. 14-day writing period and earlier-of-both-or-window publication. Cancellation before check-in day prevents reviewing; certain cancellations on/after that day allow it, with midnight measured in listing time zone. |
| [Responding to a review](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/32) | Public response and removal request. Responses publish immediately and are visible with the original review; a reply does not remove the review. Use only when suggesting a public response as the next option. |

## Request fields and evidence

Article 3582 lists: select review, select reason, explain grounds, attach documentation, submit. Find a review using words in its text; reviewer-name searches may fail. No text-length, attachment-size/type, upload-count or appeal-reset limit is stated in the inspected page. Use actual supplied form constraints, never invented limits. A rejected request counts toward the two-submission maximum; the article provides no alternate-account exception.

## bfix source verification — read 2026-10-11

Reopened all five official articles during bfix. In article 2673, “Reviews that are fake” permits submission on behalf of a participant; “Reviews involving bias, deception, extortion, incentivization, or pressure” separately covers deception/manipulation and conditions the compensation-threat ground on compensation being unwarranted. The corrected skills preserve those distinctions.

Article 546, “The following content is not allowed,” supports the complete inline categories. Advertising requires sole-purpose promotion of a separate unrelated business. Spam includes unwanted contact and disruptive repeated sharing. Illegal content and promotion of harmful/illegal activity, violent/graphic/demeaning/insulting material and rights violations are separate grounds. These rules apply to the supplied passage in context; ordinary criticism is not automatically a prohibited insult.

Article 3582, “Remove a review you’ve written,” supplies the profile route and the missing-button meaning. Its received-review route supplies eligibility, pending-request and same-review submission checks; no alternate-person exception or reset for a new ground is stated. Article 13 supplies the listing-time-zone cancellation threshold; it does not make all on/after-day cancellations eligible or make eligible reviews relevant. Article 32 confirms immediate public-response publication.
