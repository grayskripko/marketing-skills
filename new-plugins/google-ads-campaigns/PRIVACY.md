# Privacy — Paid Search Planning & Review

This package contains instructions, reference text, manifests and an icon. It runs no service, installs no connector and adds no telemetry.

- Data read: the material you supply may contain campaign names, search terms, costs, conversion counts/values, settings and screenshots. Reports can also contain personal identifiers; redact those unless needed. Credentials and raw customer contact lists are not required.
- Purpose: analyze your reports, draft scoped changes and guide optional account access setup.
- Recipients: your assistant provider processes the supplied conversation/files under its terms. Review skills fetch nothing and send no data to another service.
- Network: setup fetches official documentation URLs listed in the access skill’s references or supplied by you, plus their robots.txt. It follows only official documentation links needed for your chosen setup, respects disallows, and includes no account data in documentation requests. Pasted documentation is the fallback. Separately authorized live setup exchanges authentication, account configuration and requested report data with the chosen Google UI/API/OAuth endpoints; Editor/Scripts/MCP runtimes also process data for their route. The official MCP documents a usage-data header. These instructions add no telemetry and send no data to extra services. Review separately connected tools' destinations and terms before enabling them.
- Retention: the package itself stores nothing. Your assistant's conversation/file retention applies. If you request local analysis or change files, those artifacts remain in the chosen workspace until removed. Separately installed clients, Editor or MCP may retain configuration, downloaded data and logs according to their settings.
- Controls: redact unnecessary names, customer IDs and personal data; keep secrets in protected local configuration rather than chat or version control. You can remove local artifacts, revoke Google OAuth/tool permissions and delete conversations using the relevant provider's controls.

Account data access does not authorize campaign changes. Approve the concrete account/entity changes before live application. Never paste passwords, client secrets, refresh tokens or credential files into the conversation.
