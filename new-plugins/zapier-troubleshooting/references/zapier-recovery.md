Core rules are inline in both skills. This register makes their provenance inspectable without copying articles. Use the relevant source section when a status, edit, field, entitlement, or charge is disputed. Weave the deciding rule into the requested answer; this register does not prescribe a report, source block, or checklist. A replay entitlement restriction does not rule out user-led manual completion of only the intended missing work; distinguish that option from Zapier replay and its task charges.

No source guarantees duplicate-free replay. Destination reconciliation, starting with one low-risk run, avoiding a competing scheduled retry, and stopping on an unexpected result are authored safety judgments, not Zapier policies or universal app idempotency claims. App-specific limits, transaction semantics, and prices require case evidence.
