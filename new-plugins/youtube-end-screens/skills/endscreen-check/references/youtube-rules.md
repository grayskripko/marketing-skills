## End-screen fields and limits

https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789?hl=en — Add end screens to videos. Loaded successfully.

The deciding fields are video duration, ratio, audience/360 status, type/mode, target, start/end and placement. Source sections: opening eligibility notes, “What your end screen can show,” timing/placement controls and viewer requirements.

Last 5–20 seconds; minimum video length 25 seconds; 16:9 maximum four elements, other ratios potentially fewer. No precise ratio-specific caps or pixel safe area are published here. Video choices: most recent, viewer-specific selection, specific target. Detailed target section permits public/unlisted videos and playlists from any channel, while the initial playlist menu says public: ask the editor to confirm unlisted-playlist acceptance in Studio. Subscription and channel types are separate from website Link; website Link requires Partner Program membership. Cards/watermarks are suppressed. Made-for-kids and 360 restrictions, YouTube Music/Kids exclusions, and mobile-web exception for iPad matter. Hiding and context-dependent variation prevent guaranteed appearance. Studio supports explicit timing, drag placement, grid/snap and play preview. No official save-error code dictionary appears here: never fabricate one.

## Audience classification

https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9527654?hl=en — Set your channel or video's audience. Loaded successfully.

Section “What happens when your content is set as made for kids”: cards and end screens unavailable. Audience must be set accurately regardless of location; YouTube can override errors/abuse. Do not reclassify a child-directed upload merely to activate links. Classifying content and appeals are outside these skills.

## External destinations

https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9054257?hl=en — External links policy. Loaded successfully.

Policy covers clickable, displayed, obfuscated and spoken off-site directions. Prohibited destinations include malware, phishing and other Community Guidelines violations. An unverifiable safe destination may have its link removed. Membership does not exempt links from policy. Prohibited destinations remain prohibited when spoken or disguised; Partner Program membership is required for the clickable Link element, not merely speaking a compliant URL.
