## Field rules and limits

- Search-log fields (source, owner, query/aliases, date range, searched-at, results, missing attachments and exclusions) and record-map statuses are authored workflow conventions, not prescribed GDPR form fields.
- Opinions, assessments, communications and inferred customer scores can be personal data. Do not exclude because subjective, unfavorable or internal (EDPB sections 4.1–4.2).
- General workload or an inconvenient request is not an exemption. The controller must justify any Article 12(5) decision. A national privilege restriction requires exact applicable law; a label alone does not establish it (EDPB sections 6.3–6.4).
- A supplied export is evidence of those rows, not proof of every repository searched. An empty search proves only its documented query and scope (authored evidence convention).
- Article 15 processing information includes purposes, categories, recipients, retention period or criteria, rectification/erasure/restriction/objection rights, complaint rights, available origin information for data not obtained from the person, and qualifying automated decisions including logic/significance/consequences. Add Article 15(2) safeguards if transfers apply.
- Establish evidenced official-channel receipt first. Where an applicable national provision determines legal receipt around weekends or holidays, use supplied current official text and record arrival separately (EDPB paragraph 159, footnote 90). Without an established exception, retain ordinary receipt; internal opening or forwarding is not a new receipt.
- EU month arithmetic uses the same date next month, or the month's last day if absent; if weekend/public holiday, move to the next working day using the relevant calendar. EDPB section 5.3 cites Regulation 1182/71. Without a verified local holiday calendar, state the calendar date and mark any final working-day adjustment unresolved.
- Any suspension needs timely actual request, legal conditions and elapsed dates. Recital 63 is not permission to force narrowing or leave a broad request unanswered. Default to the receipt date when conditions are not established (EDPB sections 2.3.1 and 5.3).
