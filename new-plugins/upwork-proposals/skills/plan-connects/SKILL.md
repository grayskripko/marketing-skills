# Plan Connects

Return a spending comparison or charge explanation first. Use current base cost C, extra boost bid B, balance A, invitation status and user-approved spending cap. Ordinary maximum spend = C; boosted maximum liability = C + B; balance after full charge = A - C - B. Show conditional spending separately from maximum exposure. If maximum spend exceeds credited balance or the approved cap, label that option unfundable and show the shortfall or cap excess; do not present a negative number as an attainable remaining balance. Finish an affordability comparison with the feasible option. Uncredited returns and rewards remain separate scenarios, not available funding. True invitation responses cost no base Connects. Do not assume boosting is available for an invitation; use the actual form. Purchasing-price equivalent = Connects × $0.15 USD; label it replacement value, not cash already spent or a cash refund. Bundles, billing and account context control actual purchases.

Boosting is optional, match eligibility determines availability, and the auction has four promoted slots. It ends at seven days or the first hire, whichever is earlier. In a normal auction, the full bid is payable if among the top four at close or if the client opens, shortlists, messages, archives, declines or offers while the proposal is boosted. Later outbidding does not erase that interaction. If outbid before any eligible interaction and outside the top four at close, the boost is not ultimately charged; base spend remains. Do not confuse boost-specific refunds with base refunds. Exception: randomly selected placebo jobs do not deliver boosts to the client and do not take the boost Connects. Apply that exception only with actual history or explicit Upwork confirmation; a missing debit alone does not prove placebo status. Base application spend is separate. No promised visibility or hiring outcome.

Timing differs across official pages: the dedicated charge FAQ says eligible interaction or auction close, whichever first; the broad boost guide describes end-of-auction charges and temporary charge/refund/recharge entries. Do not promise one posting time. Use exact pasted Connects History rows and timestamps; distinguish temporary entries from final liability. The boost guide says a changing base requirement during drafting/submission can draw additional base Connects from the total bid, so reconcile base and boost separately against actual history.

Base Connects return if the client cancels before a contract or Upwork removes a policy-violating post. Rejection, another freelancer's hire, expiry without hire and withdrawal do not return them. Returns are reusable Connects, not cash. Free monthly Connects and interview rewards are conditional; use only credited balance. A verified billing method is required to buy, even from earnings. Purchase payment is not split between earnings and a billing method. For an agency's “Failed to load Connects transaction details”, official guidance points to Agency Basic and an Agency Plus upgrade; check the user's actual plan before interpreting it, and do not purchase or upgrade.

The shown freelancer fee is contract-specific, generally 0–15%. Post-fee rate = gross × (1 - fee fraction); gross needed = desired post-fee rate / (1 - fee fraction). For a hard earnings floor, round the required gross rate upward to the next permitted form increment, then recompute earnings against the floor; for supplied hours or a fixed budget, check totals with cent-rounded fees or the current form calculator. Round ordinary reported money to cents, and exclude unknown taxes or currency charges. Do not use a universal 10%.

## Reconcile a history before assigning a refund

Use separate base, boost and reward rows with signs and timestamps. Compute final balance = starting balance + sum of all credits and debits. A temporary boost return followed by an equal re-debit leaves one final charge, not two. Identify the client's eligible interaction time relative to boosted status, auction close and outbidding. Then check whether a base-return reason exists independently. A refund-labelled interview award can include the original application cost plus a separate award; it is not evidence of a canceled job. These distinctions come from the dated Connects and boost sources in [the rule register](references/upwork-rules.md).

## Worked example

Fictional Ash Meridian: base 12, boost 18, balance 45. The client declined while boosted and another bidder later displaced the proposal.

Example output: “The 18-Connects boost remains payable because the client declined while it was boosted in this normal auction ([Upwork boost guide](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406395531795-How-to-boost-your-proposal)). Maximum total = 12 + 18 = 30 Connects; remaining balance after both final charges = 45 - 30 = 15. Replacement value = 30 × $0.15 = $4.50 USD. Being outbid afterward does not cancel that boost charge.”
