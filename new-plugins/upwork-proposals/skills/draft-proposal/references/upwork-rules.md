# Upwork rule register

Read 2026-10-11. Scope: Upwork Marketplace freelancer and agency proposals. All eight official pages loaded with their article bodies through live web retrieval. Dates are access dates, not publication dates. Refresh deciding rules before submission around 2026-10-18; job-specific Connects, fee, eligibility and fields come from the current proposal form. Summaries below are paraphrases.

## Connects

https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062898-Understanding-and-using-Connects

Read 2026-10-11. Sections: use, purchase, rewards, refunds. Base requirements vary and can change. Purchase price is $0.15 USD each; verified billing is needed even when paying from earnings, and payment cannot be split. Agency purchase error “Failed to load Connects transaction details” likely means Agency Basic needs Agency Plus. Monthly free Connects and interview awards are conditional. Base Connects return on client cancellation before contracting or policy removal, not rejection, competing hire, expiry or withdrawal. Returns are account credits, not cash.

## Boost operation

https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406395531795-How-to-boost-your-proposal

Read 2026-10-11. Sections: operation, charges, refunds, differing proposal-page totals. Boosting is optional and match-dependent. Four slots; closes after seven days or first hire. For normal auctions, full bid is owed on top-four finish or client opening, shortlisting, messaging, archiving, declining or offering while boosted. Outbidding without interaction and outside winning slots avoids the final boost charge. Base changes can take extra Connects from the total bid. The guide describes auction-end billing and temporary debit, refund and re-debit entries. Random placebo jobs deliver no boosts to clients and take no boost Connects; do not infer placebo status from a missing debit alone.

## Contact information

https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051749534-How-to-keep-your-contact-information-safe-on-Upwork

Read 2026-10-11. Sections: definition, exceptions, names, website samples. Pre-contract contact restrictions cover phone, email, addresses, messaging IDs and social handles. Exceptions include Enterprise participation, specific legal needs and email for system access needed to scope work. The article also requires pre-contract job/payment discussions on Upwork; do not extrapolate legal/access exceptions into off-platform interviews. Use user-supplied current Enterprise-specific terms for an external interview; absent those terms, leave that decision unresolved. Names and company names are allowed; sample website links are allowed with Upwork-only contact instructions.

## Invitations

https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211063018-How-to-respond-to-an-invitation-to-apply-on-Upwork

Read 2026-10-11. Sections: response and Connects. Accepting or declining a client's invitation costs zero Connects. Proposed terms can differ from the invitation. Declining does not reduce future invitation chances; contact details cannot be added to a decline. Some invitations use an Uma video interview instead of a cover letter. A recommendation is not evidence of a client invitation.

## Boost charge timing

https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/40444950584083-When-and-what-will-I-be-charged-Boosted-Proposals

Read 2026-10-11. Article body: full bid charged on an eligible interaction or boosted status at close; balance charged at interaction or close, whichever occurs first. Outbidding without interaction before close avoids the boost charge. This differs from the broad guide's auction-end timing description. Liability conditions agree. Preserve the timing conflict and use pasted history to explain actual posting; do not resolve the conflict by inventing a release date or rollout.

## Submission fields and edits

https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062998-How-to-submit-a-proposal-on-Upwork

Read 2026-10-11. Sections: submission and editing. Fields separate terms, duration, cover letter, screening answers, samples and optional boost. Fixed bids can be by project or milestone; hourly terms can include scheduled increases. Profile highlights are unavailable to agencies. Some jobs require an Uma interview instead of a letter. Use the dedicated edit article below for the deciding windows and offer lock; the general guide alone does not establish the complete edit path. No universal character or attachment-size cap was established by this source; obey the actual form. Repeated pre-contract contact with an unresponsive client is prohibited.

## Submitted proposal edit stages

https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211060388-How-to-edit-your-proposal

Read 2026-10-11. Sections: editable fields, edit path, offer lock. Letter, screening answers, attachments and available freelancer highlights can change before the earlier of six hours or viewing. Submitted freelancer proposals require at least one portfolio highlight, which can be replaced; agencies have no highlights. Later only permitted terms change before an offer while unaccepted/unrejected. Find Work → Proposals and offers → Active → open proposal → Edit proposal → Save. An offer locks proposal edits; request client-approved changes to that offer. An eligible previously unboosted proposal may be boosted, but an existing boost cannot be adjusted.

## Freelancer fee

https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062538-Learn-about-the-Freelancer-Service-Fee

Read 2026-10-11. Sections: fee range, visibility, calculator, fee changes. General fee range is 0–15% per contract, visible at proposal or offer. The article's fee-change section says locked after proposal, offer or contract is sent; another section says fixed once contract begins. Use the displayed percentage, reconfirm changed offers, and do not infer that an unsent form's fee is fixed. Enterprise terms may differ. Client pays the entered gross rate; calculator estimates post-fee earnings and rounding can matter.

## Authored decision methods

Apply/pass/clarify labels, requirement-to-portfolio matching, spending comparisons and missing-detail markers are this plugin's rules of thumb, not Upwork ranking criteria. Formulas are arithmetic, not predictions: liability = base + optional bid; post-fee rate = gross × (1 - fee fraction). Hard-floor rounding is an arithmetic safeguard: round the inverse-formula result upward to the permitted price increment and recompute post-fee earnings, including cent-rounded fees for supplied totals. No hiring probabilities or market-rate claims are embedded.
