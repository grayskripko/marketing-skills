# Privacy: Booking FAQ

This package contains instructions, reference text and an icon. It runs no service.

It reads supplied inquiries, policies, booking copy and authorized task files to prepare drafts. These may contain customer contact or health information; remove unnecessary identifiers and private circumstances first. Such details are excluded from public and reusable copy. No credentials or payment-card details are needed.

Nothing is fetched, including supplied booking links. There are no network destinations, redirects, downloads, accounts, paid APIs, calendars, reservations, payments, sends or publishing actions. A local tool may count words or rows without network access. No telemetry or plugin-operated storage exists.

The assistant provider processes supplied content under its own terms and conversation retention settings. User-requested output files are subject to the host's file storage. Redact before sharing and use the host's deletion controls for conversations and files.
