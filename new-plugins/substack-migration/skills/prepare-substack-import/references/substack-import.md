# Substack subscriber import sources

All pages below loaded and were read 2026-10-11. Update dates are the dates shown by the pages, not submission dates. Recheck before the planned 2026-10-14 submission if rules or native screens change.

## Dedicated mailing-list import

URL: https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037829931-How-do-I-import-my-mailing-list-from-another-platform-such-as-Mailchimp-Ghost-or-Beehiiv
Updated 2026-08-24. Applies to subscriber uploads.

Paraphrase: upload through Subscribers/Add subscribers. Accepted formats are CSV, XLSX, ODS. Confirm opt-in and choose subscription type when payments are enabled. Duplicate addresses are skipped; invalid domains can also cause skips. Refresh for added/skipped totals. Import can be publication-wide or section-targeted; the section selector enrolls imported readers only in the chosen section. Removal of an imported list needs human support. Temporary access ends with a request for payment details.

The page supplies no fixed row/byte maximum, exact required header, full field schema, or promise to update existing records. Do not invent these.

## Import under review

URL: https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/5826291148308-Why-is-my-email-list-import-under-review
Updated 2025-12-16. Risk screening can lead to human review. The target is 72 hours, with possible longer waits during heavy volume. This is not an added/skipped outcome or guaranteed deadline.

## Import denied

URL: https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/5826215563796-Why-was-my-email-list-import-denied
Updated 2025-12-17. High-risk lists can be denied to protect sending reputation. The suggested alternative is an off-platform voluntary signup form. Denial does not by itself prove a lack of consent. The decision to avoid treating denial as a syntax error or bypassing it by dividing the list is an authored preparation rule.

## Export setup

URL: https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/20680312785428-How-do-I-move-from-Beehiiv-to-Substack
Updated 2026-08-03. Settings/Export Data offers a full subscriber export; download its completed CSV under Historical Exports. The described subscriber import is free-status, not proof of paid billing transfer.

URL: https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/14306608016404-How-do-I-move-from-Mailchimp-to-Substack
Updated 2026-07-13. Audience/All contacts → Export Audience → Export as CSV produces a ZIP. Unzip the audience export for subscriber CSV preparation. The separate campaigns ZIP is content, not subscriber data. Inspect actual consent/status evidence rather than importing all contacts indiscriminately.

## Preparation conventions

Email-only CSV with an `email` header, stable row IDs, exact-duplicate grouping, conservative suppression, and cohort ledgers are authored preparation conventions, not asserted Substack field requirements. Validate the native preview. Suspected typos need owner evidence; address shape is not proof of delivery. Samples and described screenshots cannot establish complete-list coverage.

Use this register to support the requested answer, not to reproduce all source notes. For result reconciliation, aggregate added/skipped counts do not identify individual rows; a known destination match is only a likely skip until row evidence confirms it. Case-only variants do not invalidate a separately confirmed exact address; preserve originals and make any consolidation explicit, with suppression taking precedence.
