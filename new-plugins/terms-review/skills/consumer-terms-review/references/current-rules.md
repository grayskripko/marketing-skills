## CMA37: final revision

https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/unfair-contract-terms-cma37 — updated 2026-07-22; links the current 134-page PDF, not the January draft.
https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a609329b00f3323bf1a23f3/unfair_contract_terms_guidance.pdf — final 2026-07-22 guidance.

Locator map: chapter 2 consumer/notice scope; chapter 3 prohibited restrictions; chapter 4 fairness/transparency; chapter 5 narrow exemptions; chapter 6 overview p57; hidden terms p60, exclusions p63, variation p91, consumer termination p104, trader termination p113, renewal p117, disputes p119.

Deciding details: transparency does not settle fairness. Core exemption requires transparency and prominence, does not cover notices or listed suspect terms (5.1–5.5). Variation needs predictable scope, method, valid triggers, prior notice and a real exit (6.44–6.55). Blanket substantial-prepayment forfeiture can confer unjustified gain (6.60). Small genuine reservation deposits require clear narrow retention circumstances; no fixed permitted percentage (6.62). Termination charges must account for savings, mitigation and avoid duplicate compensation (6.63–6.65). For an exit offered before adverse variation, assess actual exercise without financial loss or serious inconvenience (6.47, p94), not its nominal existence. Ordinary default deductions and genuine reservation deposits have separate tests.

## Business guide

https://www.gov.uk/guidance/writing-a-fair-contract-for-customers — updated 2026-07-22.

Sections: fairness, clarity, hidden terms, exclusions, changes, cancellation, renewal, disputes. Customer terms must be both fair and transparent. Important consequences need early attention; customers need a real chance to understand before agreement. Injury/death from negligence and statutory product/service rights cannot be excluded. Renewal risks include long/early notice, new minimum terms and reliance on inertia. Reminders, easy switch-off, rolling arrangements and refunds are fairness remedies, not universal fixed time periods. Its spring 2027 forecast is superseded by the later announced timetable below; verify actual commencement and transition before applying new subscription rights.

## Price information

https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/price-transparency-cma209 — published 2025-11-18, updated 2026-01-07.
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/price-transparency-cma209/providing-clear-and-accurate-information-about-prices-summary — published 2026-01-07.

Sections “Provide the total price” and “Include any mandatory charges”: an invitation to purchase need not offer immediate buying. Include unavoidable calculable fees and taxes in the total there, not only the final checkout. If product nature prevents calculation, equally prominent calculation information is needed. Include total once calculable. Optional delivery can be separately priced; if delivery is compulsory with several choices, include the cheapest until another is chosen. Instalment collection does not remove total-price disclosure.

Express consent is needed for payments beyond the main obligation; preselected options or inaction are not express consent. Check the real selection mechanism and records rather than treating acceptance of all terms as the optional-extra decision.

## Distance-selling boundary

https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses — loaded 2026-10-11; no page revision date displayed in retrieved content.

Before order: identity/contact, description, price including taxes, payment/delivery, cancellation conditions and duration/termination information. Confirmation on a durable medium and distance cancellation rules are distinct duties. Goods ordinarily have a 14-day cancellation period from receipt; do not transfer that starting point to services or digital content. Follow official statutory links to verify exceptions and early-performance/refund rules for the specific contract.

The standard XHTML pages failed in the reader; the official data.html exports loaded successfully. Event-date amendments and commencement still need checking for any different period. The original 32-page instrument also loaded: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/pdfs/uksi_20133134_en.pdf (original, not used as a substitute for current provision text).

Eligible service contracts: ordinary period ends 14 days after agreement day (30(2)); missing cancellation information can extend the period (31). Specifically requested urgent-repair visits have a narrow exclusion, not all appointments (28(1)(e), (2)). Early-performance charges require the applicable request/information conditions and proportionate supplied service (36); default-loss accounting does not establish a statutory deduction. Regulation 34 establishes reimbursement; the timing branch differs for goods returns and other contracts. Do not insert a deadline unless that branch and its trigger are verified for the case.
