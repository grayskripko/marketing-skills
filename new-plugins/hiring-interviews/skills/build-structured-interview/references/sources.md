# Source notes

- [OPM structured interviews](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/assessment-and-selection/structured-interviews/): research read 2026-10-09; reopened 2026-10-10. US federal assessment guidance supports job-related common questions and common rating standards. It is a design reference, not a worldwide employer mandate.
- [US DOJ AI and disability guidance](https://www.ada.gov/resources/ai-guidance/): research read 2026-10-09; reopened 2026-10-10. US ADA context: hiring tools can disadvantage applicants with disabilities, and accommodations matter. This does not determine the user's legal obligations in an unknown jurisdiction.

Apart from the source-backed design points identified above, the operational instructions in SKILL.md are this plugin's rules of thumb and product safeguards. They are not legal requirements established by these sources. The employer's authorized role brief and rubric supply case facts and rating definitions.
