# When the cause may be on the site

Pick the two or three most likely from what the user said; do not list all. Each item says what to look at and which result points where.

1. **Tracking.** An analytics tag removed or changed, a consent banner change, a new analytics property, new filters. Sessions fall while Search Console clicks stay flat.
2. **Indexing and access.** robots.txt rules, a noindex tag, canonical changes, server errors, redirects after a release or migration. Search Console's Page indexing report shows when the indexed count changed.
3. **Scope.** The whole site, or a few sections or templates. Use the Pages table. A few templates suggest a site change on them.
4. **Positions or demand.** Average position stable with impressions down means demand or query mix; position down means ranking.
5. **Seasonality and news.** The same weeks last year; a news spike that ended; a product, price or brand change.
6. **Results page changes.** New features or AI answers above the result on the main queries: click-through rate down while position is stable. Check by searching the top queries.
7. **Lost links or listings** that sent traffic.
