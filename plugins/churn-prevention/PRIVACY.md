# Privacy

Churn Prevention Kit is a set of instructions and reference text for an AI assistant. It has no server, no account and no storage of its own.

- Network scope: this plugin does no web search and fetches no pages. It reads only what you paste or attach, runs nothing, changes no files, settings or billing systems, sends no messages, and may use your assistant's code or spreadsheet tool to compute the tables it shows.
- Personal data: read only. The plugin reads exports you choose to paste, which may contain customer data; it does not store, send or change that data.
- Categories of data: whatever you paste or attach, typically account ids, plans, prices, dates, tenure, decline codes, consent flags and regions. Exports may also contain names, emails or phone numbers; the skills do not need them, ignore them and never repeat them. If a full card or bank account number appears, the skills stop and ask you to remove it.
- Purpose: computing the tables and plans you ask for, during that conversation.
- Recipients: the data goes to the provider of the AI assistant you use, under that provider's terms. The plugin sends it nowhere else.
- Retention: the plugin keeps nothing. Retention of your conversation is set by your assistant provider.
- Your controls: remove names, emails and payment details before pasting; results are given by segment, and per-account lists appear only when you ask, with account ids only.
