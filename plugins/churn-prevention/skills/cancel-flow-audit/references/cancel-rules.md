# Cancellation rules register

Re-check any row older than 6 months before acting on it. Read dates, URLs and ids stay in this file; an answer gives a row as one plain sentence with its public name and status (rule 8 of the skill), and a URL or read date only when the user asks where a rule comes from.

Status words: **in force**, **vacated**, **pre-proposal** (questions asked, no rule text), **proposal** (planned, no adopted text), **upcoming** (announced, not yet in force), **settled** (court order binding one company; an enforcement example, not a rule), **unverified** (not read on a primary or reliable secondary page when this file was built). Quoted words are short extracts with their section number; everything else is a summary in this plugin's own words.

Jurisdictions in this version: United States (federal and California), European Union (with Germany as a member-state example) and the United Kingdom. Other places: say the register does not cover them. App-store rules sit in `store-billing.md`.

## United States, California (Business and Professions Code §17602, as amended by AB 2863)

Applies to contracts entered into, amended or extended on or after 1 July 2025 (subsection (j)). URL for every row: https://california.public.law/codes/business_and_professions_code_section_17602

| Id | Subsection | What it asks for (statute phrases in quotes) | Status | Read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CA-c | (c) | A toll-free phone number, an email address, a postal address (only if the business bills the customer directly), or another "cost-effective, timely, and easy-to-use" way to cancel | in force | 2026-10-08 |
| CA-d | (d)(1) | A customer who signed up online can end it online, "at will", without steps that "obstruct or delay" ending it immediately, through a "prominently located direct link or button" or an "immediately accessible termination email" the business formats and provides | in force | 2026-10-08 |
| CA-e1 | (e)(1) | On a phone call, a save offer is allowed only after telling the customer they can finish cancelling at any time by saying "cancel"; once they do, the business must "promptly process" it | in force | 2026-10-08 |
| CA-e2 | (e)(2) | Online, a save offer is allowed only with a "click to cancel" link or button that is "prominently located and continuously and proximately displayed"; once used, the business must "promptly process" the cancellation | in force | 2026-10-08 |
| CA-g | (g)(2) | Notice of a price change at least 7 and at most 30 days before it takes effect | in force | 2026-10-08 |
| CA-h | (h) | An annual reminder stating what is charged, how often, and how to cancel | in force | 2026-10-08 |

What §17602 does **not** contain: a limit on the number of save offers, or a required cancellation receipt. Checks on those points are plugin heuristics or cite another jurisdiction.

## European Union

| Id | Rule | What it asks for | Status | Read | URL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EU-WF | Directive (EU) 2023/2673, new Article 11a of the Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU (withdrawal function) | For distance contracts concluded through an online interface that carry a right of withdrawal, and only during the withdrawal period: a withdrawal function labelled "withdraw from contract here" or equally clear wording, easy to find and available throughout; a second, confirming step; an acknowledgement on a durable medium (email is enough) without undue delay, with content, date and time. Member states had to transpose by 19 December 2025 and apply from 19 June 2026; national laws may fix exact labels | in force | 2026-10-03 | https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32023L2673 |
| DE-312k | Germany, §312k BGB (cancellation button) | For consumer contracts for continuing obligations concluded online: a button labelled "Verträge hier kündigen" or equally clear wording that opens a confirmation page with a "jetzt kündigen" button; both always available and directly and easily reachable; immediate confirmation of receipt in text form with content, date and time, and the end date. If the button is missing, the customer may cancel at any time without a notice period | in force (start date 1 July 2022 is not shown on the page read: unverified) | 2026-10-03 | https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__312k.html |
| EU-DFA | Digital Fairness Act | Planned EU law on dark patterns, subscriptions and more. Commission proposal expected in the fourth quarter of 2026; no adopted text found as of the read date. Member states other than Germany may have their own cancellation rules; this register does not list them. Say so when the user sells into another EU country.
