# Method and limits

This plugin's rule of thumb: use source-linked, nonoverlapping revenue/cost buckets and explicit assumptions to compare contribution. It is a decision-workpaper method, not a legal rule, platform fee schedule or accounting standard. No universal fee, tax rate, target margin or return rate applies.

Source research: domain-ecommerce-A.md, “Seller Margin Check” calculation specification; domain-ecommerce-B.md, “Seller Margin Check” and “Retail Ads Review”; in the repository's plans/overnight/research. Research recorded as read 2026-10-09. These are design reports, not independent proof that seller records are complete. Use the user's report definitions and fee schedules for the actual calculation.

No external law, study or platform requirement is adopted in these instructions. No live source is fetched. Recheck applicable sources separately if a later task needs a regulated act or current platform terms.
