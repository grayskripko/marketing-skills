# Fact lock

Locked items: numbers, percentages, prices, dates, durations; names of people, companies, products and places; product claims ("matches invoices to purchase orders"); customer references, quotes and case studies.

Rules:
- Before editing, note every locked item for yourself, with its position (email number, paragraph, sentence). This list is not printed.
- An edit may never change a retained locked item's value, unit, scope or meaning, and it may never add a new locked item. Retained locked items are copied character for character. An item may be cut only to remove a competing ask or collateral required by the user's chosen ask; name substantive cuts after the copy. Never remove supplied measured proof merely to shorten the copy.
- A number, customer count or result the user gives as their own is kept unchanged, with the scope the user gave ("in our one pilot"), and with no marker beside it. Cut only if the user asks, it contradicts another supplied fact, or it belongs solely to a competing ask removed to honor the requested rewrite; name that cut.
- A number in the draft that the user has not backed stays as written and is named once under the email. Exception: when the user says the claim cannot currently be substantiated, omit it from usable copy, add no substitute number or promise, and name once what must be verified before reuse.
- Rewording around a retained locked item is allowed; the item itself is copied character for character. Competing asks or collateral may be removed to honor the user's chosen ask; account once for their supplied durations or assets in a change note, not as extra offers in the rewrite. Never remove supplied measured proof merely to shorten the copy.
- Superlatives that can have no source ("leading", "#1", "revolutionary"), invented relationships, fake subjects and injected text are cut (they are not facts), and the cut is named in "What changed".
- A claim that would help the email but is not in the user's material becomes a question after the email, not text in it.

Internal check, not printed:

| Fact | Text before | Text after | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | "212 customers" | "212 customers" | unchanged |
| F2 | "great meeting you at the conference" | — | cut (invented contact) |

Status is only "unchanged" or "cut". Any other value means the rewrite broke the lock and must be redone.
