# Scorecard CE-01 to CE-12

For a line-by-line request, score each original content unit instead and use these checks only to make sure nothing is missed. Otherwise each applicable check scores 0, 1 or 2 and needs a quote from the email as evidence. Keep scoring internal unless requested; a requested full score includes every applicable check. Judge footer items only where applicable to the supplied recipient context; unknown applicability is a question, not a scored absence. The applicable maximum is 2 times the number of scored checks (24 only when all 12 apply); state every exclusion. This is a checklist score, not a reply-rate forecast. Bands and word counts below are rules of thumb of this plugin, not measured thresholds. The ids are for your own use; name checks in words when talking to the user.

| Id | Check | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CE-01 | Recipient relevance | Establishes clear recipient relevance in an order suited to the proposition | Relevance is understandable but generic | No supported relevance, or an invented reason to act |
| CE-02 | Honest subject | Subject matches the body and is not a fake reply or forward | Accurate but vague | "Re:"/"Fwd:" on a first message, or a subject that misstates the content |
| CE-03 | Clear offer | Clearly states what is offered and to whom, in as many sentences as needed | Offer is understandable but diffuse | No understandable offer |
| CE-04 | Outcome explanation | Explains the supplied inputs, actions and outputs behind a claimed outcome; a pilot result next to a mechanism does not prove the mechanism caused it unless the user's evidence says so | A claimed outcome has a vague explanation | A claimed outcome lacks an explanation. Exclude this check when no causal product outcome is claimed |
| CE-05 | Proof | Proof is specific and sourced in the user's facts | Proof is vague ("many teams"), or a specific number with no source yet (keep it as written and name it under the email; omit it from usable copy if the user says it cannot be substantiated) | A claim that contradicts the user's material, or a superlative that can have no source ("leading", "#1") |
| CE-06 | One primary next step | One clear action with effort appropriate to context and the user's objective | One action with avoidable effort or unclear relevance | Competing actions, or no usable next step |
| CE-07 | Reading burden | Enough context to decide, with no unnecessary material | Some avoidable length or missing context | Reading burden or missing context prevents understanding. English word bands are starting targets, not automatic penalties; assess required disclosures separately |
| CE-08 | Personal detail | A real, dated, sourced fact about the recipient that relates to the offer | A true fact unrelated to the offer | A detail that fits anyone in the segment, or an unsourced one. If the user gave no fact about the recipient, do not score this check; the maximum drops by 2 for this and every other excluded check |
| CE-09 | Honesty of relationship | No claims of past contact or shared history that the user has not confirmed | Vague warmth ("we should connect") | Invented meeting, call, referral or mutual contact; flattery used as a hook |
| CE-10 | Format and assets | Lightweight presentation with relevant, trustworthy assets and one clear destination | Optional assets or formatting add avoidable friction | Materially confusing or untrustworthy presentation. Required footer links are not discretionary sales links; an image or attachment alone does not imply failure |
| CE-11 | Wording quality | Accurate, specific and relevant wording | Some vague or distracting wording | Materially misleading or obstructive wording. Pattern count alone does not decide the score |
| CE-12 | Identity and opt-out | Supplied sender identity, a working opt-out, and all footer items whose jurisdictional requirement is established | One applicable item missing | Two or more applicable items missing, or a hidden opt-out. Do not assume unknown jurisdiction requires a postal address or sales notice |

In a pasted sequence, the first email uses the first-email band of CE-07, later emails the follow-up band and the last polite close the closing band.

Reason to act now is scored inside CE-01: a real reason from the user's material helps; an invented deadline or scarcity scores CE-01 as 0 and is flagged.
