# Editorial scoring aids

Use only when a score is requested. Define the scale and scope, support each displayed score with evidence from the original, and verify the total and applicable maximum. For line-by-line requests, cover each original content unit. Scores describe editorial quality, not predicted replies.

Relevant criteria can include supported recipient relevance, an honest subject, a clear offer and mechanism, bounded proof, one appropriate next step, reading burden, accurate recipient facts, honest relationship framing, useful presentation and assets, specific wording, and supplied identity and opt-out.

Do not turn suggested word lengths, link counts or missing recipient research into automatic penalties. Assess footer requirements only where applicability is established; unknown jurisdiction is not evidence of a missing mandatory footer.
