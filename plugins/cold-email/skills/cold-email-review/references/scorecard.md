# Scorecard CE-01 to CE-12

Each check scores 0, 1 or 2 and needs a quote from the email as evidence. The total is out of 24 and is a checklist score, not a reply-rate forecast. Bands and word counts below are heuristics of this plugin, not measured thresholds.

| Id | Check | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CE-01 | Reader and reason | The first two lines name who this is for and why it reaches them now | Reader is clear, reason is generic | Opens with the sender, the product or a greeting ritual |
| CE-02 | Honest subject | Subject matches the body and is not a fake reply or forward | Accurate but vague | "Re:"/"Fwd:" on a first message, or a subject that misstates the content |
| CE-03 | Offer in one sentence | One sentence says what is offered and to whom | Offer is spread over several sentences | No offer can be found |
| CE-04 | Mechanism | Says how the product produces the result | Result with a vague "how" | Result only, no "how" |
| CE-05 | Proof | Proof is specific and sourced in the user's facts | Proof is vague ("many teams"), or a specific number with no source yet (keep it, mark `[PROOF NEEDED]`) | A claim that contradicts the user's material, or a superlative that can have no source ("leading", "#1") |
| CE-06 | One ask | One ask the reader can answer in a word | One ask that needs effort (a 30-minute call up front) | Several asks, or no ask at all |
| CE-07 | Length | First email about 50 to 125 words; follow-up 30 to 80; closing email 20 to 50 (heuristics) | Up to 50 words over the band | More than 50 words over the band, or under 20 |
| CE-08 | Personal detail | A real, dated, sourced fact about the recipient that relates to the offer | A true fact unrelated to the offer | A detail that fits anyone in the segment, or an unsourced one |
| CE-09 | Honesty of relationship | No claims of past contact or shared history that the user has not confirmed | Vague warmth ("we should connect") | Invented meeting, call, referral or mutual contact; flattery used as a hook |
| CE-10 | Plain format | Plain text, at most one link, no attachment | Two links or light formatting | Images, several links, an attachment or a tracking-heavy layout in a first email |
| CE-11 | Stock phrasing | None of the patterns in `stock-phrasing-cold.md` | One or two | Three or more |
| CE-12 | Identity and opt-out | Sender name and company, a working opt-out line and, where the law requires, a postal address and identification as a commercial message | One of these missing | Two or more missing, or an opt-out that is hidden |

In a pasted sequence, the first email uses the first-email band of CE-07, later emails the follow-up band and the last polite close the closing band.

Reason to act now is scored inside CE-01: a real reason from the user's material helps; an invented deadline or scarcity scores CE-01 as 0 and is flagged.
