# Offer check

Use these checks internally unless the user requests scoring. Each applicable line scores 0 to 2; sum the displayed values and state the applicable maximum. This is a checklist score, not a reply-rate forecast. Absence of urgency is not a reason to block discovery outreach.

| Line | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specific | Names what is delivered and to whom | Partly specific | "Solutions for businesses" |
| Practical gain | A concrete change for the reader's work | A general benefit | No gain stated |
| Reason to act now | A specific real reason from the user's material | A general real reason | None given; flag invented urgency separately |
| Proof | Specific proof with a source | Vague proof | None, or proof that looks invented |

A missing mechanism sentence is not scored here; it is the first reason in the "Not ready yet" verdict.
