# Stock phrasing in cold outreach

Patterns that make an email read as mass-produced. Flag them when they appear in the user's text; never add them. Remove or replace each with something specific from the user's material, or with a `[DETAIL NEEDED]` slot. This list is this plugin's own grouping, and the ids are what the change log cites.

## Openers that could go to anyone
- CS-01 Wellness wishes as the first line (hoping the reader is well, that the week is going great).
- CS-02 "I came across your profile/company and was impressed" with no named detail.
- CS-03 "I'll keep this short" followed by a long email.
- CS-04 Introducing the sender's job title before the reader's problem.

## Empty praise and fake closeness
- CS-05 Compliments on the reader's "impressive work" with no named piece of work.
- CS-06 "As a fellow founder / leader" used to claim kinship.
- CS-07 "Quick question" as a subject when the body has no question.

## Inflated claims
- CS-08 Category words used as proof: "leading", "best-in-class", "world-class", "#1", "cutting-edge", "revolutionary".
- CS-09 Guarantees of results ("guaranteed", "risk-free") with no terms from the user.
- CS-10 "10x", "double your revenue" and similar multiples without a named source.
- CS-11 "Free" used as the main hook in the subject or first line.

## Vague value
- CS-12 "Help companies like yours grow / scale / streamline" with no mechanism.
- CS-13 "Solutions", "synergies", "leverage", "unlock", "empower" in place of what the product does.
- CS-14 "Take X to the next level".

## Pressure and filler in follow-ups
- CS-15 A follow-up whose only job is to push the thread up again, with no new reason.
- CS-16 "Following up on my last email" as the whole message.
- CS-17 "Did you get a chance to look?" as the only content.
- CS-18 Guilt or pressure closers ("I'll assume you're not interested", "last chance").

## Asks that cost too much
- CS-19 "Do you have 30 minutes this week?" in a first email to a stranger.
- CS-20 Calendar link, deck and trial offered together.
- CS-21 "Let me know your thoughts" as the only call to action.
