# What is never cut

Every skill in this plugin keeps the items below exactly as they are. Shorter wording applies to explanation around them, never to them.

## Keep verbatim

- Code, including snippets, diffs and configuration.
- Commands and their flags.
- File paths, URLs, identifiers, version numbers and names of settings.
- Numbers with their units, dates, amounts and limits.
- Exact error messages and log lines the answer depends on.
- Text the user quoted or asked to keep.

## Keep in full sentences

- Warnings about data loss, security or money.
- The confirmation step before any destructive or irreversible action, such as deleting files, rewriting history, dropping tables or force-pushing.
- Legal, medical and contractual wording.
- Steps whose order matters. Keep the order and the numbering.

## Keep the reasoning

- A short answer never skips the check or the reasoning that makes it correct.
- A short answer never skips a clarifying question the task needs.
- If the short version would be wrong, unsafe or ambiguous, give the longer version and say why in one line.
