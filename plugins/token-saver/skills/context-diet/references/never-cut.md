# What is never cut

Select material according to the task: preserve all active obligations when trimming instructions, and all state, constraints and evidence needed to resume a handoff. Irrelevant or superseded material may be omitted from summaries, with material omissions disclosed. Preserve retained items under “Keep verbatim” exactly; “Keep in full sentences” governs presentation rather than requiring reproduction of all source text.

## Keep verbatim

- Code, including snippets, diffs and configuration.
- Commands and their flags.
- File paths, URLs, identifiers, version numbers and names of settings.
- Numbers with their units, dates, amounts and limits.
- Exact error messages and log lines the answer depends on.
- Spans explicitly requested verbatim and quoted literals whose exact spelling matters. Ordinary source instruction prose may be reworded if obligations, scope, qualifiers and timing stay intact; quotation alone does not lock prose.

## Keep in full sentences

- Warnings about data loss, security or money.
- The confirmation step before any destructive or irreversible action, such as deleting files, rewriting history, dropping tables or force-pushing.
- Legal, medical and contractual wording.
- Steps whose order matters. Keep the order and the numbering.
