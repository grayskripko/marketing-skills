# Lawyer Meetings

Prepare notes for a meeting with your lawyer: a document list, timeline, conflicting accounts, questions and a concise agenda. After the meeting, turn your supplied notes into actions, owners and open questions.
For business owners and teams preparing for a qualified lawyer's review. Supply authorized contracts, messages, invoices or draft policies as readable files or pasted text. You can also supply your account of events, clearly labelled as yours. No accounts or paid APIs are required.

## What this package runs and connects to

This is a skills-only package of Markdown instructions and reference text, JSON metadata, and a static SVG icon. It includes no executable scripts, dependencies, installers, hooks, MCP or LSP servers, or background processes. Installing it does not run a program or connect an account.

These skills work only from task text, attachments, and local files that you explicitly supply. They do not instruct the assistant to run code, install software, search the web, fetch URLs or robots.txt, call APIs, send messages, publish content, or change account settings. URLs in this package identify sources or support pages; they are not connection endpoints or retrieval instructions. A supplied URL is treated as text: paste the relevant content instead.

The publisher receives no task data and operates no collection, telemetry, or storage service for this plugin. Your assistant provider still processes and may retain your conversation and attachments under its own terms. Any output file you explicitly request is created by your assistant's existing file tools, not by a bundled program.

Host file readers and, for scans, the host's available OCR may process supplied files. Key extracted passages must be checked against the image. The skills read bundled, dated reference text locally; its public source links are citations, not instructions to refresh it. No tool installation or code execution is instructed. Results default to the conversation; local file writing requires an explicit output destination.

| build-factual-chronology | Records and recollections | Events in date order, sources for each fact and disputed accounts |

## Try it

- “Index these signed agreements, draft amendments and emails for my lawyer. Show missing attachments.”
- “Build a factual timeline. Separate what the messages say from what I remember.”
- “Prepare a short lawyer meeting agenda. I want delivery of the goods. Preserve the facts that weaken my position.”

The assistant reads the supplied material directly. No need to retype readable files. It gives each source an ID and cites its location. Signed and draft documents stay separate. Missing evidence is marked “not supplied”; it is not treated as proof. Your jurisdiction (the country or region for legal review) and dates are recorded as supplied, or marked unknown. The law named in a contract is recorded separately.

- “Turn these lawyer meeting notes into an action list. Keep unconfirmed points open and record who agreed to do what.”

## Source-linked record selection

The skills include a dated knowledge snapshot and intake matrices for email provenance and export searchability, U.S. federal electronic-record preservation questions, payment reconciliation with a Stripe-specific path, and goods inspection/use/discovery/notice records. Each matrix links to source passages and says when it applies. Unknown jurisdiction or provider keeps factual intake general. UCC model-code passages guide record collection; counsel supplies the applicable enacted text. See the [source registry](skills/index-matter-documents/references/sources.md).

## Preparation for professional review

This organizes facts and questions for a qualified lawyer. It does not replace legal advice, advise signing, choose legal claims or plan how to conduct a lawsuit. It does not guarantee legal privilege (legal protection against disclosure). Supplied court dates and urgent notices are flagged for prompt lawyer review; court deadlines are never invented or calculated. The separate list of party names helps your lawyer check for conflicts of interest. The assistant cannot confirm that there are no conflicts. These are AI-assisted preparation notes: have a qualified lawyer review them before anyone relies on them.

## Data and network

Network scope: none. No URLs are fetched, including links in supplied documents. No usage data is collected. No external services, account access or paid APIs are used. Nothing is sent, filed or published. The plugin contains instructions and references, with no executable tools or service of its own.

The AI host processes supplied material under its own terms. File readers are provided by the host. For scans, key passages must be checked against the image. Optical character recognition (OCR) may help extract text. If a passage cannot be read, it remains unresolved. Pasted readable text is the no-setup route.

Use only material you are authorized to process. Remove unnecessary personal data and credentials first. List necessary party names separately for the lawyer. Keep unnecessary private details out of the brief and supporting tables. Originals are preserved. Results appear in the conversation unless you request output files and a destination; those files are retained there until you remove them.
