## 3. Summary of 2025 edition changes

https://www.aws.org/magazines-and-media/welding-journal/wj-july-25-feature-05-sperko/ — read 2026-10-13 (public preview; full text for members). AWS Welding Journal, July 2025, W. J. Sperko, "Summary of Changes to ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code Section IX": the changes become mandatory for new qualifications on January 1, 2026; all changes are listed in the Summary of Changes at the front of Section IX.

https://sperkoengineering.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025_addenda.pdf — read 2026-10-13. The same article as published, dated 7-1-25. A practitioner summary by a Section IX committee member; it states its opinions are not ASME's. Used for:
- QG-108: to update a WPS to the current edition using PQRs qualified under an earlier edition, all essential and, when toughness applies, supplementary essential variables required by the current edition must be recorded on those PQRs. WPSs qualified to earlier editions may still be used.
- QW-410.92: new supplementary essential variable; if beads on the coupon exceed 1 in in width, travel speed for heat input is measured along the transverse weave path, giving a much lower qualified heat input. (Removed for overlay by Special Notice R25-1666 above.)
- QW-407.2: the 80% PWHT time rule applies only to heat treatment below the lower transformation temperature.
- QW-202.2: now refers to the table of variables for each process for base metal and weld metal thickness ranges instead of directly to QW-451.
- QW-422: API 5L L-prefix grades take the P-Number and Group of the matching X-prefix grade.
- P-Numbers: titanium Grade 38 R54250 assigned new P-No. 54; cobalt alloy R31233 moved to new P-No. 81 from P-No. 49, which no longer exists; requalification not required, but the WPS/PQR should be revised to show the new number and the basis.
- Tension acceptance (re-read 2026-10-13, p. 4): acceptance has long allowed weld-metal tensile strength in a narrow case; the 2025 edition broadened this to whenever using the weld metal's tensile strength is permitted by the referencing code, standard or specification. Verify the applicable clause in the licensed text; keep this separate from the 5% allowance for a base-metal break.

## 3a. Practitioner and research notes used for specialist checks

https://ch-delivery.lincolnelectric.com/api/public/content/16193e81eadf469f8a6c1181448f3481?v=a2854c20 — read 2026-10-13. Teresa Melfi (Lincoln Electric), article on changes to QW-409.1 for calculating heat input, Welding Journal, June 2010, pp. 61–63. Conventional meters show average voltage and current (RMS for AC); with waveform-controlled welding the error of V × A against true energy "can be in a positive or negative direction". The 2010 edition added heat input = energy (J) / weld bead length, or power (J/s) × arc time (s) / weld bead length, measured by a meter that samples the waveform rapidly. The 2010 paragraph lettering may differ in later editions; check the declared edition. Used in the waveform heat-input rule.

https://www.munichre.com/hsb/en/services/engineering/global-inspection/resource-center/pressure-points/25q102.html — read 2026-10-13. Philip Gilston, HSB Pressure Points, March 2025, on Section I (2023 edition) PW-41.2.2: for a butt joint welded from one side only, the procedure qualification must demonstrate full penetration, and the coupon must also be a single-welded butt joint; a double-sided or backed PQR cannot support it, while a single-welded PQR can support a double-sided or backed production weld. Stated as the author's opinions. Used only for Section I jobs.

https://www.nist.gov/publications/effect-charpy-striker-configuration-low-and-high-energy-nist-verification-specimens — read 2026-10-13. E. Lucon and J. D. Splett, NIST Journal of Research vol. 123, published 2018-09-12. 2 mm and 8 mm strikers are both in ASTM E23 and ISO 148-1; 8 mm strikers tend to give higher energy at 200 J and above; results for the lots studied were partly contradictory, and NIST decided to certify verification lots separately for each striker. No conversion factor follows.
