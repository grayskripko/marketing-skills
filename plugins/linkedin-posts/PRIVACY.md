# Privacy — LinkedIn Posts

This package contains instructions and reference text. It ships no executable integration and runs no service of its own.

- Data: pasted profiles, career facts, post samples, company excerpts and message threads may contain professional names, roles, employers, dates and opinions. Only task-relevant professional details are used. Contact details, account ids, credentials, private-life details and sensitive personal information are not repeated in output.
- Purpose: rewrite profiles, draft posts or messages, and summarize supplied person or company material in the current conversation.
- Recipients: the AI assistant you use processes the conversation and attachments under its provider's terms. The package sends data to no separate recipient or service.
- Retention: the package stores nothing and has no telemetry. Your assistant's conversation and attachment retention applies. A local deliverable is saved only if you ask the assistant to save one; manage that file yourself afterward.
- Controls: supply only material you have the right to share. Redact contact details, confidential information and credentials before pasting. You can delete conversations and attachments through your assistant's controls, subject to its provider's policies.
- Network scope: none. No URLs, robots.txt, search results, profiles or services are fetched. No account connection, paid API, inbox access, sending or publishing occurs. Links in supplied material are source labels only. The assistant may count words, characters or rows locally.

No setup is needed. Pasted text and existing exports are the supported input paths. Possessing an export does not by itself establish permission to share every person's data in it.
