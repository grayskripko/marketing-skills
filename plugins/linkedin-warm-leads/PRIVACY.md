# Privacy: LinkedIn Warm Leads

This plugin is a set of instructions and reference text. It ships no code and runs no service of its own.

- Categories of personal data: it reads the LinkedIn data export files you explicitly supply for the task. These hold your own profile and activity and other people's data: your connections' names, profile links, employers, job titles, connection dates, sometimes email addresses, and the text of messages and invitation notes between you and them.
- Purpose: to help you decide whom in your own network to contact and to draft messages that you send yourself, in the current conversation.
- What it does not do: it does not look anyone up, add data about your contacts from other sources, find or verify email addresses, open LinkedIn or send anything. It does not repeat email addresses or phone numbers in answers unless you ask.
- Recipients: none beyond the AI assistant you are already using, whose provider processes your conversation under its own terms. The plugin sends nothing anywhere.
- Retention: the plugin stores nothing. A file is written only when you ask for one, in your own working folder; a sortable CSV holds your connections' names, employers and the plugin's warmth notes about them, so delete it when you no longer need it. Your assistant's own conversation retention applies.
- Controls: supply only the files the task needs (usually Connections.csv and messages.csv); remove messages or columns you do not want read; delete the downloaded archive, any file you asked for and the conversation when you are done.
- Your responsibility: you decide whom to message and remain responsible for lawful use of your contacts' data (for example under GDPR) and for respecting anyone who asked not to be contacted.
- Network scope: no network requests, no web search, no calls to other services. If your assistant has a code tool, it may run a short local script to summarise large CSV files.
