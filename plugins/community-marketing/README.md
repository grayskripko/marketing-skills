# Community Marketing Kit

Community marketing without astroturfing.

> A vendor's engineer drafts for a sysadmin forum: "Tired of flaky CI? I found Rivetci, it fixed everything. Upvote if it helps!"
> **Do not post (PC-01, PC-06, PC-10).** "I found" hides the job, which staff on a discussion board should disclose clearly (16 CFR 255.5, Example 8; FTC guidance). Not reworked (RF-1); AI-text stance unknown, so: a disclosed outline to write yourself.

## What it does

- **community-post-check**: 13 checks and a verdict on a draft for a community you don't run.
- **venue-rules**: a mode per community you belong to, from its rules.
- **advocate-program**: disclosure kit, US/UK/EU incentive rules, post audit.
- **community-health**: unanswered questions and newcomer return from your export.
- **community-blueprint**: join/sponsor/build, guidelines, moderation, incidents.

## Examples

- I work on Rivetci. Check this sysadmin reply: "Tired of flaky CI? I found Rivetci, it fixed everything. Upvote if it helps!"
- 30 days: 300 questions, 48 unanswered. Newcomers: 42 of 84 who got a reply posted again, 6 of 36 who didn't. What does it say?
- 40 ambassadors get our paid plan at no cost plus swag and post about us. What must they disclose, and what can't we ask?

## How it works

Each rule shows source, read date and label; rules over six months old are flagged. Rates carry sample sizes and intervals. Not legal advice.

## What it will not do

Fake independent posts, extra accounts, vote requests, paid positive reviews, ban evasion, member scraping.

## Data and network

Network scope: this plugin runs no web search and calls no service. It works on what you paste or attach. If you name the address of one public community-rules page and your assistant has a fetch tool, the skill may read that single page: public pages only, robots.txt respected, no login, and no second attempt when the site blocks the request; if robots.txt cannot be confirmed or the page is blocked, paste the rules instead. Reddit pages are never fetched; paste the subreddit rules instead. It posts nothing, sends no messages, and changes no files or settings.

## Personal data

Read only: community exports (whitelisted columns), advocates' posts and names if pasted, incident descriptions. Members appear as M1, M2; advocates as A1, A2. See PRIVACY.md.

## Troubleshooting

"Not checkable" names what to paste.
