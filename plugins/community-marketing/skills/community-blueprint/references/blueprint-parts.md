# Blueprint parts

Slots and criteria to fill from the user's facts. Nothing here is a finished text.

## A. One primary outcome (R-SPACES)

Pick one of Support; Product ideation and feedback; Acquisition and advocacy; Content and contribution; Engagement; Success. Give it one metric with a formula the user can compute from their own data, for example Support → unanswered share and time to first response (community-health metrics 1 and 2). A second outcome only if the user insists, and then ranked.

## B. Join, sponsor or build

| Option | Weekly hours needed (user's estimate) | What you own | Exit risk | When it fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Not yet: test demand | about 2 hours, once | nothing yet | none | demand is unknown: ask a sample of members or customers (the user sets the number) whether they would join and where they already talk; build only if enough say yes (threshold set by the user; heuristic of this plugin) |
| Join existing venues | the poster's own time | nothing; your posts live on someone else's site | rules or owners change | members already gather elsewhere and the poster takes part personally |
| Sponsor a venue through its owners (Mode 4) | the owners' terms plus follow-up | an agreement, not the members, unless the deal says so | the owner can end it | a respected venue fits and the owners offer it |
| Build your own | moderation, answering and newcomer replies, every week | member list, export and search indexing, depending on the platform | high if the platform has no export | the stated hours cover the weekly load |

Demand unknown (nobody has asked members whether they want it): recommend the test first, not the build. Recommend building only if demand is shown and the stated hours cover the weekly load; otherwise say which hours are missing. Hours not given: ask at the end and mark the build option "cannot judge".

## C. Platform scores

Score each candidate the user names 0, 1 or 2 on: admin export of members and posts; public search indexing (wanted or not); moderation tools (roles, slow mode, approval queue, audit log); real-time chat vs threaded discussion fitting the outcome; members' familiarity; platform rules that limit what the owner can do. Weights come from the user. No brand ranking beyond the user's own list.

## D. Member journey with graduated access (heuristic of this plugin)

read only → introduction or rules acknowledgement → first post → links allowed after N posts or N days → direct messages to other members after the same threshold. The user sets N. Graduated access limits drive-by promotion and spam bots and gives newcomers a protected first step. Every newcomer's first post gets a reply within the reply window.

## E. Guidelines draft

Separate **rules** (enforced, at most 8, each one sentence a moderator can apply) from **norms** (tone, good faith; encouraged, not enforced). When rewriting existing rules, print a before/after table and score each existing rule 0–2 on specific, enforceable, visible to newcomers. Slots:

1. Purpose: who the space is for, in one line.
2. Rules, numbered.
3. Norms.
4. Promotion policy: members (a showcase thread or similar); vendors (disclosed in the opening sentence, in an agreed place, at a frequency the owner sets); staff (always labelled as staff). The company's own staff follow it too.
5. Staff and moderator conduct: visible staff label; no moderator action on threads about the moderator's own product; moderator tools never used to place own links or for any outside reward (P-RDT-MOD5 as a model).
6. Official links and staff identification, so members can check which accounts and links are genuine and spot impersonators.
7. Privacy: no collecting members' details, no sales messages to members, no member exports by members.
8. AI-written content: allowed, labelled, or banned; say which.
9. Enforcement ladder and appeal (`moderation-and-incidents.md`).
10. Platform floor: the platform's own rules apply too (for example P-DSC13, P-DSC18, P-DSC19 on a chat server).

## F. First 90 days

Founding members invited one by one from people who already help others; a reply rota so every newcomer's first post gets an answer; partnerships with other venues only through their owners (Mode 4); one measurement date. Then hand over to community-health with a stop rule: if the primary metric has not moved by the review date, change the plan or close the space.

## G. Risks

One person carrying most of the activity (check R-CAF later); depending on a venue you don't own; moderator burnout; staff answering everything so members never do; rules nobody enforces.
