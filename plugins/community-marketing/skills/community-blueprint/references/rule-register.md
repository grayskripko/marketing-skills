# Rule register

Every row carries its read date and URL. Status "read" means the official page was opened on that date. Status "unverified" means the official page could not be opened at build; such a row is cited only with the word "unverified" next to it. Labels: **law** (statutes and binding rules, including the FTC trade regulation rule in 16 CFR 465); **guidance** (a regulator's or agency's guidance, not binding by itself; this includes the FTC Endorsement Guides, 16 CFR 255, which the FTC calls administrative interpretations of the law it enforces); **code** (self-regulatory advertising code); **platform** (a site's own rules); **plugin policy** (stricter than any source, never presented as a legal duty). When the current date is more than six months after a row's read date (for the rows below: after 2027-04-03), add "re-check this rule at the source". Re-verify every row before each release.

## Platforms

Sitewide rules only. A community inside a platform (a subreddit, a server, a forum section) adds its own rules, which come from the user: pasted, or one public rules page. The community's rule wins when stricter; when it is looser, the law rows still apply to anyone connected to the product. "AI text" = whether the sitewide rules allow generated or AI-edited text; "not stated" means the community's own rules decide and, until seen, the stance is unknown.
