## Apply the distinctions

For “ignored rules,” identify the launch, source-selection setting, selected file and observed trace event before proposing content edits. For stale commands, supplied complete task definitions establish a replacement's existence; only execution evidence establishes its result. For contradictory approvals, overlapping scope and policy ownership must be known before merging text. For an SDK or retrieval boundary, expose the missing loader/query evidence rather than moving rules arbitrarily. To diagnose a past turn, inspect its existing model-request payload and passage provenance; a new replay cannot recover an absent original insertion record. Keep that diagnostic next step separate from verifying a corrected future response.
