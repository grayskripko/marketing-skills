# Diagnosis scorecard (DG-01 to DG-12)

Use this 12-item rubric as a landing-page default. Select applicable criteria by page purpose and supplied material; mark irrelevant checks N/A and unobservable checks unknown. Print scoring only when requested. Give evidence and a quote of up to 12 words for each scored criterion. Sum earned points and show the available denominator (2 per applicable, observable criterion); state unknown coverage separately. Compare only equivalent rubric configurations. Report critical factual defects separately; a total cannot offset them. This is a qualitative checklist, not a conversion forecast.

| id | Criterion | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DG-01 | Positioning clarity | Audience, alternative and differentiator are all clear in the first screen | One or two of the three are clear | None is clear |
| DG-02 | First screen does one job | One message and one action | Two competing messages or actions | Three or more |
| DG-03 | Reader as subject | Most sentences are about the reader's situation or result | Mixed | Mostly about the company |
| DG-04 | Specificity | Numbers, named steps or named systems in most claims | Some | Almost none |
| DG-05 | Mechanism stated | The main promise says how it works | Partly, or only for minor promises | Not at all |
| DG-06 | Real proof present | Identifiable testimonials, cases with numbers, or counts with a source | Proof exists but is vague or unsourced | No proof |
| DG-07 | Urgency is real or absent | A real reason from the page or the user's material (a real deadline, a cost of waiting the reader recognises), or no urgency at all (never add one) | — | Urgency with no real basis ("limited time" with no date or plan behind it); flag it |
| DG-08 | Single primary call to action | One primary action, others clearly secondary | Two actions of similar weight | Three or more |
| DG-09 | Call to action says what happens next | Names the next step ("Book a 20-minute walkthrough") | Partly | Generic ("Learn more", "Submit") |
| DG-10 | Skim test | Headings and calls to action alone carry the pitch | Partly | Headings are labels; the pitch is lost |
| DG-11 | Honest trade-off | Where claims are heavy, the page states a limit or a fit condition; or the page makes no heavy claims (note "no heavy claims") | Heavy claims with a limit only hinted | Absolute or unlimited claims ("all-in-one", "for every business", "never") with no fit condition |
| DG-12 | Stock-phrasing density | 0 to 1 SP-xx hits per 100 words | More than 1, up to 3 | More than 3 |

Notes:
- DG-12 bands are optional rough heuristics for comparable texts of at least 100 words, not empirical quality boundaries. For shorter copy, show phrases, hit count and word count with a qualitative explanation; do not give a density grade.
- With text-only input, use an explicitly stated opening-text boundary as a proxy; it does not establish the rendered first screen or visual weight. Score textual competing actions without claiming equal visual prominence.
- Score what is on the page, not what the user says the product does. What the user says goes into Assumptions or the offer check.

## Offer check

| Element | Present when |
|---|---|
| Specific | The reader can say what they would get, in concrete terms |
| Practical gain | Spend less, earn more, save time, remove a hassle, reduce a risk, stated for this reader |
| Real reason to act now | Present (from the page or the user's material), none (fine; not a defect), or invented (flag) |
| Proof | Something the reader can verify or recognise |
