# Kinds of evidence

What would back each kind of claim, and what does not.

| Claim | Evidence that backs it | Evidence that does not |
|---|---|---|
| A percentage or time saving | A measurement with who, how many, the period and the baseline | One anecdote stated as a general result; a vendor estimate with no measurement |
| A customer count | A current count from the user's own records, with what counts as a customer | Sign-ups counted as customers; an old peak figure |
| A ranking or "leading" | A named ranking or independent test, with its date and scope | Internal opinion; a ranking in an unrelated category |
| A comparison | A like-for-like comparison the user ran or can cite | A comparison with an outdated version of the alternative |
| A testimonial | The customer's own words, their name or role, and their permission; any relationship disclosed | A paraphrase presented as a quote; an unnamed "happy customer" |
| A guarantee | The terms the business actually offers | A general intention |
| An award or certificate | Its name, issuer and year | A nomination presented as a win |
| Urgency or scarcity | A real deadline or limit in the user's plan | A countdown that resets; a limit that does not exist |

When the evidence is missing, the ledger names the row above that fits, so the user knows what to collect.
