# Claims ledger format

## Statuses (relative to the user's evidence only)

| Status | Use when |
|---|---|
| Backed by your evidence | An evidence line states the same thing at the same strength and scope |
| Needs evidence | No evidence line relates to the claim |
| Stronger than your evidence | An evidence line supports a narrower, smaller or more qualified version |
| Remove | The claim cannot be checked as written, is a testimonial with no identifiable source or with substance beyond the evidence, or states urgency or scarcity that the evidence contradicts (when the evidence is silent on it, use "Needs evidence") |

## Ledger

| # | Claim | Location | Evidence line | Status | Accurate rewrite or what is needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "trusted by 10,000 teams" | Hero, line 2 | Notes: "212 paying customers" | Stronger than your evidence | "Used by 212 paying customers" |
| 2 | "the leading invoicing tool" | Hero, line 1 | none | Needs evidence | A named, dated ranking; otherwise cut |
| 3 | "saves 50% of AP time" | Features, line 4 | Notes: "one pilot: approval time down 31% over six weeks" | Stronger than your evidence | "In a six-week pilot with one customer, approval time fell 31%" |
| 4 | "Offer ends Friday" | Banner | Notes: "no price change planned" | Remove | The evidence gives no deadline |

## Counts

One line before the ledger, leaving out any status with zero: "Of 4 claims, 2 are stronger than your evidence, 1 needs evidence and 1 should be removed."

## Rules

- Quote the evidence line exactly as the user gave it, shortened with "…" if long.
- A rewrite may only narrow, scope or soften a claim to match the evidence. It never adds a fact that is not in the evidence.
- Where two evidence lines conflict, show both and judge the claim against the weaker line.
- Replace private people's names in quoted evidence lines with a role or a label ("Customer A").
- A testimonial with its source and permission in the evidence, whose substance matches an evidence line, is "Backed by your evidence" with "confirm the exact wording with the customer" in the last column.
- End with the one-line note from the skill: this is an evidence check, not legal advice.
