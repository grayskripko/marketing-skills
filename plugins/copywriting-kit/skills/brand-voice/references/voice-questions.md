# Voice interview (no samples)

Three short rounds. Ask one round at a time. The most useful questions come first, so the user can stop after round 1 and still get a usable profile.

## Round 1. Reader and stance (5 questions)

1. Who reads your writing most often, and what do they already know about your field?
2. When a reader finishes one of your pages, what should they feel sure of?
3. Which three words should a reader use to describe how you write? Which three words should they never use?
4. Do you write as "we", as "I", or about the company in the third person?
5. How formal are you with customers: contractions, first names, jokes?

## Round 2. Habits (5 questions)

6. Paste or describe one sentence you wrote that you like. What makes it right?
7. Paste or describe one sentence from your site that you dislike. What is wrong with it?
8. Which phrases do people in your industry overuse that you want to avoid?
9. Do you use exclamation marks, emoji, questions in headings, bold text? Where?
10. Short sentences or long ones? Lists or paragraphs?

## Round 3. Edges (up to 5 questions)

11. How do you talk about competitors, if at all?
12. How do you talk about limits or things your product does not do?
13. Are there legal or brand lines that must appear, or words you cannot use?
14. Who is the reader you most want to win, and how do they talk?
15. Anything else a writer should know before writing as you?

## After the interview

Build the profile with `voice-template.md`. Mark every rule that comes from the interview rather than from samples as "from interview", so the user can check it against real writing later.
