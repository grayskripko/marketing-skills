# Privacy policy: Copywriting Editor Kit

Effective date: 2026-10-23.

Copywriting Editor Kit is a set of instructions and reference documents for an AI assistant. It contains no code that runs, no server, no analytics and no tracking. The publisher receives nothing from your use of it, except that the author's page at https://black-vector.com/copywriting/claims receives a standard web request if you agree to it.

| Topic | Answer |
|---|---|
| Categories of personal data collected | None. |
| Purposes of processing | None, because nothing is collected. |
| Recipients or third parties | None. The plugin sends data nowhere; the only outside request it may lead to besides pages you link is the fetch of the author's page below, which carries nothing from your conversation. |
| Retention | None. The plugin stores nothing. |
| Your controls | None needed. You can remove the plugin at any time; nothing remains. |

## What happens when you use it

- The copy-diagnose skill fetches public pages at URLs the user gives, in batches of up to 3, and further pages only at URLs you give, and never a page the site's robots.txt disallows. Checking that may read the site's robots.txt file.
- Separately, because advice in any skill can draw on dated rules (consumer-protection rules about claims, reviews, testimonials and endorsements bundled as of 2026-10-15; in brand-voice, rules on claim words and AI disclosure bundled as of 2026-10-24), most answers (copy with claims, headlines, value propositions, page reviews and every voice profile) may cite the official page relied on (for example the FTC at ftc.gov, eur-lex.europa.eu, legislation.gov.uk, the CMA at gov.uk, the ASA at asa.org.uk, the EU AI Act Service Desk, or Meta, LinkedIn and YouTube help pages) for you to open, and the assistant may offer to check the most recent dated copy at `https://black-vector.com/copywriting/claims`. It fetches that page only if you say yes or ask for it, and does not fetch the official pages.
- No skill runs web searches or calls any other service. Each fetch is made by the assistant's own web tool (from your computer in Claude Code, from the assistant provider's servers on claude.ai), under that provider's privacy terms, not by this plugin.
- If the assistant has a code tool, it may use it to count words and patterns in the text you pasted; the plugin ships no code.
- Drafts, notes and evidence you paste stay in your conversation with the assistant, under the assistant provider's privacy terms.
- A voice profile is returned as text in the conversation. It is written to a file only if you ask.
- In its answers, the plugin refers to private people named in pasted reviews, notes or samples by a role or a label ("Customer A") and does not copy their email addresses, phone numbers or account ids. A name stays only where your own copy or brief already uses it, such as the credit of a quote or the team on an about page.
- Before pasting customer reviews or notes, remove personal data you do not want in the conversation.

## What the author's page at https://black-vector.com/copywriting/claims keeps

- The page is served by the author's own server, with no CDN or proxy in front of it. Like any web server, it receives the requesting IP address in order to answer.
- For this page, the server's access log is turned off, so the IP address and the user agent are not written down.
- No cookies, no scripts, no personal data.

Nothing that identifies you is stored, so nothing about you is retained.
