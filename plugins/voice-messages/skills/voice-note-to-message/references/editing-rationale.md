# Editing rationale

The fidelity checks and workflows are this plugin's rules of thumb. They are editorial instructions, not research-backed performance claims. Preserve the speaker's meaning before adapting the format; verify uncertainty, ownership, scope and corrections against the supplied text. No law, platform rule or study is used to decide an edit here.
