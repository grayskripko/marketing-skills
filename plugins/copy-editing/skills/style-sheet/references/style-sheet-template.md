# Style sheet layout

## 1. Summary

`Documents: 3 (D-A 640 words, D-B 410, D-C 520) · families checked: 10 · with variants: 4 · fact conflicts: 1 · open decisions: 1`

## 2. Census

| Family | Variant | D-A | D-B | D-C | Total | First locations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 3. Fact conflicts

| # | Fact | Value and location 1 | Value and location 2 | Question |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 4. Cross-references

| Reference | In | Status (present, missing, renamed) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Decisions

| Item | Chosen form | Basis (your rule n, majority n of m, named reference, plugin default, error) | Share |
|---|---|---|---|

## 6. Word list (A–Z)

One line per word or name, in the chosen form, with a short note where useful:

```
book (verb), booking (noun)
email
log in (verb), login (noun)
```

## 7. Find and replace

| Find (exact) | Replace | Documents | Whole word | Count |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Case-sensitive unless stated. A longer find string comes before a shorter one it contains. Never includes open decisions, fact conflicts or locked items.

## 8. Open decisions

| Item | Variants and counts | Why open | Question |
|---|---|---|---|

## 9. Paste-back sheet

Under 150 words, plain lines the user can paste at the top of a later request:

```
Style sheet (decided [date]): UK English, -ise. Product: Tallybook.
email, log in (verb), login (noun). Numbers: words to nine.
Dates: 4 November 2026. Times: 9 am. Headings: sentence case.
Open: members vs users.
```
