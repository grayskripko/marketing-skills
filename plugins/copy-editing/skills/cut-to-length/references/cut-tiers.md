# Cut tiers

Work from C1 down. Stop at the first tier that reaches the target. Log each cut with its tier and the words it saved, and check that the saved words add up to the original count minus the result; if they do not, recount. Never print "approx." in the ledger.

| Tier | What goes | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | a point made twice, or a summary of what was just said | keep the clearer of the two statements |
| C2 | lead-ins ("We are pleased to let you know that"), hedges ("in many ways", "somewhat"), sign-posts ("In this section we will") | a hedge that carries real uncertainty is meaning; keep it |
| C3 | long phrases with a short equivalent (`wordy-phrases.md`) | obligation words stay |
| C4 | second and later examples of the same point | keep the example the reader is most likely to recognise |
| C5 | secondary points, starting with the one that answers the least important reader question | list what went in "what the reader loses" |
| C6 | short sentences joined where they share a subject | do not build sentences over 25 words |

## Protected items

The items the user names; when the user names nothing, every number, name and date. They are never shortened ("8 am" stays "8 am", not "8"), paraphrased or dropped. A number keeps its qualifier and unit word for word ("more than 15" never becomes "15+"; no "~", no rounding, no unit swaps) unless the user approves. Other locked items keep their value and wording but may leave whole in C5, logged and listed under "what the reader loses".

## Impossible targets

Count the words the protected items need on their own. If that count, or that count plus the fewest joining words that still make a sentence, is above the target, the target is impossible. Say so, give the shortest version that keeps them all, and print its count.

## What the reader loses

One or two lines: the points, examples or nuance removed in C4 and C5. Write "nothing factual" only when no cut went beyond C3.

## Reading-time targets

Target words = minutes × the user's words per minute, rounded down. State the speed used.
