# Angle matrix method

An angle is a reason to buy, stated from one kind of buyer's side, that can be traced to something a real customer said. The matrix keeps angles distinct and keeps invented ones apart.

## Evidence ids

- Number every pasted item: E1, E2… (reviews, ad comments, call or support notes, survey answers). Record the kind and date if given.
- Replace any name, email or handle with the id. Never repeat personal details, even in quotes.
- Quote at most one short phrase per evidence item; otherwise paraphrase.

## Matrix columns

| Angle | Segment | Motivation (gain, relief, fear of loss, identity) | Awareness level | Objection it answers | Proof available (F-id) | Evidence (E-ids) | One-sentence message | Distinct from |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Awareness level uses the five awareness stages named by Eugene Schwartz in *Breakthrough Advertising* (1966), from a buyer who does not yet see the problem, through one who knows the problem, then one who knows solutions exist, then one who knows this product, to one ready to buy. The level decides how much the opening line must explain.

## Steps

1. Tag each evidence item with the problem, the outcome wanted, the trigger moment and any objection.
2. Group items by those tags. A group with at least two evidence items from different sources becomes an angle; a group with one becomes "thin — one source".
3. **Near-duplicate merge:** two angles merge if a reader would summarise them in the same sentence or they share the same motivation and outcome. Keep the better-evidenced wording; list the merged ids.
4. **Coverage gaps:** list segments or objections that appear in the evidence but no angle answers.
5. **Hypotheses — no evidence:** angles the user or the model suggests without any E-id go in a separate list, never in the matrix.
6. Rank angles by evidence count and spread of sources, not by taste; print the counts.

## Pasted competitor ads (optional, paste-only)

Ad libraries are not fetched: facebook.com's robots.txt disallows all automated agents (read 2026-10-03), and the other libraries are script-driven apps. The user pastes ad text and any dates the library showed.

| Ad | Concept | Offer | Proof type | Format | Call to action | First seen (as pasted) | Variants seen |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- Run length and variant count are weak signals: libraries show no spend or results for most commercial ads.
- **Claims you cannot reuse:** the competitor's ratings, awards, customer counts, rankings, test results and named endorsers belong to them. List them so none drift into the user's copy.
- No ranking of competitors and no positioning document; the output is a tally plus the list above.
