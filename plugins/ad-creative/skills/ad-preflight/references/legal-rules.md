# Claim rules by jurisdiction (US, UK, EU)

Paraphrased in our own words, with the page and the date read. Not legal advice: the output names the rule and what proof would answer it, and leaves the decision to the user and their adviser. Rows marked **unverified** could not be read on the official text on the read date; they are shown as "unverified — read at the link before relying on it" and never decide a verdict on their own.

## United States (FTC)

| Id | Rule (our wording) | Source | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| US-FTC-SUB | Policy Statement on Advertising Substantiation (1984). An objective claim needs a reasonable basis before the ad runs. If the ad says or implies a level of support ("tested", "clinically shown", "doctors recommend"), the advertiser must hold that level of support. | ftc.gov/public-statements/1984/11/ftc-policy-statement-regarding-advertising-substantiation (redirects to the current page) | 2026-10-03 |
| US-FTC-END | Endorsement Guides (2023 revision; FAQ "What People Are Asking"). A connection between endorser and brand that viewers would not expect (payment, free product, employment, family) is disclosed clearly; in video the disclosure sits in the video itself, ideally both on screen and spoken; a disclosure only in the caption, a link or a comment is easy to miss and unlikely to be enough. An endorser's unusual result needs either proof that it is typical or a clear statement of what people generally get. | ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides | 2026-10-03 |
| US-FTC-RV | Trade Regulation Rule on Consumer Reviews and Testimonials, in force from 2024-10-21. Bans fake or false reviews and testimonials (including AI-made personas posing as customers), undisclosed insider reviews, incentives tied to positive sentiment, suppression of negative reviews by threats or false claims, and fake follower or view counts. | ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers (page dated November 2024) | 2026-10-03 |

## United Kingdom (CAP Code, section 3, "Misleading advertising")

Source for every row: asa.org.uk/type/non_broadcast/code_section/03.html, read 2026-10-03 (page showed 24 September 2026).

| Id | Rule (our wording) | Read |
|---|---|---|
| UK-CAP-3.1 | No ad may mislead, and none may be likely to. | 2026-10-03 |
| UK-CAP-3.3 | Leaving out, hiding or blurring material information is misleading. | 2026-10-03 |
| UK-CAP-3.7 | Hold documentary evidence for objective claims before the ad goes out. | 2026-10-03 |
| UK-CAP-3.9 / 3.10 | Significant limits and qualifications must be stated clearly and must not contradict the headline claim. | 2026-10-03 |
| UK-CAP-3.11 | No exaggerating what a product can do or how well it performs. | 2026-10-03 |
| UK-CAP-3.17 to 3.22 | Price statements must not mislead; quoted prices include unavoidable taxes and duties; delivery charges stated; linked-purchase conditions stated; "from" and "up to" must not overstate how often the benefit is available. | 2026-10-03 |
| UK-CAP-3.23 to 3.26 | "Free" only if the person pays nothing beyond the unavoidable cost of responding and delivery; conditions of a free offer stated; a money-back offer is not a "free trial". | 2026-10-03 |
| UK-CAP-3.27 to 3.31 | Estimate demand reasonably and disclose likely shortages; no switch-selling; no false claim that a product or terms are available only for a very short time (3.30); no false market information. | 2026-10-03 |
| UK-CAP-3.33 / 3.34 | Comparisons cover products meeting the same need and compare material, relevant, verifiable features. An unqualified superlative ("the best") is read as a comparison with all competitors and needs evidence unless it is obvious puffery (principle stated before rule 3.32). | 2026-10-03 |
| UK-CAP-3.39 | A price comparison must not claim a false saving; a reference price far from the usual selling price is likely to mislead. | 2026-10-03 |
| UK-CAP-3.44 to 3.50 | No fake consumer reviews; incentivised reviews marked as such; reviews not filtered or ordered misleadingly; documentary evidence that testimonials are genuine; testimonials about the product advertised, with permission, and their factual claims must not mislead. | 2026-10-03 |
