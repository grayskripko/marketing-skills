# Common beliefs, checked

| Belief | What the checked pages or the maths say | Source, read 2026-10-03 |
|---|---|---|
| "A single exclamation mark gets a search ad rejected." | The punctuation page bans the same mark repeated in a row and gimmicky symbols; a single "!" is not addressed. | support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/14847994 |
| "Emoji are fine in search ads." | Emoji fall under unsupported characters, which are not allowed. | same page |
| "The ad needs Excellent Ad Strength to run." | Ad Strength does not directly decide serving eligibility and is not part of Ad Rank. | support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9921843 |
| "Pin every headline to control the message." | Pinning is possible but the page advises against it for most advertisers; it cuts the combinations tested. | support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7684791 |
| "Never use the word 'you' in social ads." | The personal-attributes rule is about stating or implying what the viewer is (health, age, money trouble…), not about the pronoun. "You can start tonight" is fine; "your diabetes" is not. | transparency.meta.com personal-attributes page |
| "The credit category covers finance ads." | Since 2025-01-14 the US special category is "financial products and services", which is wider than credit. | developers.facebook.com special-ad-category docs |
| "LinkedIn intro text stops at 600 characters." | The page gives 3,000 as the maximum and 150 as the length before truncation. | linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a426534 |
| "1,000 impressions is enough to call a winner." | Whether a gap is real depends on the rates and the gap size. At a 2% CTR, telling 2.0% from 2.4% needs about 21,109 impressions per ad at the usual 95% / 80% settings. | test-stats.md |
| "Higher CTR means the better ad." | CTR can rise while buyers per impression stay flat or fall; read the declared primary metric. | test-stats.md |
| "Ads in the same ad set are an A/B test." | Delivery picks who sees each ad, so the comparison is observational. | test-stats.md |
| "'Eco-friendly' is harmless wording in the EU." | From 2026-09-27 a generic environmental claim without recognised excellent environmental performance is banned outright (Annex I point 4a). | European Commission Q&A on Directive (EU) 2024/825, September 2026 |
| "'#1' is harmless puffery." | The CAP Code reads an unqualified superlative as a comparison needing evidence unless it is obvious puffery; the FTC asks for a reasonable basis; TikTok bars unverifiable absolute rankings. | legal-rules.md UK-CAP-3.33 / 3.34, US-FTC-SUB; policy-rules.md TT-MIS-RANK |
| "Offsets let us call the product carbon neutral in the EU." | From 2026-09-27, offset-based neutral, reduced or positive impact claims about a product are banned outright (Annex I point 4c). | European Commission Q&A on Directive (EU) 2024/825, September 2026 |
| "A disclosure link in the caption covers a paid creator video." | The FTC's FAQ says caption-only or link-only disclosures are easy to miss; disclose inside the video. | legal-rules.md US-FTC-END |
| "Google won't change my headlines." | Text customization (formerly automatically created assets) and Final URL expansion can generate text from the landing page. | support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14528373 |
| "TikTok captions can be 100 characters." | No maximum is stated on TikTok's in-feed spec page; the length is unknown. | ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads |
