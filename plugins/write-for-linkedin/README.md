# Write for LinkedIn®

Rewrite your LinkedIn® profile, draft a post in your voice, or help reply to a recruiter or contact. Works from the facts and text you supply.

Independent project; not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn. Trademarks mentioned belong to their respective owners. LinkedIn is a registered trademark or trademark of LinkedIn Corporation and its affiliates in the United States and/or other countries.

For professionals, job seekers and small business teams who want drafts they can review and paste themselves.
Needs pasted text or an existing export. No account connection, API key, paid service or extra install is required.

## What you get

| Skill | Give it | You get |
|---|---|---|
| linkedin-profile | profile sections, career facts and target reader or role | a headline, About or experience rewrite |
| linkedin-post | topic notes or a draft; your own writing samples if available | a post in your voice, with your facts |
| linkedin-message | recipient context, your purpose or a pasted conversation | a connection note, outreach message or reply |
| linkedin-brief | person or company excerpts, posts or exported rows | a meeting or writing brief with source pointers |

## Try it

- “Rewrite my headline: project coordinator at Bramble Works. I manage only retail fit-outs. Target role: project manager.”
- “Draft a plain post: at Bramble Works we added a handoff checklist. In one pilot only, weekly missed handoffs fell from 10 to 6. Include the percentage reduction.”
- “Reply to this: ‘Please don't contact me again.’ Give me a short acknowledgment.”
- “Brief me for a meeting: company excerpt says Bramble Works does only retail fit-outs. Profile excerpt from May 2026 says the operations lead coordinates three sites.”

## How to supply material

Paste just the relevant sections, or attach an existing text, CSV, PDF or other export your assistant can read. Label excerpts with their source and date when known. For voice matching, add a few examples of your own writing; no fixed sample count is required. If the assistant cannot read a file, paste the relevant text.

No setup path: use pasted text. If you already have an export, the agent can help identify the needed sections or columns and remove unrelated data. Export availability and menus can differ; this package does not log in or retrieve one. Use material you have the right to share. Never paste passwords, cookies, API keys or login codes. Redact contact details and private information first.

The agent preserves your numbers, roles, claims and scope words. A target role stays a goal, not an achieved title. Missing facts get a short marker or a question. Derived figures include their formula and inputs after the draft.

## Limits and risks

Drafts cannot establish whether an old profile is current or whether a claim is true outside your supplied material. Review accuracy, permissions and private details before using the result. There are no guarantees of reach, replies, hiring or sales. This package does not scrape, access inboxes, send, publish, edit an account or set up automation. It does not help with spam, false familiarity or fake engagement.


## Data and network

Network scope: none. No URLs, robots.txt, search results, profiles or services are fetched. Source links are reading labels, not runtime fetch instructions. No telemetry. No accounts or paid APIs are required. The package ships instructions and reference text, with no service or executable integration. Your assistant processes pasted text and supplied files under its own terms. The package stores nothing; an assistant may count text locally, and save a local deliverable only if you ask. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

## Troubleshooting

Only a profile URL available: paste the visible text you are allowed to share. An old or conflicting source: include its date and any newer excerpt; otherwise the brief keeps the uncertainty. Want a message sent or a profile updated: review the draft and apply it yourself in your own account.
