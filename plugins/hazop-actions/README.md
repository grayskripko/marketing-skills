# HAZOP Actions

Reconcile HAZOP and PHA recommendations with your action tracker and close-out records. For PHA coordinators, process safety engineers and HAZOP scribes at operating plants and consultancies. Paste the worksheet or recommendation list, the tracker rows and the closure records into your message; no account or connector is needed.

| Skill | What you get |
|---|---|
| hazop-action-reconcile | Recommendations with no action, actions with no recommendation, wording changed from the approved worksheet, trackers built from an old revision, missing owners and dates, overdue items, with counts that add up |
| hazop-closure-check | Closed, declined and transferred actions checked against the MOC, work order, drawing or procedure revision they cite: supported, stated only, record not finished, mismatch, date problem, declined without a written reason |
| hazop-revalidation-carryover | The five-year revalidation due date with its arithmetic, and the open recommendations from the last study that are missing from the new one |

Try: "Which HAZOP recommendations are missing from this action list?", "Are these PHA actions really closed?", "When is our revalidation due and what do we carry over?"

## What it knows

Matching rules for node, deviation and recommendation numbers, including split and merged actions and renumbering after approval. The OSHA PSM and EPA RMP rules that change a status: the written schedule and documented resolution in 1910.119(e)(5) and 40 CFR 68.67(e); life-of-process retention; recommendations resolved before startup of a new facility; and OSHA's enforcement answers on the one-to-two-year expectation, turnaround items, declining a recommendation in writing, siting-study logs, and the five-year clock that a shutdown does not stop. Also how a HAZOP software export, a site's transfer-to-MOC closure rule and IChemE's Delta HAZOP guidance change the reading of a record. Dated sources: [references](references/official-sources.md).

## Limits

They do not run or revalidate a HAZOP, add hazards or safeguards, rank risk, rewrite recommendations or close actions. A closure record shows the paperwork, not the field condition.
