# Twitter Posts

Write X posts, threads and replies, or check what supplied posts say about a topic or account over the dates you choose.

Pasted text or supplied CSV/JSON exports need no account, key, installation or paid API. Source links are citations and are not fetched.

| Skill | Give it | You get |
|---|---|---|
| twitter-post-draft | Facts, a draft and optional voice examples | A post, thread or reply ready to review |
| twitter-topic-search | Supplied posts, topic and optional dates | Findings with quotes and source references |
| twitter-account-research | An account export and selected dates | Dated statements and supported comparisons |
| twitter-thread-read | Pasted post or thread | Summary of its argument, replies and gaps |

## Try it

- “Turn these facts into a short post; keep ‘only’ and ‘might’.”
- “Find mentions of export errors in this supplied CSV during September.”
- “Compare this account's supplied statements before and after launch.”
- “Explain the disagreement in this pasted thread.”

## Data and network

Network scope: none. No URLs, robots.txt, search providers, post services or APIs are fetched. The package makes no account connection and does not post or schedule. It adds no telemetry or storage service. Your assistant processes supplied material under its own terms; redact unnecessary private details. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

## Limits and troubleshooting

A link alone is not readable post evidence: provide the relevant permitted text. Exports can be partial. Missing dates, quoted posts and attachments remain unknown; counts cover only supplied material that meets your filters. A post shows what its author said. It does not prove the claim is true. Drafts invent no personal experience, source claims or engagement promises. This release cannot search live posts or fetch account history.

Documentation links show where pages are intended to be published. They do not prove those pages are online. Use the bundled README and privacy notice.
