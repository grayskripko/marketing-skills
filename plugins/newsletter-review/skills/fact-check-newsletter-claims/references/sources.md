## Answer shape

Lead with the artifact the user requested. Apply the evidence checks within it; these notes do not require extra sections, a checklist or a fixed report format.

For a numerical correction, keep the supplied counts with requested percentages and leave calculations in the requested explanation. With 11 and 7 responses out of 24 and unknown overlap, the exact union is unknown; its bounds are 11/24 to 18/24 (45.8%–75%). These are respondent shares, not shares of all invited workshops.

For a corrected sponsor paragraph followed only by decisions, preserve the exact attributed quote, eligibility, delivery cost and one-off purchase terms. Mark a conflicting approved price and ask which applies. Delete unsupported time-saving and deadline claims; do not add sentences about absent measurements or an unstated deadline when the remaining copy makes neither promise.
