# Sales Enablement Kit

Get sellers ready, with a quoted line behind every score: "We lose half a day to it" (06:10), never sized → CS-03 = 0.

## What it does

| Skill | Give it | You get |
|---|---|---|
| call-coaching | 1 to 5 transcripts | quoted rubric rows, talk and question counts, a drill |
| rep-certification | product or pricing notes | a source-keyed quiz; a role-play buyer who hides facts |
| demo-runbook | discovery notes, capabilities, slot | one scene per pain, check-ins, PASS/FIX |
| question-bank | 3+ conversations | answers per buyer question, proof status |
| ramp-plan | role, sales cycle, coach hours | weeks, gates, coach hours needed vs available |

## Examples

- "30-min demo for a VP Support. Pains: first reply takes 9 hours, no weekend cover. We have AI triage, shared inbox, reports."
- "Make a 10-question rep quiz with answer key from these rules: Pro $40/seat, min 10 seats, annual only, max discount 15%."
- "Role-play a skeptical CFO at a 200-person logistics firm. I sell route-planning software. Score me when I say stop."

## How it works

One 0–2 rubric for coaching, certification and ramp gates (11 rows, maximum 22, pass at 16). Every number shows its rule. No benchmarks; missing proof shows as [PROOF NEEDED]. Tuned for English.

## Data and network

Network scope: this plugin fetches nothing and runs no web search. It reads only what you paste or attach, sends nothing, changes no files or settings unless you ask, and may use the assistant's code tool to count words, questions and shares; without one it counts by hand and says so.

## Personal data

Read only, for a seller's own practice and coaching. Transcripts hold buyers' words too; strip names first if you can. The skills use labels such as Seller-1 and never repeat emails or phone numbers. Recording calls needs whatever notice or consent your jurisdiction requires. See PRIVACY.md.

## What it will not do

Rank, compare or monitor sellers, or feed scores into hiring, pay or dismissal (EU AI Act, Annex III 4(b)); you get a coaching plan instead. Teach false deadlines; play a real person; fetch pages; write decks or outreach.

## Troubleshooting

"Not computed": speaker labels missing. "Source gap": add the answer and rerun.
