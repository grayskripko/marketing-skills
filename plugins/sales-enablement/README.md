# Sales Enablement Kit

Coach a sales call, quiz reps on your own pricing, rehearse with a practice buyer, plan a demo around what the buyer said, and see how long a new seller needs before selling alone. Built only from your calls and your material; every score points to the line in the call that earned it.

## What it does

| Skill | Give it | You get |
|---|---|---|
| call-coaching | 1 to 5 transcripts | what to say differently, a score with quotes if asked |
| rep-certification | product or pricing notes | a quiz with answer key; a role-play buyer who holds facts back |
| demo-runbook | discovery notes, capabilities, slot | a run of show: one scene per pain, what not to show, a time check |
| question-bank | 3+ conversations | the buyer questions your team answers differently or without proof, with one proposed answer each |
| ramp-plan | role, sales cycle, coach hours | weeks to selling alone; coach hours needed vs available |

## Examples

- "30-min demo for a VP Support. Pains: first reply takes 9 hours, no weekend cover. We have AI triage, shared inbox, reports."
- "Make a 10-question rep quiz with answer key from these rules: Pro $40/seat, min 10 seats, annual only, max discount 15%."
- "Role-play a skeptical CFO at a 200-person logistics firm. I sell route-planning software. Score me when I say stop."

## How it works

Scores, when you ask for them, are backed by quotes from the call and use your rubric and pass rule if you give one. Every computed number shows how it was counted. No outside benchmarks, and no defaults filled in where your own numbers are missing: it asks instead. Real product and customer claims use your supplied material; missing proof is flagged. Practice scenarios may contain clearly labelled fictional details. Tuned for English.

## Data and network

Network scope: this plugin fetches nothing and runs no web search. It reads only what you paste or attach, sends nothing, changes no files or settings unless you ask, and may use the assistant's code tool to count words, questions and shares; without one it counts by hand and says so.

## Personal data

Read only, for a seller's own practice and coaching. Transcripts hold buyers' words too; strip names first if you can. The skills use labels such as Seller-1 and never repeat emails or phone numbers. Recording calls needs whatever notice or consent your jurisdiction requires. See PRIVACY.md.

## What it will not do

Decide hiring, pay or dismissal from scores (EU AI Act, Annex III 4(b)); it describes what the scores show and leaves the decision to people. Teach false deadlines; play a real person; fetch pages; write decks or outreach.

## Troubleshooting

"Not computed": speaker labels missing. "Source gap": add the answer and rerun.
