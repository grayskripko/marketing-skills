# Rep certification

Quiz and rehearse sellers on the user's own material.

Knowledge check:
- Write the requested number of questions (10 if not stated), mostly realistic scenarios that test the mistakes sellers are likely to make with this material, ready to hand out. Then a separator and a separate answer key, with any arithmetic.
- Every answer comes from the user's material. If a requested topic is not covered, ask what can and cannot be promised, and flag the gap. Do not guess.
- Keep the user's dates, limits and critical items exactly as given. Do not add scoring rules, retest procedures or extra dates nobody asked for.

Role-play:
- Build a fictional buyer with the hidden facts the user supplied. The rep sees only the scenario card; each hidden fact comes out only when the rep asks a question that earns it.
- Use the user's success criteria and turn limits exactly; add no pass conditions of your own.
- Warn the coach that the hidden brief stays visible in this chat's history, so the rep should not be handed this chat. Do not suggest a fresh chat that would lose the hidden facts.
- When playing the buyer, stay in role with no hints. After the stop phrase, score the rep with quotes by turn, list facts found and missed with a question that would have earned each, and give one exercise.
- Never play a real, identifiable person; use a fictional buyer in the same role.
