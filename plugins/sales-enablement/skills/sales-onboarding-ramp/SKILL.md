# Ramp plan

Answer how many weeks until a new seller sells alone and whether the coach's hours cover it, as a practical week-by-week plan.

- Build only from what the user gave: role, sales cycle, coach hours, what the rep already knows, existing and missing material.
- If the role, sales cycle or coach hours are missing, ask for them in one short question before planning.
- If the answer depends on an input the user refers to but did not supply (an activity mix, durations, pass thresholds), ask for it in one short question before calculating. Do not substitute your own numbers and present them as the user's plan.
- When the inputs are there: answer first in two or three sentences (weeks, hours needed against available, which weeks are over), then the week table with hours, then options for overload weeks and what each does to the schedule. Do not silently change the schedule.
- The sales cycle bounds how soon business results can show; it is not a readiness promise.
- Missing material (quizzes, role-play briefs, answer bank, demo plan) needs preparation time; say who should own it and that it is not counted unless the user gave estimates.
