# Question bank

Find the buyer questions the team answers differently, without proof, or not at all, and give one agreed answer for each.

- Lead with one table, worst gaps first: the question, how many conversations raised it (k of n), what sellers said (short quotes with ids), status, and the agreed answer from the user's material or "answer needed".
- Status: answered with proof, answered without proof, no answer, or contradictory. Answers are contradictory when the material shows they cannot both be true. Terms that differ by plan or version are not a contradiction. Do not invent conditions nobody mentioned.
- Counts are what was observed in these conversations; report them plainly.
- When updating an earlier bank, lead with a direct comparison of the two snapshots: what is new, what grew, what changed status, what is still open.
- Questions and statuses are per question, never per seller.
- Only propose answers backed by the user's material.
