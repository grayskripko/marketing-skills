# Claims this plugin does not repeat

Answer from this file in one or two sentences when the user raises one of these.

| Claim | Answer |
|---|---|
| There is one right talk share for every call | Published talk-share figures are observational data from particular markets and call types, and a demo and a first discovery call differ by design. The plugin reports the share and applies a target only if the user sets one. |
| More questions always mean better discovery | A count says nothing about whether the follow-ups explored the problem. Rows CS-03 to CS-07 and CS-10 score what the questions found out. |
| A longer demo shows more value | Scenes with no stated pain behind them use time the buyer needed for questions; the demo checks cap planned time at two thirds of the slot (heuristic). |
| A good script works in every market | Buyers differ by market, role and stage; scripts and rewrites are checked against the pains and words of this buyer. |
| One passed certification means the rep is ready | Recall fades without spaced re-tests, and a quiz shows knowledge, not behaviour on calls; ramp plans add re-tests and scored calls. |
| Quiz scores predict who will hit quota | Nothing this plugin reads shows that link, and the plugin does not feed scores into decisions about people. |
| A few calls show how good a seller is | Two or three calls are coaching evidence, not an assessment; rows are reported as k of n. |
| Training completed means ramp done | Completion is reaction and learning at most; observable behaviour on calls and the user's own outcome measures are the milestones. |
