# Privacy

Sales Enablement Kit is a set of instructions and reference text for an AI assistant. It has no server, no account and no storage of its own.

- Network scope: this plugin fetches nothing and runs no web search. It reads only what you paste or attach, sends nothing, changes no files or settings unless you ask, and may use the assistant's code tool to count words, questions and shares; without one it counts by hand and says so.
- Personal data: read only. Transcripts and notes you choose to paste contain personal data of sellers and buyers; the plugin does not store, send or change it.
- Categories of data: whatever you paste. Typically call transcripts, call notes, email threads and chat logs, which hold sellers' names and words and also the names, job titles, employers and words of buyers and other people on the call who are not you (third parties); deal details; and your own product, pricing and messaging material. The skills do not need contact details; emails and phone numbers are never repeated.
- Purpose: producing the coaching, quiz, role-play, demo plan, question bank or ramp plan you ask for, during that conversation.
- Use limits: scores and certification results are practice feedback for the person coached, not input to hiring, pay, promotion, ratings, discipline or dismissal; the skills decline those uses and do not rank, compare or monitor sellers.
- Recipients: the data goes to the provider of the AI assistant you use, under that provider's terms. The plugin sends it nowhere else.
- Retention: the plugin keeps nothing. Retention of your conversation is set by your assistant provider.
- Your controls: replace names with roles before pasting (the skills use labels such as Seller-1 and Buyer – finance lead by default, replace names inside quotes and print a name key only when you ask); paste only the calls you need; recording and reviewing calls needs whatever notice or consent your jurisdiction requires.
