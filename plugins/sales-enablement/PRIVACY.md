# Privacy

Sales Enablement & Role-Play is a set of instructions and reference text for an AI assistant. It has no server, no account and no storage of its own.

- Network scope: this plugin fetches nothing and runs no web search. It reads only what you paste or attach, sends nothing, changes no files or settings unless you ask.
- Personal data: read only. Transcripts and notes you choose to paste contain personal data of sellers and buyers; the plugin does not store, send or change it.
- Categories of data: whatever you paste. Typically call transcripts, call notes, email threads and chat logs, which hold sellers' names and words and also the names, job titles, employers and words of buyers and other people on the call who are not you (third parties); deal details; and your own product, pricing and messaging material. The skills do not need contact details; remove emails and phone numbers before pasting.
- Purpose: producing the coaching, quiz, role-play, demo plan, question bank or ramp plan you ask for, during that conversation.
- Use limits: scores and quiz or role-play results are practice feedback for the person coached, not input to hiring, pay, promotion, ratings, discipline or dismissal; for those uses the skills say the scores are not a basis, leave the decision to people and describe each seller's scores separately, without ranking or comparing them.
- Recipients: the data goes to the provider of the AI assistant you use, under that provider's terms. The plugin sends it nowhere else.
- Retention: the plugin keeps nothing. Retention of your conversation is set by your assistant provider.
- Your controls: replace names with roles before pasting (or ask the assistant to use labels such as Seller-1 and Buyer – finance lead); paste only the calls you need; recording and reviewing calls needs whatever notice or consent your jurisdiction requires.
