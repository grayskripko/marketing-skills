# Lead Magnets Kit

Plan, check and measure lead magnets with dated US, EU and UK consent rules. A readout:

| Magnet | Sign-ups / visits | Calls / visits |
|---|---|---|
| Checklist | 312/2,400 = 13.0% (11.7–14.4) | 9/2,400 = 0.4% (0.2–0.7) |
| Calculator | 81/900 = 9.0% (7.3–11.0) | 14/900 = 1.6% (0.9–2.6) |

The checklist grows the list; the calculator brings 4.1 times more calls per visit. Call counts are under 20, so the verdict is provisional.

## What it does

| Skill | You get |
|---|---|
| magnet-plan | scored pick for your offer, gate decision, fields, consent box text |
| magnet-build | checks, then the asset from your notes |
| optin-check | 20 dated checks on page, form and emails; rewrites |
| magnet-followup | who may receive what, consent records, export audit |
| magnet-readout | rates with intervals, a verdict per magnet |

## Examples

- "We sell invoicing software to agencies; buyers compare tools. Pick a lead magnet and say whether to gate it."
- "Check our opt-in form: email, phone, company size and a pre-ticked 'send me offers' box. Visitors from UK and EU."
- "Checklist: 2,400 visits, 312 sign-ups, 9 sales calls. Calculator: 900 visits, 81 sign-ups, 14 calls. Which works better?"

## How it works

Legal rows carry a source and read date, are flagged after six months, and are checks, not legal advice. Figures come only from you; no industry rates, no invented proof.

## Data and network

Network scope: only optin-check fetches anything: the public URL of your own opt-in page, plus at most one thank-you page you name, after checking that site's robots.txt; it never submits a form, logs in or follows links. No skill searches the web or calls another service, and nothing is sent, changed or stored unless you ask; your assistant's code tool may compute the tables shown.

Personal data, read only: exports may hold names and emails; the skills ignore them. Remove those columns first. See PRIVACY.md.

## What it will not do

Buy or scrape lists, pre-tick consent, tie EU or UK downloads to marketing consent, invent proof or deadlines, write email series, size A/B tests.

## Troubleshooting

"Not checked" and "ask" rows name what is missing. "Few events": under 20.
