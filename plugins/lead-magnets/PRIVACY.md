# Privacy: Lead Magnets

Lead Magnets consists of instructions and reference text for an AI assistant. It has no server, no account and no storage of its own.

- Network scope: the skills fetch nothing. Paste the page, form, thank-you page or email text you want checked. No skill searches the web or calls another service, and nothing is sent, changed or stored unless you ask.
- Personal data: read only. Sign-up exports you choose to paste may contain personal data; the plugin does not store, send or change it.
- Categories of data: whatever you paste or attach, typically page text, form fields, sign-up and visit counts, and sign-up exports with record ids, dates, regions, sources and consent fields. Exports may also contain names, email addresses and company names; the skills do not need these, ignore them and never repeat them; a record id that holds an email address, name or phone number is shown as its row number instead.
- Purpose: answering your request during that conversation.
- Recipients: the data goes to the provider of the AI assistant you use, under that provider's terms. The plugin sends it nowhere else.
- Retention: the plugin keeps nothing. Retention of your conversation is set by your assistant provider.
- Your controls: remove contact columns before pasting; record ids are enough for every skill.
