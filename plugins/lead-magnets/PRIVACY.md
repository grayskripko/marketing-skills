# Privacy

Lead Magnets Kit consists of instructions and reference text for an AI assistant. It has no server, no account and no storage of its own.

- Network scope: only optin-check fetches anything: the public URL of your own opt-in page, plus at most one thank-you page you name, after checking that site's robots.txt; it never submits a form, logs in or follows links. No skill searches the web or calls another service, and nothing is sent, changed or stored unless you ask; your assistant's code tool may compute the tables shown.
- Personal data: read only. Sign-up exports you choose to paste may contain personal data; the plugin does not store, send or change it.
- Categories of data: whatever you paste or attach, typically page text, form fields, sign-up and visit counts, and sign-up exports with record ids, dates, regions, sources and consent fields. Exports may also contain names, email addresses and company names; the skills do not need these, ignore them and never repeat them.
- Purpose: answering your request during that conversation.
- Recipients: the data goes to the provider of the AI assistant you use, under that provider's terms. The plugin sends it nowhere else.
- Retention: the plugin keeps nothing. Retention of your conversation is set by your assistant provider.
- Your controls: remove contact columns before pasting; record ids are enough for every skill.
