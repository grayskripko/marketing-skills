# Opt-in check

Open with the verdict (can it go live, and the most serious problem), then the corrected lines ready to paste, then one line per problem saying why.

- Fix only what is wrong. Keep the design, the valid copy and the user's wording. Do not add new boxes, opt-ins, copy, launch requirements or checklists beyond the fix.
- Use the real figures the user verified (counts, dates). A claim the user cannot back is removed, not reworded.
- What to look for: a pre-ticked or bundled marketing box is not consent in the EU and UK (GDPR Recital 32; CJEU Planet49, 2019); EU and UK individuals should get the download without agreeing to marketing; each field needs a use before the next step; counts and testimonials must be real; conditions of a "free" offer sit beside it; the page says how and when the asset arrives.
- Confirmation email: judge it by its main purpose. If promotion is removed and it only delivers what was asked for, say that commercial-email duties may not apply; if that is unclear, leave it as an open question, not a failure.
- Facts the user has not given (sender settings, mail volume) are unknowns, not failures. At most three questions at the end.
