# Consent register

These rows are checks, not legal advice. Each row gives the rule in this plugin's own words, the source, the date it was read, and what to check. A row read more than 6 months before today prints "re-check this rule". "Unverified" means the primary text could not be read on the date shown; confirm it before relying on the row. No penalty amounts are given: they change and no design choice depends on them.

| CR-01 | EU | Consent must be freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous, shown by a statement or a clear act. Silence, inactivity and pre-ticked boxes are not consent. In France, the CNIL asks for an affirmative act such as ticking an empty box; accepting general terms is not enough. | GDPR Art 4(11) and Recital 32, gdpr-info.eu; CJEU C-673/17 Planet49, judgment of 2019-10-01, publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/62017CJ0673; CNIL "La prospection commerciale par courrier électronique", dated 2026-06-10, cnil.fr | 2026-10-03 | unticked box, active choice |

Plugin default for EU and UK individuals (cautious reading, used as the nearest analogue): the asset is delivered without any marketing consent. | GDPR Art 7(4), Recital 43; EDPB Guidelines 05/2020 on consent v1.1 (adopted 2020-05-04), paras 26, 35 to 37, 39 to 41 (Example 6a in para 40), edpb.europa.eu/system/files/documents/files/file1/edpb_guidelines_202005_consent_en.pdf | 2026-10-03 | download not tied to the box |

| CR-09 | US | CAN-SPAM covers every commercial email, business-to-business included. ## Default decided for this plugin

For EU and UK individuals the asset is delivered to everyone who asks for it, and marketing is a separate unticked optional box, one per stream. This is a cautious reading of CR-02 and CR-03, with the cookie-wall passage cited as the nearest analogue. The output may explain the cost (fewer marketing opt-ins) but never designs a download that depends on the marketing tick for these readers.
