# Magnet follow-up

Open with who gets what, one row per group the user named, and where a person takes over.

- The user's own form and team rules come first. Keep the user's deadlines and limits word for word: a stated response time is a requirement, not a target.
- Rules to apply (checks, not legal advice): useful tips that promote the company are marketing. EU individuals need consent; a download alone gives delivery only. UK individuals and sole traders: the soft opt-in needs a past sale or negotiations for a similar product, with an opt-out offered when the details were collected; a download alone is not enough. <!-- ICO PECR electronic mail marketing, https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guide-to-pecr/electronic-and-telephone-marketing/electronic-mail-marketing/ checked 2026-10-15 --> UK corporate subscribers may be emailed without consent, with sender identity and an opt-out; named staff addresses are still personal data. US: CAN-SPAM is opt-out, not a bar: commercial email, B2B included, may go without prior consent if it carries the sender's postal address and a working opt-out, honoured within 10 business days. <!-- FTC CAN-SPAM compliance guide, https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business checked 2026-10-15 --> Bulk senders (5,000+ a day to Gmail): SPF, DKIM and DMARC, one-click unsubscribe plus a visible link, unsubscribes honoured within 2 days, spam-complaint rate kept below 0.3%. <!-- Gmail https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126 and https://support.google.com/a/answer/14229414 (48 h); Yahoo https://senders.yahooinc.com/best-practices/ (2 days, <0.3%) checked 2026-10-15 -->
- Handover rules, each with who acts and what they do: opt-out requests and complaints are suppressed first, across all streams, then confirmed; replies and questions; demo or call requests (a named person answers within the user's stated time; it enrols no one in any stream).
- Export audit: count each defect and the distinct records affected, exactly. Keep record defects apart from open questions about consent scope. Never infer a permission the record does not show. Give the concrete fix for each defect type, and list the evidence to keep per record: form version and wording shown, timestamp, source, confirmation, and a change log of later edits. Never print an email address or name.
- No send schedules, tracking advice or sections the user did not ask for. At most three questions at the end.
