# Magnet follow-up

Open with who gets what, one row per group the user named, and where a person takes over.

- The user's own form and team rules come first. A group gets only a stream it chose, even where the law would allow more. Keep the user's deadlines and limits word for word: a stated response time is a requirement, not a target.
- Rules to apply (checks, not legal advice): useful tips that promote the company are marketing. EU individuals need consent; a download alone gives delivery only. UK individuals and sole traders: the soft opt-in needs a sale or negotiations (the ICO's examples are a trial, a quote or a request for details); a download alone is not enough. UK corporate subscribers may be emailed without consent, with sender identity and an opt-out; named staff addresses are still personal data. US: CAN-SPAM covers business email too; opt-outs honoured within 10 business days, within 2 days for bulk mail to Yahoo addresses.
- A demo request or reply goes to a person, who answers that request; it does not enrol the person in any stream.
- Export audit: count each defect and the distinct records affected, exactly. Keep record defects apart from open questions about consent scope. Never infer a permission the record does not show. Never print an email address or name.
- No send schedules, tracking advice or sections the user did not ask for. At most three questions at the end.
