# Effort sizes

Hours for one person who knows the subject, from first outline to a version ready to publish, excluding visual design and any build of a working tool. Heuristic of this plugin; the user's own hours replace these.

| Magnet | Hours | What drives the number |
|---|---|---|
| checklist, 10 to 20 items | 3 to 5 | turning the team's notes into "done when" criteria |
| template with one worked example row | 5 to 8 | finding an example the team may show |
| scorecard, 8 to 15 questions with bands | 6 to 10 | testing that two people score the same answer alike |
| calculator spec with three test cases | 8 to 12 | agreeing the formula and its defaults |
| sample or mini-audit format | 4 to 6 to design, plus the time per sign-up | delivery time per sign-up, multiplied by the expected sign-ups |
| workshop or webinar, one session | 6 to 12 including the session | the session and its follow-up |
| short course outline, 5 lessons | 6 to 10 | one clear job per lesson |
| guide outline plus first section | 6 to 10 | sourcing every claim |
| own-figures report | 16 or more | cleaning, anonymising and approving the figures |
| swipe file, 10 examples | 4 to 8 | permissions for each example |

S6 in magnet-plan: 2 when the upper bound fits the stated hours, 1 when only the lower bound fits, 0 when neither fits.
