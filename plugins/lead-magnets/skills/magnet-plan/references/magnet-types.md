# Magnet types

Ten types. Each row says when the type suits, the hard requirement that excludes it before scoring, the buyer stage it usually serves, and the capture mode it usually ends up with under magnet-plan's capture rule (the rule decides, not this column). No conversion figures are given for any type: the plugin ships none.

| Type | Suits when | Hard requirement (fail = excluded) | Usual stage | Usual capture mode |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| checklist | the reader repeats a multi-step task and fears skipping one | the steps come from the team's real process | comparing or deciding | open, or summary open |
| template | the reader has to fill in a document or spreadsheet and has nothing to start from | a template the team itself uses | comparing or deciding | summary open |
| scorecard / self-assessment | the reader wants to know where they stand | questions the team can score consistently, with 3 to 4 bands | noticing or comparing | use first, then save by email |
| calculator | the answer depends on figures only the reader has | a formula someone on the team can walk through step by step | comparing or deciding | use first, then save by email |
| sample or mini-audit | the offer is a service or data product and a slice of it on the reader's own case shows the value | the team can deliver it to every sign-up at the stated capacity | deciding | full gate (the reader sends their case) |
| workshop or live session | the reader needs hands-on help to set something up | a date, a capacity and a host | deciding | full gate with a seat limit stated truthfully |
| short course | the topic takes several steps spread over days | lessons the team already gives in this order | noticing | summary open |
| guide | the reader needs the whole process explained once | expert time and sources for every claim | noticing | open |
| own-figures report | the company holds aggregated figures nobody else has | the figures exist, are the company's own, and may be published | comparing | summary open or full gate |
| swipe file / example library | the reader learns by copying good examples | the examples are the team's own or used with permission | comparing | summary open |

Stage words used in this plugin: noticing (the reader has just named the problem), comparing (weighing ways to solve it), deciding (choosing, implementing or justifying the spend).
