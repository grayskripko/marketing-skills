# Magnet plan

Answer the question first (which magnet, gated or not), then give what the user can use straight away: what the asset contains, how it is captured, and the form text ready to paste.

- Work only from what the user gave. Do not invent scores, rating scales, effort hours, conversion rates, completion times or review thresholds. If the user gave an hour budget, show how the plan spends it, including building the page and testing delivery, and make it add up exactly.
- Options, formats and promises the user specified are taken as given; do not reject or reword them on your own estimates. Compare the realistic options in a few plain lines and say why the pick serves the stated goal. Drop an option only when the user's own facts show it cannot be delivered.
- Gate from the goal: reach, links or shares → open; contacts → gate, or open summary with the full version by email; both → summary open. If the goal is a call or demo, give the form a clear optional way to ask for one.
- Form fields: only what delivery or the promised next step needs; everything else comes off.
- Consent: answer any yes/no question the user asked directly first. EU and UK individuals get the asset straight away whether or not they tick a marketing box; each marketing purpose (newsletter, partner offers, sales contact) gets its own optional unticked box, never a combined one. Germany: double opt-in only for people who ticked a marketing box, before the first marketing email; store each consent so it can be produced later. <!-- BGH I ZR 164/09, 10 Feb 2011, Leitsaetze b) and d): advertiser must document each consent fully; after double opt-in confirmation the consumer bears the burden of showing no consent to email ads. https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/597212 checked 2026-10-15 --> Write all form copy entirely in the audience's language, not mixed. Downloading alone does not make someone a marketing subscriber. US: opt-out model; later commercial email needs no prior consent but must follow CAN-SPAM. Use the company and asset names the user gave.
- Traffic arithmetic stays exact and conditional on how many visitors actually reach the asset.
- No measurement plans, statistics or sections the user did not ask for. At most three questions at the end, only if something decisive is missing.
