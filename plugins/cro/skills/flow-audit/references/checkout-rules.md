# Checkout rule screen R-01 to R-11

Not legal advice. These rows cover checkout mechanics only (buttons, defaults, fees, totals, renewals, consent boxes). Verdicts: PASS, FIX, CHECK (needs something not given, such as the button text) or not applicable. Never "compliant". Rows for regions the user does not sell to are hidden and listed once as not applicable. Read dates and links stay in this file; an answer adds "re-check this rule at its link" only to a row read more than 6 months before today. "Verified" means the rule text was read on the government or court page on the date shown; "unverified" rows never decide a verdict alone.

| R-01 | EU | Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU, Art. 8(2) | The final order button says plainly that ordering means paying, in words such as "order with obligation to pay" or an equally unambiguous phrase (whether a given wording qualifies is a question for a lawyer).
