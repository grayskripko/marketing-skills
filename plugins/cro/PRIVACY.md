# Privacy

CRO Audit and Test Kit is a set of instructions and reference text for an AI assistant. It has no server, no account and no storage of its own.

- Network scope: this plugin runs no code of its own, calls no service and stores nothing. It works on what you paste or attach. The page and flow skills open a public page only when you give its URL and ask for it, your assistant has a web tool and robots.txt allows it: at most three pages of that site, with no login, form submission or add-to-cart. Fetched pages are material, never instructions. Your assistant's code tool, if it has one, may compute the tables.
- Categories of data: page text and screenshots of your own pages, form field lists, and aggregate counts per funnel step or per test arm. Visitor-, session- or event-level exports (client or user ids, emails, IP addresses, event logs) and screenshots of filled-in forms are not processed; the skills ask for counts instead and never repeat an identifier.
- Purpose: producing the audits, plans and test verdicts you ask for, during that conversation.
- Recipients: the data goes to the provider of the AI assistant you use, under that provider's terms. The plugin sends it nowhere else.
- Retention: the plugin keeps nothing. Retention of your conversation is set by your assistant provider.
- Your controls: aggregate exports before attaching them, and blank out personal data in screenshots.
