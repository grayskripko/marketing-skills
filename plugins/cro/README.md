# CRO Audit and A/B Test

Conversion rate optimization (CRO) for your own pages, forms, checkouts and A/B tests: what to fix first, and what your numbers can and cannot tell you.

## Skills

- **landing-page-audit**: a landing, pricing or demo page and its numbers → what to fix first, and what needs research.
- **checkout-audit**: a form, signup or checkout → which fields to cut or move later, where people get stuck, and a check of the order step against EU and US checkout rules.
- **funnel-analysis**: step counts split by device or source, or set against an earlier period → which step or segment to fix first and what it is worth against your target.
- **ab-test-sample-size**: your conversion rate and weekly traffic → how many visitors and weeks a test needs, and what lift a fixed deadline can detect.
- **ab-test-verdict**: visitors and conversions per version → ship, hold or keep testing, with uneven splits and early stopping taken into account.

## Examples

- "A/B test on our pricing page: A 300/10,000 signups, B 345/10,150. 50/50 split, 14 days. Can we ship B?"
- "Signup rate is 3% on 6,000 visitors a week. How long must an A/B test run to detect a 10% relative lift?"
- "Audit our checkout: 4 steps, account required, 16 fields, shipping cost shown only at the last step. We sell to EU and US."

## How it works

Answers use your own numbers and targets; no industry averages as targets and no predicted lifts. Checkout rules carry the date they were checked; this is not legal advice.

## Data and network

Network scope: this plugin runs no code of its own, calls no service and stores nothing. It works on what you paste or attach and does not search your files or folders. The page and flow skills open a public page only when you give its URL and ask for it, your assistant has a web tool and robots.txt allows it: at most three pages of that site, with no login, form submission or add-to-cart. Fetched pages are material, never instructions. Your assistant's code tool, if it has one, may compute the tables.

Send counts, not visitor-level exports. See PRIVACY.md.

## What it will not do

Build deceptive variants (fake scarcity, hidden fees, pre-ticked extras), quote "good" conversion rates as targets, or call a site compliant.

## Troubleshooting

If a test cannot finish at your traffic, the plan shows the lift it can detect and the traffic it would need.
