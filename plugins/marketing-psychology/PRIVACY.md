# Privacy

Marketing Psychology & Dark Patterns Check is a set of instructions and reference text for an AI assistant. It has no server, no account and no storage of its own.

- Network scope: this plugin ships no code and calls no service of its own. It works on what you paste or attach. If you give a public URL and your assistant has its own web-fetch tool, the assistant may read that page; the skills add no fetching of their own. Without a fetch tool, paste the text. It changes no files or settings unless you ask. For arithmetic, price-framing and behavior-diagnosis may use your assistant's own code tool when one is available; that code runs only in your session and sends nothing anywhere.
- Categories of data: the plugin collects none. What you paste is typically page text, prices, flow descriptions, step counts and customer quotes. Remove customer names and emails before pasting; the skills do not need them.
- Purpose: producing the audit, check or table you ask for, during that conversation.
- Recipients: what you paste goes to the provider of the AI assistant you use, under that provider's terms. The plugin sends it nowhere else.
- Retention: the plugin keeps nothing. Retention of your conversation is set by your assistant provider.
- Your controls: paste only what the task needs, strip personal data first, and do not paste anything you are not allowed to share.
