# Marketing Psychology Kit

Evidence grades and dated rule checks for the psychology in your marketing. Each lever is quoted, graded and paired with the fact that makes it legitimate.

| Element | Legitimate only if | Rules (read 2026-10-03) | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Ends tonight", timer resets daily | the offer really ends then | FTC Act §5; UCPD Annex I point 7; DMCC Act Sch. 20 para 7 | High |

## What it does

| Skill | Give it | You get |
|---|---|---|
| dark-pattern-check | a flow | dated US, EU, UK rules, honest versions |
| persuasion-audit | a page or email | graded lever ledger, proof inventory |
| price-framing | tiers and trials | normalised prices, anchors, decoy test |
| evidence-check | a claim | graded evidence card |
| behavior-diagnosis | "why don't users do X" | COM-B barrier map with intervals |

## Examples

- "Our banner says 'Was $120, now $79, ends tonight' and resets daily. Is that a dark pattern in the EU and US?"
- "Audit our signup page: hero 'Save 10 hours a week', 3 client logos, 'Only 2 spots left!' timer. Keep, fix or remove?"
- "Tiers $29/$79/$199 a month, 3/10/unlimited seats, Pro annual $790. Check anchors, decoy and discount framing with the math."

## How it works

Studies come only from a bundled, DOI-checked ledger. Rules carry read dates; older than six months shows "stale: re-check".

## Data and network

Network scope: this plugin ships no code and calls no service of its own. It reads what you paste or attach; when you give a public URL and your assistant has a web-fetch tool, it reads that page and at most four more public pages you name, with no logins or forms and robots.txt respected. Without a fetch tool it asks you to paste the text. It changes no files or settings unless you ask. For arithmetic, price-framing and behavior-diagnosis may use your assistant's own code tool when one is available; that code runs only in your session and sends nothing anywhere.

## Personal data

Remove customer names first; any left become labels.

## What it will not do

Fake reviews, counters, deadlines or "was" prices; hidden trial terms; targeting children or people in difficulty; profiling a named person; lift predictions; legal advice.
