# Effort sizes

Hours for one experienced writer, including research, drafting, editing and publishing. Heuristics of this plugin; the user's own numbers replace them.

| Format | Hours |
|---|---|
| glossary entry or short answer page | 2 |
| update of an existing piece | 2 |
| recap or roundup of the team's own material | 3 |
| short article (about 600 to 900 words) | 4 |
| checklist (lead magnet) | 4 |
| explainer or how-to (about 1,200 to 1,800 words) | 6 |
| comparison or buyer's guide | 8 |
| template or calculator spec (lead magnet) | 8 |
| customer story with an interview | 10 |
| long guide or hub page | 12 |
| original research or benchmark from own data | 16 |
