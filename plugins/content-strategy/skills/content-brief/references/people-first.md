# People-first checks

Paraphrased from Google Search Central, "Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content" (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content, read 2026-10-08), and used here as planning checks:

- Does the piece give original information, research or analysis, or does it restate what others have already published?
- Would the intended reader leave with what they came for, or need to search again?
- Is it written for a defined audience that would come to the company directly, or only to attract search visits?
- Is the team writing on topics where it has real experience, rather than many unrelated topics in the hope that some perform?
- Is length set by what the reader needs? Google says it has no preferred word count.

Scaled content: Google's spam policies describe scaled content abuse as generating many pages mainly to manipulate rankings rather than help users (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies, read 2026-10-08). Google's guidance on generative AI content says that using AI tools to generate many pages without adding value for users may violate the scaled content abuse policy, and that AI output must be fact-checked before publishing (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content, read 2026-10-08).
