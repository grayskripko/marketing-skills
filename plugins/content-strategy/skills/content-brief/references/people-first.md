# People-first checks

Paraphrased from Google Search Central, "Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content" (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), and used here as planning checks:

- Does the piece give original information, research or analysis, or does it restate what others have already published?
- Would the intended reader leave with what they came for, or need to search again?
- Is it written for a defined audience that would come to the company directly, or only to attract search visits?
- Is the team writing on topics where it has real experience, rather than many unrelated topics in the hope that some perform?
- Is length set by what the reader needs? Google says it has no preferred word count.

Scaled content: Google's spam policies describe scaled content abuse as generating many pages mainly to manipulate rankings rather than help users (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies). Google's guidance on AI-generated content says the way content is made is not the issue; mass low-value pages are (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content).
