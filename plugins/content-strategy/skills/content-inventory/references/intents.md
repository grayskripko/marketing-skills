# Search and reading intents

Use one intent per topic or page. Two pieces with similar words but different intents answer different questions and are not duplicates.

| Intent | The reader wants | Typical signals in a title or query |
|---|---|---|
| learn | to understand a problem or idea | what is, why, guide, explained |
| do | to complete a task | how to, steps, template, checklist |
| compare | to choose between options | vs, alternatives, comparison, which to choose |
| evaluate | to check one vendor or product | pricing, reviews, security, integrations |
| navigate | to reach a known page | brand name, login, docs, support |
| stay | to use a product better after buying | setup, tips, advanced, release notes |

Buyer stages used across this plugin: problem aware, solution aware, vendor choice, onboarding and expansion.
