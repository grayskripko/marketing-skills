# Myths and plugin positions

Answer briefly when the user raises one. Never use these as a reason for a fate or a score.

| Claim | What is known |
|---|---|
| Old content is penalized, so deleting old pages lifts the site | Google's helpful-content page lists adding or removing lots of content mainly to seem fresher among its self-assessment questions, as a sign of content made for search engines. This plugin never retires a page for age alone. |
| Publishing more often ranks you higher | No Google guidance ties rankings to frequency; its spam policies name scaled content abuse. |
| AI-written content is penalized as such | Google's guidance on AI-generated content judges quality and purpose, not the tool used. |
| There is an ideal word count | Google says it has no preferred word count. |
| Topic clusters are a ranking factor by themselves | No primary source says so. Here they are a planning structure. |
| Search volume alone decides priority | Plugin position: demand evidence is one of six criteria. |
| Gated assets always convert better | Plugin position: decide per asset by the email rule in lead-magnet-plan. |
| Scores from leaked internal documents explain rankings | Unconfirmed readings of leaked material. Never used as a mechanism. |
