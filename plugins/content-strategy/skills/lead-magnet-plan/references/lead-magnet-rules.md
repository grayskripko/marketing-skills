# Lead magnet rules

## Asset types

| Type | Picks it when | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| checklist | the reader repeats a task and fears missing a step | the team's own process |
| template | the reader must produce a document or sheet | a template the team really uses |
| calculator spec | the decision depends on the reader's own numbers | a formula the team can explain |
| benchmark | the company holds aggregated data others do not | own data, with permission to publish |
| guide | the reader needs to understand a whole process | expert time |
| short course outline | the topic needs several steps over days | a sequence the team already teaches |

## Gate rule (heuristic of this plugin)

Gate the asset only when both hold:
1. it contains the company's own data or a working tool the reader cannot get elsewhere; and
2. a follow-up exists that serves the reader after they sign up.

Otherwise publish it ungated, with a conversion point on the page. Print which condition decided it.

## Follow-up

Titles only. No email text, no sales sequences, no outreach.
