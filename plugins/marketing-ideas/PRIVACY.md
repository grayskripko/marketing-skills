# Privacy

Marketing Ideas Kit is a set of instructions and reference text for an AI assistant. It has no server, no account and no storage of its own.

- Network scope: this plugin ships no code and calls no service of its own. It reads what you paste or attach and, only when you give the address of a public page and your assistant offers a web tool, that one page, after checking the site's robots.txt; it never logs in, fills forms or follows links. It may use the assistant's code or spreadsheet tool for arithmetic and changes no files or settings unless you ask.
- Categories of data: product facts and aggregate counts you paste (prices, margins, budgets, visits, signups, customers). No personal data is needed; if names or contact details appear in what you paste, the skills ignore them and do not repeat them.
- Purpose: producing the shortlist, check, test card or readout you ask for, during that conversation.
- Recipients: what you paste goes to the provider of the AI assistant you use, under that provider's terms. The plugin sends it nowhere else.
- Retention: the plugin keeps nothing. Retention of your conversation is set by your assistant provider.
- Your controls: paste counts and product facts only; remove names and contact details before pasting.
