# Idea patterns

Mechanisms, not named tactics. Each is adapted to the product before it is proposed. No company names and no results from other companies are kept here: such stories are usually self-reported and cannot be checked. Colour refers to `platform-rules.md`; "family" refers to `channel-map.md`.

P28 is retired: review requests are out of scope for this plugin.

Columns: mechanism; prerequisites (all must hold, or the idea fails G4); demand states it suits; colour; cheapest test shape.

## Direct and personal

| # | Mechanism | Prerequisites | Demand states | Colour | Test shape |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P17 | A short message to tightly matched businesses that names their situation | a lawful basis for the message; a list of matching businesses, not individuals | known pain, few large buyers | amber (sender identity, opt-out, local law) | 50 to 100 businesses, one follow-up |

| P33 | A small roundtable or dinner for buyers on a problem they share | access to a handful of buyers | few large buyers | green | one evening, five to eight guests |

## Excluded patterns, with the reason

- Matching people across sites or social profiles to find personal openers: profiling private individuals (ground rule 9).
- Using another product's or a trending app's name in your name, keywords or ads to catch its demand: impersonation risk and app-store rules 2.3.7 and 4.1(c).
- Reviews written by staff, friends or paid writers who never used the product, or rewards for positive reviews only: 16 CFR 465.2, 465.4, 465.5.
- Buying followers, views, votes or engagement: 16 CFR 465.8 and launch-platform rules.
- Posting in communities as if a neutral member while promoting: undisclosed material connection.
- Countdown timers that reset, hidden fees, hard cancellation: the dark-patterns row.
