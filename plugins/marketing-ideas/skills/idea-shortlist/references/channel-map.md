# Channel families

Fourteen families. The shortlist writes one idea per family or "not applicable: reason". A channel only works as a pair: a source that brings strangers every week, and a converter that turns them into buyers. First-signal bands are heuristics of this plugin, not measurements; no family carries a cost figure, because costs must come from the user.

| # | Family | Source of strangers | Typical converters | Demand states it fits | Cost structure | First signal (heuristic) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Search capture | people searching the problem or product type | a page that answers that search, a tool page, a comparison | searched-for | writing hours, slow compounding | months |
| 2 | Paid search | the same searches, bought per click | a landing page matched to the search | searched-for | per click | days |
| 3 | Paid social and display | feeds and sites where the buyer spends time | a landing page, a lead form, a trial | known pain, new category | per view or click; needs creative | days to weeks |
| 4 | Direct outreach | a list of matching businesses or roles | a short message, then a call or trial | known pain, few large buyers | hours per contact | days |
| 5 | Partners and resellers | people who already sell or advise the buyer | the partner's recommendation, a bundle | all four | revenue share or hours | weeks |
| 6 | Integration and marketplace listings | users of a platform the product plugs into | the listing page and an install | searched-for, known pain | listing effort, sometimes fees | weeks |
| 7 | Product exposure and referral loops | non-users who see the product's output, or customers' contacts | the output itself, a referral offer | any, if prerequisites hold | product work, rewards | weeks |
| 8 | Free tools | people searching for or sharing a small utility | the tool, then an upgrade or signup | searched-for, known pain | build hours | weeks to months |
| 9 | Content with distribution | readers reached through a channel the team controls or rents | the piece, then a list signup | new category, known pain | writing plus distribution | weeks to months |
| 10 | Other people's audiences | newsletters, shows, communities, groups the buyer already follows | a guest spot, a sponsored slot, a classified | known pain, new category | per placement or hours | days to weeks |
| 11 | Events and speaking | attendees of trade shows, meetups, conferences | a talk, a booth conversation, a workshop | few large buyers, known pain | fees, travel, hours | weeks |
| 12 | Launch and deal platforms | visitors of launch sites and deal marketplaces | the launch page, the deal | any; a wave, not a source | prep hours, revenue share | a day, then gone |
| 13 | PR and news hooks | journalists and trade editors, and their readers | a story with data or a clear first | new category | hours | weeks |
| 14 | Customer marketing | existing customers and their networks | stories, referrals, expansion offers | any, with retained users | hours, rewards | weeks |

## Per family: prerequisites, common failure, cheapest test

1. **Search capture.** Needs a searched-for demand state and time. Fails when pages target searches buyers do not make. Cheapest test: three pages on the searches buyers already use, then read impressions after a month.
2. **Paid search.** Needs search volume and an affordable click (check-line). Fails when the click costs more than the per-click check-line. Cheapest test: one tight group of searches with a spend cap.
3. **Paid social and display.** Needs a way to reach the buyer role and a creative that names the pain. Fails when the audience cannot be narrowed to buyers. Cheapest test: one audience, two messages, a cash cap, a two-week floor.
4. **Direct outreach.** Needs a lawful basis and a list of businesses that match (`platform-rules.md`). Fails with vague offers. Cheapest test: 50 to 100 tightly matched businesses (an editable default of this plugin), one offer, one follow-up.
5. **Partners and resellers.** Needs a partner whose customers are your buyers and a reason for them to care. Fails when the partner gains nothing. Cheapest test: one partner, one joint offer, a named contact.
6. **Integration and marketplace listings.** Needs a working integration and a platform with buyers in it. Fails when the listing is buried. Cheapest test: list on the one platform where most current customers already are.
7. **Product exposure and referral loops.** Needs output that non-users see, or retained customers willing to refer. Fails without retention. Cheapest test: a visible line on the output, or asking the happiest customers in person.
8. **Free tools.** Needs a small job buyers search for or share, close to the paid product. Fails when the tool's users are not buyers. Cheapest test: a rough version on one page before building more. Designing the tool is out of scope; this family only asks whether it would bring signups.
9. **Content with distribution.** Needs a source of readers every week, not only the writing. Fails as "write and hope". Cheapest test: one piece placed where buyers already read.
10. **Other people's audiences.** Needs an audience that verifiably contains buyers. Fails on broad audiences. Cheapest test: a classified or a guest spot before a full sponsorship.
11. **Events and speaking.** Needs buyers concentrated at an event, and follow-up capacity. Fails when booths collect contacts nobody follows up. Cheapest test: attend and run meetings before paying for a booth.
12. **Launch and deal platforms.** Needs a buyer who visits those platforms, a list to capture the wave, and support capacity. Fails as a channel: it arrives once. Cheapest test: a waitlist page to see if anyone from that audience signs up.
13. **PR and news hooks.** Needs genuine news or data. Fails with announcements nobody needs. Cheapest test: pitch one trade editor with one data point.
14. **Customer marketing.** Needs retained, satisfied customers. Fails when churn is high. Cheapest test: ask five customers for a story or an introduction. Review requests are out of scope here; the review rows in `platform-rules.md` only mark what is never proposed.
