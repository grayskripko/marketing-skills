# Marketing Ideas Kit

Checks whether a marketing idea can pay for its customers before you spend, drops ideas that break disclosure or platform rules, and gives the rest a cheap test with a stop line. By Sergey Skripko.

> "Sponsor a 3,000-reader newsletter for $1,200? $39/mo plan, 85% margin, 6-mo payback; assume 2% click, 10% trial, 40% paid."
>
> **Test small first.** Affordable: $198.90 per customer, $7.96 per click, $0.16 per reader, against a $0.40 quote. Needs 6.03 customers (7); assumed rates give 2.4, about two-fifths of the cost back in six months.

## What it does

| Skill | Give it | You get |
|---|---|---|
| idea-shortlist | a product description | main bet, two probes, check-lines |
| idea-ranking | your list of acquisition ideas | three to test first |
| idea-payback-check | one idea, ideally a quote | verdict, break-even, cheapest test |
| idea-test-card | ideas you picked | volume check, pass and stop lines, run time |
| idea-readout | counts from tests you ran | scale, iterate once, stop or inconclusive |

## Examples

- The newsletter question above.
- "Marketing ideas for my $39/mo stock app for indie bookstores: 85% margin, 6-mo payback, $300/mo cash, 2 founders, 140 stores."
- "Results: 120 cold emails, 0 replies. 18 stores via distributor reps: 5 trials, 2 paid. Scale, iterate or stop each channel?"

## How it works

Every idea needs a source of buyers and a converter. Costs are judged against your figures; other numbers are labelled assumptions. Rule rows (US, EU, UK) are dated.

## Data and network

Network scope: this plugin ships no code and calls no service of its own. It reads what you paste or attach and, only when you give the address of a public page and your assistant offers a web tool, that one page, after checking the site's robots.txt; it never logs in, fills forms or follows links. It may use the assistant's code or spreadsheet tool for arithmetic and changes no files or settings unless you ask.

It stores nothing and needs no personal data (see PRIVACY.md).

## What it will not do

Write copy or ads, run ad accounts, build prospect lists, request or fake reviews, or target named people. Not legal advice.

## Troubleshooting

"Unknown": a cost or observed rate is missing.
