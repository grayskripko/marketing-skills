# Sources for method transfer rules

Every page below was opened and the cited passage read on 2026-10-13. The package makes no network requests at run time; these notes were embedded when it was built. The user's approved protocol, filed method and own procedures decide acceptance; the sources below explain why the skills treat certain points as decisive.

## 1. FDA guidance: Analytical Procedures and Methods Validation for Drugs and Biologics (July 2015)

https://www.fda.gov/files/drugs/published/Analytical-Procedures-and-Methods-Validation-for-Drugs-and-Biologics.pdf — read 2026-10-13 (full text via pdftotext). Guidance page https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/analytical-procedures-and-methods-validation-drugs-and-biologics — read 2026-10-13: final, docket FDA-2015-N-0007, not marked withdrawn. Nonbinding recommendations.
- VIII.B.2 (Analytical Methods Transfer Studies, printed p. 11–12): transfer is typically managed under a transfer protocol that details the parameters to be evaluated and the predetermined acceptance criteria; two or more labs execute the preapproved protocol; a sufficient number of representative test articles, for example the same lot(s), are used by both labs; comparative studies evaluate accuracy and precision, especially interlaboratory variability; for a stability-indicating procedure, forced degradation samples or samples containing pertinent product-related impurities should be analysed at both sites. Refers to USP <1224> for more guidance (USP text not opened; sold).
- VIII.B.1 (Alternative Analytical Procedures): replacing an approved procedure needs a comparability study showing the new method is equivalent or superior; equivalence, non-inferiority or superiority studies use appropriate statistical methods (ASTM E2935 cited; not opened).
- VIII.A: changes to a procedure (equipment, reagent) call for full or partial revalidation depending on the change.

## 2. EU GMP Part I, Chapter 6 Quality Control (in operation since 1 October 2014)

https://health.ec.europa.eu/document/download/c74c8720-27bf-4252-808f-d65a206a90bb_en?filename=2014-11_vol4_chapter_6.pdf — read 2026-10-13. The EudraLex Volume 4 index (https://health.ec.europa.eu/medicinal-products/eudralex/eudralex-volume-4_en, read 2026-10-13) lists this as the current Chapter 6 with no newer revision.
- 6.37: before transfer, the transferring site verifies the test method complies with the Marketing Authorisation or technical dossier; the original validation is reviewed against current ICH/VICH requirements; a documented gap analysis identifies supplementary validation before the transfer starts.
- 6.38–6.39: a detailed protocol identifies the testing and methods, training needs, standards and samples, transport and storage conditions, and acceptance criteria based on the current validation and ICH/VICH requirements.
- 6.40: deviations from the protocol are investigated before closure; the transfer report documents the comparative outcome and identifies areas needing further revalidation, if applicable.

## 3. WHO TRS 1044, Annex 4: WHO guidelines on technology transfer in pharmaceutical manufacturing (2022)

Page https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/trs1044-annex4 — read 2026-10-13 (published 30 April 2022; replaces TRS 961 Annex 7, 2011). PDF https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/medicines/norms-and-standards/guidelines/production/trs1044-annex4-technology-transfer-in-pharmaceutical-manufacturing.pdf?sfvrsn=9a174f1a_1&download=true — read 2026-10-13.
- 12.24: approaches include confirmation testing, comparability testing between sending and receiving units, co-validation, or paper-based knowledge transfer; the choice is risk based and scientifically justified.
- 12.25: the protocol covers objective, scope and responsibilities, materials and methods, experimental design and acceptance criteria, documentation, handling of deviations and test samples.
- 12.27: the receiving unit formally agrees acceptance criteria before execution of the protocol, executes it and generates the transfer report.
- 12.34–12.36: number of analysts defined and justified; parameters defined and justified; acceptance criteria set to determine success.
- 12.43, 12.46: deviations and changes documented and investigated, and assessed before close-out.

## 5. ICH Q2(R2) Validation of analytical procedures (EMA page)

https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/ich-q2r2-validation-analytical-procedures-scientific-guideline — read 2026-10-13. Current version Q2(R2), Step 5, legal effective date 14/06/2024; Q2(R1) (effective 01/06/1995) shown in document history. Used for the point that "current ICH requirements" in EU GMP 6.37 now means Q2(R2).

FDA final guidance Q2(R2) Validation of Analytical Procedures (March 2024), https://www.fda.gov/media/161201/download — read 2026-10-13 (pdftotext). Nonbinding recommendations.
- §2.2 (printed p. 5): for transfer to a different laboratory, partial or full revalidation and/or comparative analysis of representative samples should be performed; justification for not performing additional transfer experiments should be provided if appropriate; covalidation using data from multiple sites can demonstrate predefined performance criteria and could also satisfy transfer requirements at the participating sites. Changes the answer: an evidenced covalidation or justified reduced approach is assessed as such, not treated as a missing comparative study; protocol-required comparisons still apply.

## 6. USP <621> Chromatography revision bulletins

The bulletins establish these dated amendments; the full licensed chapter was not read. Apply the edition that governs the user's procedure.
- Posted 31 Mar 2023, official 1 Apr 2023: https://www.uspnf.com/sites/default/files/usp_pdf/EN/USPNF/revisions/gc-621-rb-notice-20230331.pdf — read 2026-10-13 (direct download blocked; read in a browser session). Signal-to-noise noise is calculated on a window of at least 5 times the peak width at half height. Changes the answer: differing S/N with matching chromatograms points first to the data-system noise window.
- Posted 22 Nov 2024, official 1 Dec 2024: https://www.uspnf.com/sites/default/files/usp_pdf/EN/USPNF/revisions/gc-621-rb-notice-20241122.pdf — read 2026-10-13 (same route). For isocratic and gradient LC, column internal diameter may be adjusted even without a change in particle size and/or length; the changes are acceptable provided system suitability criteria are fulfilled and selectivity and elution order of the specified impurities are shown to be equivalent. Changes the answer: an ID-only change may be an allowed adjustment, but not on system suitability alone and not for other simultaneous changes.

## 7. FDA inspection records (examples, not final agency determinations)

- SGS North America Inc., Form FDA 483, issued 20 Mar 2026, observation 1: https://www.fda.gov/media/193529/download — read 2026-10-13 (scanned; pages 1–2 read by OCR). Reference standards were used in method transfer activities (transfer reports dated November 2025) without verification of identity, quality and suitability before use; the procedure relied on certificate-of-analysis review. Changes the answer: matching standard lots is not evidence of qualification for use.
- FEI 3014028588 (Durham), Form FDA 483, inspection 24 Mar–10 Apr 2025, observation 8(a): https://www.fda.gov/media/186635/download — read 2026-10-13. A related-substances HPLC method transfer was completed and effective on 3 Aug 2023, yet drug-substance batches used in a submission were tested and released in 2022 with the method not yet implemented. Changes the answer: for release or submission use, test dates are compared with implementation, not only protocol approval.
- ABR Laboratory LLC, warning letter 320-25-42, 10 Feb 2025, violation 1: https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/abr-laboratory-llc-696872-02102025 — read 2026-10-13. A plate-count medium different from the USP <61> medium was not shown to be equivalent or better, and a plan for suitability by product category did not show suitability for each individual drug product. Changes the answer: medium equivalence and per-product suitability are separate evidence questions.

## Rules of thumb of this plugin (no external source)

- Near-match lot or document numbers (one character different) are never merged without confirmation.
- A mean difference inside an equivalence margin is not reported as equivalence when the protocol asks for an interval or equivalence test.
- A different instrument model is a difference only when the method or protocol names it or it changes a stated parameter.
- Precision differences such as 0.5 vs 0.50 are not reported as method differences.
