# Google field guidance

Sources actually opened 2026-10-10. Applies to Google Business Profile fields, not a complete eligibility assessment. Paraphrases below; no copied long passages.

- https://support.google.com/business/answer/3039617?hl=en — read 2026-10-10. Business name uses real-world branding. Main hours and special/holiday hours are distinct. Description field: at most 750 characters, no URLs/HTML; primarily business details rather than promotions, prices or sales. This limit does not apply to website text or every profile field.
- https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en — read 2026-10-10. Name must represent the real business, without unnecessary information. Businesses that only travel to customers should hide their address; a staffed hybrid storefront may display its public address. Private residential addresses stay out of output. Branch phone/website must represent that location. Businesses operating only by appointment should not provide main opening hours; booking availability is not automatically a public opening schedule. Apply this only to confirmed whole-business appointment-only operation, not merely an appointment requirement for a particular service. If that scope is unclear, ask before deciding the main-hours edit. Do not infer eligibility merely from pasted copy.

Owner-controlled source priority, no-fetch scope, dated-snapshot handling and approval-first replacements are authored workflow choices, not additional Google requirements. The blanket exclusion of price and promotion copy from description drafts is also an authored drafting choice; Google says descriptions must not focus on promotions, prices or sales. Counting spaces and punctuation is the local counting convention; the source states a 750-character maximum without defining Unicode counting. Sources may change.
