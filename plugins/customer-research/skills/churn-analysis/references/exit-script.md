# Follow-up script for customers who cancelled

When asked for an exit-interview script, adapt these optional participant-facing probes for a human interviewer. Select and order them around unresolved explanations and available time. Do not ask the requester to answer them unless they are the participant. Prefer concrete past events over speculative win-back promises.

1. "Think back to when you first thought about stopping. What was going on that week?"
2. "What did you try before deciding to cancel?"
3. "Who else was part of the decision, and what did they care about?"
4. "If you still do this job, how do you do it now, and how did you choose that approach?"
5. "What did you expect to have working by the relevant value milestone, and what actually worked?" Adapt the milestone to the product promise and supplied tenure; use the first month only when onboarding makes it relevant.

Instructions to include for the human interviewer: obtain participant permission before recording; explain actual retention/access arrangements; keep notes under a source ID with role and segment instead of a name. Consent is unconfirmed until recorded. This is practice, not legal advice.
