# Follow-up script for customers who cancelled

Five questions about what happened, in order. Ask about the past, not about what would win them back.

1. "Think back to when you first thought about stopping. What was going on that week?"
2. "What did you try before deciding to cancel?"
3. "Who else was part of the decision, and what did they care about?"
4. "What are you using now to get this done, and how did you pick it?"
5. "What did you expect to have working by the end of the first month, and what did you actually have?"

Notes: ask for permission before recording; keep notes under a source id, with role and segment instead of the name. This is practice, not legal advice.
